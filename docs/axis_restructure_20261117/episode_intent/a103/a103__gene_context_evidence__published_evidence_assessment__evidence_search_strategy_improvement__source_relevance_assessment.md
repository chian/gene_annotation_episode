## What this episode is for

Its goal, in its own words (`goal.py` with `source_contracts.py`):

> For A103, identify the actual locus and genome before using a source claim. Keep neighboring-gene identity, order, orientation, spacing, fusion, cooccurrence, and alternative explanations tied to their source and scope.
>
> Investigate which returned sources merit examination for the assigned question. Search results are source-discovery information. Examine original sources and their conditions before asserting biological evidence. A page child can investigate where and how to examine a selected source.

In short: It is handed search results (source refs) for a question and decides which of those sources deserve examination, reading and acquiring as needed and commissioning a page examination of a chosen source.

## Task and instructions

One unit is one model-selected action: a tool call, or a child commission. There is no reasoning-commit action (`source_interpretation.py`) and no waiting (`waiting.py`).

The instructions (`task_instructions.py`, this folder's own copy) are composed in this order:

1. The literature investigation text `INSTRUCTIONS` ("You own an iterative scientific annotation improvement, not a tool itinerary. ..."), held in the file itself.
2. This episode's goal text above, appended (the binding's `instructions`).
3. The tool-knowledge text, when tool knowledge is in the context; the launch-instruction text, when the launch-instruction library is.
4. The paragraph on a pending child delivery (`redeliver_report`).
5. `REASONING_COMMIT_REQUIREMENT` and the two wait paragraphs are only added when `reason` / `wait` are permitted; they are not here.
6. Under the exchange protocol the old history guidance is replaced by: use `request_context` exchanges for own attempt metadata (no completed action or observation), and read earlier outcomes with `episode.history` (view=records).

Assignment form (`from_parent.py`), version `search_assignment_v1`:

## How its credit is counted

- Credit kind `scientific` (`credit.py`): the axis's scientific credit view for this episode (`services.scientific_credit(context, receiver_selection)`). No projector and no score judgments of its own (`scientific_records.py`).
- After each own action whose outcome counts as observed, the credit view is read again; its `credit` is saved with the action and handed to the controller as that unit's input (`task.py`). A unit that fails while executing is recorded `failed` and still enters the iterator with the credit measured after it (`task_state.py`). A response that fails admission is recorded as a `rejected` decision effect and creates no unit. Request_context exchanges create no unit.
- Nothing in these files bounds, clamps or rescales credit.
- What a child return does to credit is not in these files: the from_child file only collects the refs the child delivered; the credit is whatever the axis credit view says afterwards.

## What it reports to its parent

- With a report contract (the normal case): the parent's requested form, filled from the model's latest admitted `report_update` together with the report facts, saved once and returned as `{'form': ...}` (`report_delivery.py`). The fields are whatever the parent composed when commissioning.
- No report facts of its own (`to_parent.py`: `fact_sources`, `facts`, `current_report_facts` all empty).
- Before delivery the report is validated: the assignment must still be valid and every reference in the form must resolve in this episode's context.
- Without a report contract: `{'credit': <current credit>, 'pending_handles': ...}`.

## How it differs from its relatives

- **Own to this episode:** the search-result assignment (`search_assignment_v1`); the acquisition tools, with `public.ncbi.search` limited to PubMed; no source restriction; a single child slot (page examination); `source_references.py` is the short 14-line version (no `_artifact`, no `_within_source`).
- **Different from its child `source_evidence_examination`:** that child is held to one assigned page source, has no acquisition tools, three child slots, and validates a `page_assignment_v1` (artifact must match the source representation).
- **Custody quirk:** its summary reads `source_refs` although its assignment names the field `search_result_refs`, so `literature_source_custody` is always empty. Carried over as written; worth a decision.
- **Compared with the A101 counterpart** (from `docs/axis_restructure_20261117/episode_intent/a101/`, not its code): A103 keeps the decision call split into `call.py`, `decision_contracts.py`, `decision_exchange.py`, `decision_provider.py`, `saved_decision_exchange.py` (A101 had one `task_decision.py`), carries its own TASK prompt renderer (`task_presentation.py`, `task_presentation_policy.py`) instead of `render_literature_source_prompt`, holds its own copy of the instruction text in `task_instructions.py`, uses a separate `action_policy.py` / `child_commissioning.py` / `task_suspension.py`, and its provider call does not compare against `_ORIGINAL_MODEL_*` provider objects.
