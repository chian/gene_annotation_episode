## What this episode is for (its own words)
This episode monitors the progress of one assigned execution target (`goal.py`):

> "Investigate which further exposed progress information is useful for the assigned target. Use actual status, measured quantities, committed references and source-bound logs. Liveness establishes process presence only; elapsed time alone establishes neither productive work nor a stall. Unknown information stays unknown. Earlier outcomes and your admitted child forms inform later decisions. You may explicitly commission an ordinary monitor for one exposed direct dependency; no descendant traversal, fanout, cadence or depth is prescribed. Return the original parent's typed form at your closure even while the target remains active. Do not report private reasoning, intentions or unrestricted findings. Recorded facts use saved delivered observations; closure does not poll or compose another report with a model. The monitor's information-credit projector and bound yield rule govern continuation. [or: The caller's monitoring projector and configured controller own credit and continuation.] Repeated observation identities and read timestamps alone earn no new information."

The planning context (`goal.planning_context`) contains the objective, the target_handle, the requested_fields, the unresolved question "Which exposed progress information would improve the original requested report?", and the progress definition "Actual received information under the caller-bound monitoring projector; repeated identities and timestamps alone earn no credit." It also contains the yield_settings.

## Task and instructions
`task_instructions.py` is this episode's own instruction file; the job siblings do not have one. It joins the goal with these blocks:
- ACTION_BOUNDARY: "Do not emit a stop action or invent a turn budget, approval review, quality gate, experiment, term or observation."
- CREDIT_COMPONENT_GUIDANCE: current_evidence_vector describes the progress proxy, not biological truth.
- RESOURCE_ESTIMATE_GUIDANCE: "A timing experiment earns monitoring credit for timing information learned and updates execution expectations. Missing history means duration is unknown. Job elapsed time and the duration of an own monitoring action are distinct measurements."
- REPORT_GUIDANCE: a commissioned child returns only the parent's typed form; report_update is filled inside the reasoning response; redeliver_report is for report_delivery_failure; do not recommission merely to fix representation.
- HISTORY_GUIDANCE, or EXCHANGE_HISTORY_GUIDANCE under `decision_exchange_v1`.
- HOST_EXECUTION_GUIDANCE.
- Tool knowledge and the launch library.
- REPAIR_GUIDANCE.
- WAIT_GUIDANCE, plus NATIVE_EVENT_GUIDANCE when native event information exists.

`task.py` is the iteration loop. It is simpler than the job siblings: it has no `reason` exemption, no job_child storage-failure branch, and no component-channel, MinHash or reads.map feedback. Report admission uses grain `monitor`. `task_decision.py` holds the call, exchange, provider and contract validation in one file.

## What it reads from its parent's assignment
`monitor_assignment_v1` (`contracts.py`):
- `target_handle`: must be in scope as an `execution_handle`.
- `requested_fields`: a subset of execution_status, measurements, log_windows, committed_record_refs, dependencies, result_available.
- `known_progress_refs`: each must read as a progress snapshot of the same target.
- `permitted_committed_record_types`: a subset of RECORD_SCHEMAS.

## Children
- Child role `monitor`, which is this same episode type, recursively. It is allowed only when `services.dependencies(target_handle)` is non-empty.
- Sent to the child (`to_child_execution_progress_assessment.prepare_dependency_request`): a `monitor_assignment_v1` whose target_handle must be an exposed direct dependency of the parent's target, and whose permitted_committed_record_types must not widen the parent's.
- Taken back (`from_child_execution_progress_assessment.receive_progress`): the child's typed form, validated. Every `episode_progress` reference in it is read and collected, and the references are admitted into the receipt index with a content-digest signature over form, schema and scope (`provider._receive_progress`).

## Decision state and what the model sees
It uses the same generic `decision_state` field set as the job siblings, plus `monitoring_information_credit` / `monitoring_information_binding` when the credit view has them. Its own `decision_context` holds monitor_assignment, monitor_target_description, monitor_direct_dependencies, monitor_received_progress_refs, monitor_information_meaning ("Exposed direct relationships and already delivered observations. Empty measurements/log windows do not assert absence or a stall.") and monitor_goal. `decision_inputs.py` is the job version without shared_information_context or requested_action_history, and with expectations for role `monitor`. The prompt renderer is `render_monitor_prompt`.

## Tools offered
- `progress.observe(requested_fields[, native_operation])`: fields must stay within the assignment's requested_fields. `native_operation` appears only when the target descriptor supplies a `selected_observation_schema`. "Retain the original observation identity when facts are unchanged. This operation never traverses dependent work."
- `progress.read(progress_ref)`: reads only already-received refs.
- `progress.log(progress_ref, log_index)`: reads one retained log window through `source.read`, keeping source and artifact byte custody. An empty window returns empty text.
- Common actions: `tool`, `child`, `wait`, `redeliver_report`.

## Credit
`credit_kind` = `monitoring`. `credit_view` is the caller-bound role credit (`services.bound_role_credit`). `information_credit` uses ProgressInformationCredit(progress_reader, information_unit): credit is the count of newly informative received snapshots times information_unit. Repeated identities and timestamps earn nothing. Credit is not bounded.

## Continuation
This episode has its own private estimator, `_monitor_information_yield`. Credit scores must not decrease (`monitor_information_credit_cannot_decrease`), and the estimator calls `estimate_nonnegative_yield` with binding `monitor_information_accumulation_v1`: forecast = the next monitor action's information credit; decision = the predicted upper gain at or below the monitor cutoff. Settings are `yield_settings = {kind: information_accumulation, cutoff >= 0, uncertainty_alpha in (0,1)}` together with an `information_unit > 0`. This maps to the new accumulation estimator. Without yield_settings, the caller's controller owns continuation.

## Report to parent
Fact sources: monitoring_progress_refs (episode_progress refs), monitoring_target_handle, monitoring_execution_state (latest lifecycle state or null), monitoring_result_available, monitoring_observed_at_unix_seconds (taken from UTC observed_at without refreshing), monitoring_committed_record_refs (permitted types only), and monitoring_dependency_handles (direct dependencies only). `validate_report` requires x-source fields to equal these facts. Any record reference in the form must be of a permitted type and must have been received. When the parent is gene_context_evidence and an annotation task is required, `monitoring_progress_refs` is a required return source. Closure does not poll.

## Differences from similar episodes
It is the only one of the four episodes here that has a child (a recursive monitor on a direct dependency), uses the accumulation estimator rather than completion with a target, and has its own instruction blocks. Its task loop and decision inputs are reduced versions of the job siblings'.
