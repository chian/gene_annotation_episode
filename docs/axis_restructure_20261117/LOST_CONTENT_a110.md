# Lost content: A110 (biochemical_feasibility_evidence) graders and grading sheets, 19 November 2026

Scope (owner, 19 November 2026): only graders, subscoring sheets and scoring sheets. That covers grader
definitions and prompts, sheet schemas, cell components, sheet columns, how a grader fills a sheet, and which
sheet an episode writes. Goals, instruction text, credit, yield, thresholds, tools and messages are out of scope.
Each row records one owner decision about a grader or a sheet.
