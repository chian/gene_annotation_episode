## CONTRACT MISFITS

- BLOCKED-ON-JOB-SERVICE: job_access; wanted call: `ctx.services['job_access'].observe_progress(...)` (see above).
- new field: execution_progress_assessment information_unit (assignment: the old monitor took it from its configuration; `credit` needs it and has no settings; the BLAST completion target is it).
- new field: execution_progress_assessment target_description (assignment, optional `record`: `resource_kind` and `service_or_program` of the target, the old `services.describe_progress_target` answer, so that `read_from_parent` can decide `blast_status_target` and `yield_estimator.py` reads only the parent input). Omitted means not a BLAST target (the old `is_blast_job` returned False without a description).
- new field: execution_progress_assessment monitoring_progress_snapshots (return, selectable: the delivered snapshots by identity, which the commissioning monitor's `credit` needs because it has no services). The child monitor's own commission selects it together with `monitoring_progress_refs`.
- new field: execution_progress_assessment version (assignment, declared `monitor_assignment_v1`: the old schema's `version` property; the parent copies it).
- new field: execution_progress_assessment committed record refs the monitor may read (assignment `record_refs`): the old monitor read committed records of any permitted type from the shared Store; under custody they must be passed. Not added to the form (the old assignment had no such property); callers must also supply `information_unit` and the declared `version`.
- completion credit may pass its target: for a BLAST status target the controller is `CompletionYieldController` with `target = information_unit` (README 7b), and the credit is `information_unit * newly_informative_received_snapshot_count` (old formula, unchanged, unbounded). Two newly informative snapshots (for example the target's and a child monitor's) give `2 * information_unit > target`, and the controller raises `ValueError('completion_credit_exceeds_target')`. Also, the old estimator forecast zero after one original BLAST status observation whether or not it was informative; the completion controller stops only when the credit reaches the target, so an uninformative observation does not stop it (it stops on its forecast falling to the cost). The settings' `cost` must be below `information_unit` (`completion_cutoff_must_be_below_target`).
- a child monitor is commissioned with no `target_description` (the old assignment did not carry one for a dependency; the old child described its own target through the provider), so a BLAST dependency is monitored under the accumulation controller.
- the old `null` facts (`monitoring_execution_state`, `monitoring_result_available`, `monitoring_observed_at_unix_seconds` without an observation) are `record` fields holding `{'value': ...}`: a form has no null.
- tool names: the old `progress.observe`, `progress.read`, `progress.log` are `progress_observe`, `progress_read`, `progress_log` (a tool name matches `[a-z][a-z0-9_]*`). The commission selection of the model is `monitor_dependency` with `dependency_handle`.
- progress observation identities and `known_progress_refs` are provider identities, not Store records: they travel in `list` fields, not `record_refs`, and are not custody.
- `monitor_private_yield_binding_not_enabled` and the cutoff checks are kept in `yield_estimator.py` over `settings['cost']` and `settings['parameters']['uncertainty_alpha']`. Cost zero passes the old check but an accumulating controller then never stops; the run's settings must give a cost above zero.
- the monitor's credit raises `monitor_information_credit_cannot_decrease` against the credit the controller last saw (README 7b); the projection is monotonic by construction, so it cannot fire today.
- the old goal text had two variants (own yield settings, or the caller's projector); only the own-yield variant is carried: the episode always builds its own controller from its settings.
- the old `outcome_page_size` setting is not needed: every outcome is in the view.
- the old dependency check of a child commission (`prepare_dependency_request`) raises from `msg_to_child` while the commission is built (a wrong commission fails); the target list comes from the latest delivered observation's `job` dependencies, not from a provider read before the first observation (no scope is in the assignment).
- A101 has `validate_form` (`forms/sequence_homology_evidence/communication.py`): `final_to_parent.validate_report` calls it over the reported facts against the old `fact_sources` report schemas (the old call validated the parent-authored form; a parent writes no form now).
