# hpc_work_planning (A103): blocked, unplaced and contract misfits

Episode: `gene_annotation_episode/episodic_workflow/root/a103/gene_context_evidence/hpc_work_planning`. Job episode (the owner of an HPC commission's ledger: it plans, launches, monitors and collects HPC work, directly with twelve tools or through five children). Five children, slots `hpc_prediction`, `hpc_execution`, `launch`, `monitor`, `results` (the old child roles). The old source was read from `gene_annotation_episode_disk` (`git show 97308b94c:<path>`); this working tree has no `.git`. The old files of this episode and of the A102 head's `hpc_work_planning` are identical but for names, so the carried A102 episode (`a102/structural_homology_evidence/hpc_work_planning`) was the shape; every library path, child form and the model transport were taken from A103's own pieces.

## CONTRACT MISFITS

- BLOCKED-ON-JOB-SERVICE: job ledger handoff record; wanted call: `ctx.store.table(JOB_LEDGER).get(handoff_ref)`.
- BLOCKED-ON-JOB-SERVICE: job_access; wanted call: `await ctx.services['job_access'].observe_target(target, key=key, resumed=ctx.resumed)` (and `read_file`, `write_allocation_control`, `observe_queue`, `run_command`, `read('execution_expectations', ...)`, `read('native_event_targets', ctx.key)`).
- BLOCKED-ON-JOB-SERVICE: estimate_table; wanted call: `ctx.services['estimate_table'].latest(resource, kind)` (and `observe`, `history`, `policy`, `record`, `read`).
- new field: hpc_work_planning scope (assignment, record; the old code took it from the request message), new field: hpc_work_planning input_refs (assignment, `record_refs`, optional: the records the planned jobs read, passed on to the launch child, whose form takes them optionally).
- new field (sent): hpc_allocation_execution commission, scope and ledger_credit (its form requires all three; the old code read them from its configuration, its request and the shared ledger); execution_job_submission input_refs, handoff_unit and already_handed_off_resource_ids; execution_progress_assessment information_unit; execution_result_acquisition workspace, resource_kind and acquired_record_unit (the workspace is the frozen plan's, which `hpc_handoff` checked equals the handoff target's), initial_result_refs and initial_result_artifact_refs. The prediction sister takes owner and commission identity only, as the old request carried. The launch child's form has no `hpc_execution_job` field and no `scope`: what the old `LaunchPreparation.initial_decision_context` supplied rides in `ChildMessage.message`.
- `commission_source_ref` of the assignment is a `record_refs` field holding exactly one reference (`read_from_parent` raises `hpc_commission_source_ref_must_hold_exactly_one_reference` otherwise); the old field was a single text. `commission_id` is free text (the old `enum` came from the construction settings, which a form cannot read); exactly one of the two is required. `tool_request` must name all five keys (the old `obj` required all).
- the tools are handed no summary, so `action` adds `scope` to the arguments of every model-selected tool call, and `decide` adds `commission_identity`, `scope` and the tool request to `hpc_initialize`.
- `hpc_initialize` is a new tool: the old `state` and `initialize` ran as a side effect of the first decision context and of every `execute`; `decide` cannot write, so it offers `hpc_initialize` as the first unit (no model call). Credit before that unit is zero.
- the ledger a sister writes is read again by the unit after a commission of it: `decide` offers `hpc_read` of the commission when the last unit was `hpc_prediction` or `hpc_execution` (the old decision context read the ledger live at every decision). `credit` has no ledger: it takes the ledger credit from the last tool outcome, or from the `hpc_credit` the execution sister returned (its credit is that same ledger view; this is not a sum of children's credits). After an `hpc_prediction` unit the model credit it wrote shows only at the next unit, so that unit's marginal credit is understated.
- the two sisters disagree on the ledger access: this episode writes the `HPCLedger` file; `hpc_allocation_execution` opens that file by `settings['directory']`, and `hpc_resource_prediction` is itself blocked at its ledger open (`BLOCKED-ON-JOB-SERVICE: hpc_ledger`, in its own `task.py`): a `hpc_prediction` commission is built correctly here and fails inside the child.
- RETURN_FORM: `finding` is `FINDING_FORM` (accepted and remaining obligations, plans frozen and closed, results held, the credit components, a qualification). The four old fact sources `hpc_credit`, `hpc_model_ref`, `hpc_accepted_obligations`, `hpc_result_refs` are selectable (`required=False`); `hpc_model_ref` is text; `hpc_result_refs` is `record_refs` and returns the result sources only: a parent that reads their bytes also needs each source's artifact (decision T5), which the old fact did not name (no field is added here; the sisters declare `hpc_result_artifact_refs` as a new field).
- `final_to_parent` calls the A103 axis validator `validate_form` over the old `hpc_report_form` properties; the validator's own unavailable-reference error (`report.hpc_model_ref: reference is unavailable at this boundary`) is raised before the old `hpc_report_model_not_in_ledger` check when a model reference is not in the ledger.
- the old `hpc_report_form` the model repaired a report with and the old `report_update`/`report_values` are not carried: every old report field was a recorded fact, now selected by the parent.
- yield: `AccumulationYieldController('hpc_work_planning', initial_credit, settings['cutoff'], settings['parameters'])` (README 7b: `HPCContinuationEstimator` has no controller of its own; the credit is a signed measured gain with no maximum or target; the old commission `yield_bindings['hpc']` is the settings; the intent record's "completion with a target" is not used: the remaining obligations are not a commissioned finite target in `parent_input`). The controller stops the Episode only when its cost (`cutoff`) is above zero and units stop earning. Completion credit does not apply, so "completion credit may pass its target" does not arise.
- `selection` of the commissions: `hpc_prediction` selects the four facts; `hpc_execution` the four facts plus `hpc_result_artifact_refs` and `hpc_handoff_refs`; `launch` selects `execution_handoff_refs` and `execution_completed`; `monitor` the seven `monitoring_*` fields the monitor's form declares (not `monitoring_dependency_handles`); `results` selects nothing: `execution_result_refs` and `execution_completed` are required fields of that child's form and are always returned (selecting a required field raises `FormError`).
- the head `a103/gene_context_evidence/` (`home.py`, `contract_binding.py`, `runtime_source.py`, its tests) may still name the deleted `hpc_work_planning/hpc_runtime.py` or the old `HPCEpisode` class; they break until the head is carried. `hpc_work_planning/episode.py` of the earlier port (`HPCEpisode(HPCRuntime, A103ContractBinding)`) is replaced by `HPCWorkPlanning(EpisodeBinding)`.
- the old tests of this episode bound shared old-root test classes and are retired (`docs/axis_restructure_20261117/retired_tests/a103__gene_context_evidence__hpc_work_planning.tsv`); the new tests are structural and exercise the pure parts (form, controller, credit, commissions, report, blocked primitives): no real-runner test is possible while the ledger handoff record and both services are blocked.
