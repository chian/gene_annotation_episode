## CONTRACT MISFITS

- BLOCKED-ON-JOB-SERVICE: job_access; wanted call: `await ctx.services['job_access'].exec_command(assignment, command, key=ctx.operation_key, resumed=ctx.resumed)`.
- validate_form: A101 has `forms/sequence_homology_evidence/communication.py::validate_form(payload, schema, resolver)`, but the old call validated the parent-written JSON-Schema report form with `x-source` fields; that form no longer exists (the system admits the result exactly against `RETURN_FORM`). `messages/final_to_parent.py validate_report` therefore keeps only the old fact-agreement check and calls no `validate_form`.
- fact-source names with a dot (`execution.masking_*`, `execution.blast_utility_*`) cannot be field names of a `RecordForm` (`^[a-z][a-z0-9_]*$`): not declared; "rename nothing" and the form's name rule conflict. They are local-resource families and are always empty for this hpc role.
- completion credit may pass its target: the target is the credit the completion keys earn (`read_from_parent.completion_target`, new: the old code had a completion proof and no number), but the old credit counts every satisfied requirement key, including ones outside `completion_keys`; when such a key is satisfied as well the credit exceeds the target and the controller raises `ValueError('completion_credit_exceeds_target')`. A target of zero or an initial credit at or above the target also fails the controller.
- completion credit may reach its target without a completion proof: the old completion needed every completion key satisfied and the last receipt to satisfy the endpoint predicate; the controller stops at the credit target, reached when the keys are satisfied by any receipts. `practical_completed` can then be false in the return although the controller stopped (the finding says so).
- the completion controller stops an Episode the moment its credit reaches the target, but a requested endpoint that later loses an established fact lowers credit (signed): an episode already stopped does not see it.
- new field: hpc_job_execution completion_target (parent input, derived from `progress_requirements` and `completion_keys` by `read_from_parent`; the assignment has no such field).
- new field: hpc_job_execution task_yield_class (parent input, derived from `requested_job` and `endpoint`; the old `_request_yield_class` read the assignment in the yield estimator).
- new field: hpc_job_execution finding (return; the old episode returned the parent's requested JSON-Schema form filled from the model's report update and had fact sources only). Declared `FINDING_FORM`: `completed`, `endpoint`, `commands_run`, `commands_failed`, `satisfied_keys`, `qualification`.
- the parent's own assignment cannot be read by the child (README 5 item 14): the old admission (child choice among the parent launch's frozen HPC requests, `frozen_hpc_launch_request_missing`, the parent's `immutable_input_versions`, masking and utility input custody, the native and standard BLAST database job-input validation against the Store, `allowed_resource_ids`/`allowed_kinds` constraints, the `launch_instructions` selection inherited from the parent launch) is not carried. The parent must copy the chosen request into this child's assignment in its `msg_to_child`; `launch_instructions` and `scientific_manifest` are assignment fields.
- `execution_result_origin` is `null` in the old facts when no result windows exist; a form has no null, so it is declared a `record` and returned as `{}`. The result fields (`execution_result_refs`, `execution_file_source_refs`, `execution_file_capture_outcomes`, `execution_result_origin`) and every native result family are always empty: this role received no result or handoff records and acquires no results.
- the old record `source` of provider `episode.practical_receipt` (identity-checked against the old runner's tables) is replaced by one artifact per receipt written by `ctx.store.artifact`; the old receipts were published at every report as well; now each receipt is published by its own unit.
- the hook-era `home_factory.py` and `contract_binding.py` of the A101 head (outside this folder) still import `hpc_job_execution.runtime` / `HpcJobExecutionEpisode`, which are deleted here (`runtime.py`, the old `episode.py` class and `tests/test_runtime_link.py`); those imports fail until the head is carried.
