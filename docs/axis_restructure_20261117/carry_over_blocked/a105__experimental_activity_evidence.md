published-evidence assessment (slot `published_evidence_assessment`, the old role `literature`). Each call below is

## Contract misfits

- Tool names: a tool name is `[a-z][a-z0-9_]*`, so `source.read` is `source_read`, `process.search` is `process_search`, `process.revise` is `process_revise` ("names both sides use are the OLD names, unchanged" cannot hold for tools). The assignment's `selection.child_tool_info[].name` keeps the old dotted names.
- Child slot names are the child folder names: the old role `literature` is the slot `published_evidence_assessment`; the self slot is `experimental_activity_evidence`.
- Git: the working copy `/home/chia/repos/gene_annotation_episode` has no `.git` (it is a link to `/tmp/ramrepo`); the old files were read with `git -C /home/chia/repos/gene_annotation_episode_disk show 97308b94c:<path>`.
- new field: experimental_activity_evidence scope (the old episode took the scope from its request).
- new field: experimental_activity_evidence evidence_refs must also name the records of the scope's subjects and the proposed annotation record (the old head received its scope's subjects and the proposed annotation by itself; custody passes only named records, decision T5). `proposed_annotation_ref` stays a text field.
- ASSIGNMENT_FORM: one field per property of the old `_ASSIGNMENT` (`selection`, `proposed_annotation_ref`, `evidence_refs`, `constraints`, `context`, `revision_origin`), the old `_REFERENCE` patterns are not form types (no string format, README section 5 item 11). `selection.version` is plain text (the old `SELECTION_VERSION` constant is in `experimental_activity_evidence_records` of the old shared root, which has no module in the library). The option references of `selection` are records resolved against the frozen library (`settings['library_directory']`), so a `ChoiceField` over the thought patterns (the hook-era port's form) cannot be written at import time. The old `required: proposed_annotation_ref` under an annotation task (`child_assignment_schema` through `services.annotation_task_required`) and the old `required` `context.evidence_task` of the credit definition are not services of the new system: `proposed_annotation_ref` is optional, `evidence_task` is required.
- The finding unit is the commission's (`context.evidence_task.credit_unit`, the old schema text "use the finding unit in the explicitly configured credit definition"); the old head compared the commission's unit with the configured credit definition's digest (`validate_yield_bindings`, `scientific_yield_credit_definition_mismatch`). That comparison is not carried (README 3d: the settings are `cost` and `parameters`).
- Settings the run's `continuation` entry for `experimental_activity_evidence` must hold: `cost` (above zero, or an accumulating controller never stops), `parameters` (`{'uncertainty_alpha': ...}`), `library_directory` (the frozen experimental-activity library), `tool_registry` (`pdfinfo_executable`, `pdftotext_executable`, `pdftoppm_executable`, `job_limits`), and optionally `knowledge_library_directory`.
- Credit changed meaning at the return: the old head returned its credit gain (closing finding progress minus the credit it opened with, so inherited knowledge was not its gain); `final_to_parent` returns `completion['credit']`, the cumulative credit its controller last saw, which includes the findings it holds from the start (those the commissioner passed). The opening credit is the `initial_credit` of the controller.
- Credit: accumulation, one `credit_unit` per distinct retained finding, never bounded, clamped or rescaled. With no assessment held the library's projection of an empty known set (`finding_unit` times zero) is computed in `credit.py::finding_progress` without calling the library (the library call is blocked, above); this is the one place the library's result is reproduced rather than called. The assessments counted are those the Episode holds (`records.list('assessment', scope)`): the scope's subjects must be held.
- `reason` is the required-grading action (AGENTS.md, "Required literature evidence processing"): the old head committed with a plain `store.commit_reasoning` and no grading; a `reason` selection now needs `evidence_processing` and saves its subscoring sheets in the same unit through the library's `literature_required_grading.execute` (blocked, above). The old model schema of the `reason` kind is the tool's arguments.
- RETURN_FORM: `credit` and `finding` required (finding: the typed investigation findings with source claim, source subject, qualification, the saved sheets, the examined spans of a no-applicable-evidence finding, the progress record and the children's returns); selectable `experimental_activity_evidence_results` and `committed_record_refs`, the two old fact sources of the head (`required_return_sources`: `committed_record_refs` for a non-root parent under an annotation task, the results for a root parent). The head cannot know its commissioner's depth, so `msg_to_child` selects `committed_record_refs` whenever the head itself holds a proposed annotation and `experimental_activity_evidence_results` when the model sets `request_results`. new field: experimental_activity_evidence finding.progress, finding.child_returns, finding.child_finding_refs, finding.child_sheet_refs, finding.qualification (what the old parent held through receipt inheritance and the old `known_assessment_refs` finding).
- Two sources of records from the literature child: this head reads the child's returned records from the nested `record_refs` fields of the child's `finding` (`assessment_refs`, `sheet_refs`, `child_finding_refs`, `child_sheet_refs`); the child declares no top-level `record_refs` field. The cell families a literature task returns name their sheets in list fields, not in `record_refs`.
- The head's two instruction texts (`ScientificGoal.instructions`, the published-instruction entry and the evidence-axis entry) are carried in `task.py` with the wait, redelivery and `process.revise` wording of actions that no longer exist reworded (`process_revise`; no wait). The goal text of `goal.py` is a new short statement of what the head is for.
- `process_revise`: the old preparation resolved the pinned thought pattern against the frozen library, the selected tools against the run's tool catalog (`tool_library.resolve_invocations`) and authenticated `revision_origin` against the preparation record in the journal. Here the thought pattern is resolved against the frozen library, the tool names against the registry tools of this episode, and `revision_origin.parent_invocation_ref` is this Episode's id (`scientific_invocation_ref` has no module in the library); the authentication of `revision_origin` is not carried (a wrong commission fails).
- The old inherited-proposed-annotation checks (`child_proposed_annotation_differs_from_original_task`, `proposed_annotation_not_received_by_parent`, `literature_child_proposed_annotation_differs_from_parent`) are not carried: `msg_to_child` carries the head's proposed annotation into a head child when the model gives none, and passes the model's literature assignment as written.
