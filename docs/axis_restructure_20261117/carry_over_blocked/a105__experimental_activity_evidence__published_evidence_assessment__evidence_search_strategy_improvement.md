# a105 evidence_search_strategy_improvement: blocked, unplaced and contract misfits (19 November 2026)

Every call below is written against its intended custody-aware form, raises `RuntimeError('library_not_carried:...')`
at the point of the call (never at the top of `credit`, of a tool or of a message function) and is reported with its
exact pattern. The same library functions are called by this episode's child, `source_relevance_assessment`, and its
descendants.

## Contract misfits

- Tool names: a tool name is `[a-z][a-z0-9_]*`, so `source.read` is `source_read`, `public.literature.search` is `public_literature_search` and `literature.assay_grading.populate` is `literature_assay_grading_populate` ("names both sides use are the old names, unchanged" cannot hold for tools).
- Child slot name: the slot is the child folder name `source_relevance_assessment`; the old role was `literature_search`.
- `validate_form` is not built for A105: the old `validate_report` called it; it is not called here (no `validate_form_not_built:experimental_activity_evidence` is reached because nothing calls it). The return form is admitted exactly by the system.
- new field: evidence_search_strategy_improvement scope (the old episode took the scope from its request).
- new field: evidence_search_strategy_improvement received_record_refs (the old episode took what it received from its binding context). The parent must name every record this episode reads and every record its commission to the `source_relevance_assessment` child will name: the earlier search-result sources and their artifacts, the question-context records, the proposed annotation and its hypothesis, and the records of the commissioned task such as sheets (a passed record does not bring the records it names).
- The 39 other `requested_*` properties of the old assignment schema are declared as plain optional lists (the old enums are the family libraries' COMPONENTS, checked where the family is read); only the three of the task kinds have options.
- The RETURN_FORM declares the cell families as list fields with a shortened meaning, without an item form (the old `report_schema` rows are the family libraries' schemas).
- new field: evidence_search_strategy_improvement finding.child_returns, finding.child_finding_refs, finding.child_sheet_refs (what the commissioned child returned: the old parent admitted the child form into its own repositories and held its records through receipt inheritance).
- new field: evidence_search_strategy_improvement report tool (`sources`: source_ref, statement, qualification): the old report was the parent's declared typed form filled from the model's report update; the model's closing value is now the outcome of the declared `report` tool.
- The old child envelope written by the model (`version`, `question_context_refs`, `search_result_refs`, `literature_task`, `proposed_annotation_ref`, requested components) is now the `ChildCall` arguments `objective` and `search` (`search_result_refs`); `msg_to_child` adds this episode's `question_context_refs`, `literature_task`, `proposed_annotation_ref`, all requested arrays it was given, `scope` and the records. The selected fields are `literature_source_lead_dispositions` (leads), `literature_screening_answers` (screening) and the cell family of each requested array. `action` rejects a search result naming a source this episode does not hold (validation of the model's own answer, provisional decision of 19 November).
- This episode reads the child's returned records from the nested `record_refs` fields of its `finding` (`assessment_refs`, `sheet_refs`, `child_finding_refs`, `child_sheet_refs`); the child declares no top-level `record_refs` field. The returned cells of a family name their sheets in a list field, not in `record_refs`, so a sheet is held here only through those `finding` fields.
- `literature_source_custody` is empty: the old `decision_context` listed `assignment['page']` or `assignment['source_refs']`, and a strategy assignment has neither.
- Completion: the target is `credit_unit` times the number of experiments (`a105_experiments`) or `credit_unit` (the other non-lead tasks) and credit cannot pass it (at most one answer per target unit); `settings['cost']` must be below it (`completion_cutoff_must_be_below_target`), and for `verified_source_leads` (accumulation) a cost above zero is needed for the controller ever to stop. No `completion credit may pass its target` finding.
- The acquisition tools need `ctx.settings['tool_registry']` (the old `A105ToolRegistry` constructor arguments: `pdfinfo_executable`, `pdftotext_executable`, `pdftoppm_executable`, `job_limits`); no service wraps the public clients, so the call is blocked on the registry (above).
- Nested custody of the sources: the child's source-answer task kinds that name `source_refs`, examined spans or a measurement selection are passed to the child unchanged (as the old `to_child` did); the `search_result_refs` the model names are only those it holds.

## Not repaired

- messages/final_to_parent.py: selectable fields literature_completed_work and literature_current_scoring_sheets. Selecting either now raises explicitly (library_not_carried for completed_work, whose library module completed_action_reports does not exist; a named ValueError for current sheets when requested_assay_grading_components is empty) instead of a bare KeyError. literature_completed_work cannot be produced until the library module exists; the parent's msg_to_child does not select either field.
