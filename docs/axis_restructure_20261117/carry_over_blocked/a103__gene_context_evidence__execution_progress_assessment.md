- BLOCKED-ON-JOB-SERVICE: job_access; wanted call: `ctx.services['job_access'].observe_progress(...)` (see above).
- Self-nesting: the job brief says "child episode folders: none"; the old episode commissions an ordinary monitor of itself for an exposed direct dependency, so `CHILD_SLOTS = {'monitor': BINDING_PATH}` (contract: a slot may name the episode's own path), with `msg_to_child.py` and `read_from_child.py` present.

- new field: execution_progress_assessment information_unit (assignment: the old monitor took it from its configuration; `credit` needs it and has no settings).
- new field: execution_progress_assessment monitoring_progress_snapshots (return, selectable: the delivered snapshots by identity, which the commissioning monitor's `credit` needs because it has no services).
- new field: execution_progress_assessment version (assignment, declared `monitor_assignment_v1`).
- new field: execution_progress_assessment committed record refs the monitor may read (assignment `record_refs`): not added (the old assignment had no such property); the parent's `msg_to_child` cannot pass them today.
- new field: execution_progress_assessment scope: old `observe_progress(..., scope, ...)` and `read_progress(ref, scope)` took the request's scope; the assignment has no scope property and none was added.
- the old `null` facts (`monitoring_execution_state`, `monitoring_result_available`, `monitoring_observed_at_unix_seconds` without an observation) are `record` fields holding `{'value': ...}`: a form has no null.
- tool names: old `progress.observe`, `progress.read`, `progress.log` are `progress_observe`, `progress_read`, `progress_log`. The model's child selection is `monitor_dependency` with `dependency_handle`, not a nested assignment (the child's `known_progress_refs` is always empty, its permitted types those of the parent).

- the old `native_operation` argument of `progress.observe` is carried; its translation `native_observation_options` lived in the deleted shared root, so the value is passed to the service as written.
