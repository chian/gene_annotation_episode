# hpc_work_planning (A104): blocked, unplaced and contract misfits

Episode: `gene_annotation_episode/episodic_workflow/root/a104/evolutionary_history_evidence/hpc_work_planning`. Job episode (the owner of an HPC commission's ledger: it plans, launches, monitors and collects HPC work, directly with twelve tools (plus the new `hpc_initialize`) or through five children). Slots `hpc_prediction`, `hpc_execution`, `launch`, `monitor`, `results` (the old child roles). The old source was read from `gene_annotation_episode_disk` (`git show 97308b94c:<path>`; the working tree has no `.git`). The hook-era files of the earlier port (`hpc_runtime.py`, `binding.py`, `tests/test_hpc_runtime.py`) were deleted first; the old tests are in `retired_tests/a104__evolutionary_history_evidence__hpc_work_planning.tsv`. Nothing was added to `episode_support/`. `tests/test_binding.py`: 17 passing tests (class definition, ten files, no episode import, forms, credit, controller, commissions, children's reports, report check, blocked calls). No real-runner run is possible: the first unit that touches the ledger or a service raises.

## CONTRACT MISFITS

- BLOCKED-ON-JOB-SERVICE: HPC ledger evidence record and HPC ledger credit record; wanted call: `records.table(HPC_LEDGER).select({'owner': owner})` (for `ledger_refs` of the prediction commission).
- BLOCKED-ON-JOB-SERVICE: job ledger handoff record; wanted call: `ctx.store.table(JOB_LEDGER).get(handoff_ref)`.
- BLOCKED-ON-JOB-SERVICE: job_access; wanted call: `await ctx.services['job_access'].observe_target(target, key=key, resumed=ctx.resumed)` (and `read_file`, `write_allocation_control`, `observe_queue`, `run_command`).
- BLOCKED-ON-JOB-SERVICE: estimate_table; wanted call: `ctx.services['estimate_table'].latest(resource, kind)` (and `observe`, `history`, `policy`, `record`, `read`).
- the two A104 sisters disagree on the ledger: `hpc_resource_prediction` reads the owner ledger as custody rows (`HPC_LEDGER`, `HPC_CREDITS`, from `ledger_refs`); `hpc_allocation_execution` reaches it through a service `hpc_ledger` (`ctx.services['hpc_ledger'].open(owner_episode_id, scope, commission)`, not a service of `LAUNCH_MONITOR_EXTRACT_MECHANICS.md`). This episode, the owner, writes the `HPCLedger` file at `settings['directory']/hpc/<digest(run_id, episode_id)>` (library as written, the brief's rule), which neither sister reads. Wanted: one ledger design (a declared work table passed in custody) before the three can be linked together.
- validate_form: A104 has `validate_form` (`forms/evolutionary_history_evidence/communication.py`), and it is used. The old report schemas of `fact_sources` are rebuilt as `REPORT_SCHEMAS` in `messages/final_to_parent.py`; the old fact `hpc_accepted_obligations` had no `report_schema` and the validator needs item types, so its items are identifiers (namespace `hpc_obligation`, resolved against the accepted obligations), as in the A104 prediction child.
- new field: hpc_work_planning scope (assignment, record; the old code took it from the request message), new field: hpc_work_planning input_refs (assignment, `record_refs`; the records the planned jobs read, passed on to the launch child).
- `commission_source_ref` of the assignment is a `record_refs` field holding exactly one reference (`read_from_parent` raises `hpc_commission_source_ref_must_hold_exactly_one_reference` otherwise); the old field was a single text. `commission_id` is free text (the old `enum` came from the construction settings, which a form cannot read); exactly one of the two is required. `tool_request` must name all five keys (the old `obj` required all).
- new field (sent to the prediction child): hpc_resource_prediction ledger_refs and yield_binding (the commission's `yield_bindings['hpc_prediction']`); (sent to the execution sister): hpc_allocation_execution scope, handoff_unit, acquired_record_unit and information_unit (units from the settings, in the `ChildCall` arguments); (sent to the launch child): execution_job_submission record_refs, handoff_unit, acquired_record_unit and received_handoff_resources; (sent to the results child): execution_result_acquisition resource_kind, workspace (from the plan's manifest), acquired_record_unit. The launch child's form has no `hpc_execution_job` field and its `read_from_parent` does not read its message, so what the old `LaunchPreparation.initial_decision_context` supplied rides in `ChildMessage.message` and does not reach the child's tools. Wanted: a new field execution_job_submission hpc_execution_job. The launch child's `scientific_task` (old `services.scientific_parent_task`) is not sent: nothing here holds it.
- `hpc_allocation_execution`'s assignment form has no `input_refs`/`record_refs` field: the records the planned jobs read (`input_refs` of this episode) cannot be passed to the execution sister, so a launch by the sister has only the commission source in custody. Wanted: new field hpc_allocation_execution input_refs.
- selection: the launch child's `execution_handoff_refs`, `execution_completed` and the results child's `execution_result_refs`, `execution_completed` are required fields of their return forms (always returned, never selected: selecting one raises `FormError`), so those commissions select nothing; `hpc_prediction` and `hpc_execution` select the four facts; `monitor` the seven `monitoring_*` fields it declares selectable (not `monitoring_dependency_handles`).
- the tools are handed no summary, so `action` adds `scope` to the arguments of every model-selected tool call and `decide` adds `commission_identity`, `scope` and the tool request to `hpc_initialize`.
- `hpc_initialize` is a new tool: the old `state` and `initialize` ran as a side effect of the first decision context and of every `execute`; `decide` cannot write, so it offers `hpc_initialize` as the first unit (no model call). Credit before that unit is zero.
- the ledger a sister writes is read again by the unit after a commission of it: `decide` offers `hpc_read` of the commission when the last unit was `hpc_prediction` or `hpc_execution`. `credit` has no ledger: it takes the ledger credit from the last tool outcome, or from the `hpc_credit` the execution sister returned (its credit is that same ledger view; this is not a sum of children's credits). After an `hpc_prediction` unit the model credit it wrote shows only at the next unit, so that unit's marginal credit is understated.
- RETURN_FORM: `finding` is `FINDING_FORM` (accepted and remaining obligations, plans frozen and closed, results held, the credit components, a qualification). The four old fact sources are selectable (`required=False`); `hpc_result_refs` is `record_refs` and returns the result sources only: a parent that reads their bytes also needs each source's artifact (decision T5), which the old fact did not name (no field is added).
- yield: `AccumulationYieldController('hpc_work_planning', initial_credit, settings['cutoff'], settings['parameters'])`. The intent record names completion with the commission's obligations as target; it is not used because the credit is the owner ledger's signed measured credit (accepted accomplishment plus model improvement plus validation scores), which has no declared maximum and can pass the obligations' total (completion credit may pass its target: model-improvement and validation credit are added to the obligation credit, so the completion controller's `ValueError('completion_credit_exceeds_target')` would fail the run), and the target is a ledger record the controller cannot read from `parent_input` (README 7b: `HPCContinuationEstimator` has no controller of its own). The old commission `yield_bindings['hpc']` is read from settings. The controller stops the Episode only when its cost (`cutoff`) is above zero and units stop earning.
- `hpc_monitor_wait` still sleeps in the process until the scheduled time (`MonitorCronController.wait`: `asyncio.sleep`, as before); the system has no wait (README 5 item 18).
- outside this folder, broken until the head is carried: `a104/evolutionary_history_evidence/runtime_bindings.py` (line ~382, hook-era home) imports the deleted `hpc_work_planning.binding.HPCBinding`; `a104/evolutionary_history_evidence/tests/test_model_run.py` (line 39) and `tests/test_runtime_head_link.py` (line 18) import the deleted `hpc_work_planning.hpc_runtime`. Not touched here.
- the old tests of this episode bound shared old-root test classes and are retired; the new tests are structural and exercise the pure parts (form, controller, credit, commissions, report, blocked primitives): no real-runner test is possible while the ledger handoff record and both services are blocked.
