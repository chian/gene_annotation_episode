# a105 published_evidence_assessment: blocked, unplaced and contract misfits (19 November 2026)

This episode is the parent of `evidence_search_strategy_improvement`. At 97308b94c its old files are the same as that
child's apart from the role (`literature` against `literature_strategy`), the assignment (`literature_assignment_v1`
with `source_refs` against `strategy_assignment_v1` with `prior_search_refs`), the child (`literature_strategy`
against `literature_search`) and the receive-side functions of `to_parent.py` / `from_child_*.py`. The binding is a
private copy of that carry-over with those differences, so its blocked calls are the same library calls. Each call is
written against its intended custody-aware form, raises `RuntimeError('library_not_carried:...')` at the point of
the call (never at the top of `credit`, of a tool or of a message function) and is reported with its exact pattern.

## Contract misfits

- Tool names: a tool name is `[a-z][a-z0-9_]*`, so `source.read` is `source_read`, `public.literature.search` is `public_literature_search` and `literature.assay_grading.populate` is `literature_assay_grading_populate` ("names both sides use are the old names, unchanged" cannot hold for tools).
- Child slot name: the slot is the child folder name `evidence_search_strategy_improvement`; the old role was `literature_strategy`.
- new field: published_evidence_assessment scope (the old episode took the scope from its request).
- new field: published_evidence_assessment received_record_refs (the old episode took what it received from its binding context). The parent must name every record this episode reads and every record its commission to the child will name: the sources and their artifacts, the question-context records, the proposed annotation and its hypothesis, the records of the commissioned task.
- new field: published_evidence_assessment finding.child_returns, finding.child_finding_refs, finding.child_sheet_refs (what the commissioned child returned: the old parent admitted the child form into its own repositories and held its records through receipt inheritance).
- new field: published_evidence_assessment report tool (`sources`: source_ref, statement, qualification): the old report was the parent's declared typed form filled from the model's report update; the model's closing value is now the outcome of the declared `report` tool.
- The 39 other `requested_*` properties of the old assignment schema are declared as plain optional lists (the old enums are the family libraries' COMPONENTS, checked where the family is read); only the three of the task kinds have options. The RETURN_FORM declares the cell families as list fields with a shortened meaning, without an item form.
- This episode reads the child's returned records from the nested `record_refs` fields of the child's `finding` (`assessment_refs`, `sheet_refs`, `child_finding_refs`, `child_sheet_refs`); the child declares no top-level `record_refs` field. The returned cells of a family name their sheets in a list field, not in `record_refs`, so a sheet is held here only through those `finding` fields.
- The old model envelope for the child (`strategy_assignment_v1`: `version`, `question_context_refs`, `prior_search_refs`, `literature_task`, `proposed_annotation_ref`, requested components) is now the `ChildCall` arguments `objective` and `strategy` (`prior_search_refs`); `msg_to_child` adds the question context, the task, the proposed annotation, every requested array it was given, the scope and all held records.
- Completion: the target is `credit_unit` times the number of experiments (`a105_experiments`) or `credit_unit` (the other non-lead tasks). Credit cannot pass it: at most one answer per target unit (the assessment, screening, measurement and assembly answers are single; the experiment answers are keyed by experiment), so no "completion credit may pass its target" case arises. `settings['cost']` must be below the target (`completion_cutoff_must_be_below_target`), and for `verified_source_leads` (accumulation) a cost above zero is needed for the controller ever to stop.
- The acquisition tools need `ctx.settings['tool_registry']` (the old `A105ToolRegistry` constructor arguments: `pdfinfo_executable`, `pdftotext_executable`, `pdftoppm_executable`, `job_limits`); no service wraps the public clients, so the call is blocked on the registry (above).
