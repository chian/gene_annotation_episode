## Contract misfits
- new field: local_job_execution record_refs (records the masking, utility and NMF job inputs name; old `initial_references`).
- new field: local_job_execution scientific_task (old `services.scientific_parent_task`: `scientific_question`, `selected_tool_info`).
- new field: local_job_execution completion_target (derived in `read_from_parent`, not an assignment field): the sum of the credits of every original `progress_requirements` entry.
- completion credit may pass its target: no (requirement credits are non-negative and the target is their sum); the controller's `ValueError` is the failure if it ever did. A profile `cutoff` of at least the total requirement credit raises `completion_cutoff_must_be_below_target`.
- Dotted old fact-source names (21: `execution.masking_*`, `execution.blast_utility_*`) are not lowercase identifiers: "rename nothing" cannot be met; not declared (`NOT_DECLARABLE`), so a parent cannot select them. All were empty for a launch.
- Object-typed or null sources (`execution_result_origin`, the two database `..._acquisition` objects): a form has no null type; selecting them raises `NotImplementedError`.
- The old `validate_report` checked against the parent's JSON-Schema form; the parent writes no form now: `final_to_parent.validate_report` builds the closed schema of the selected fact fields from the library sources' own `report_schema` and calls the A107 `validate_form` with a resolver over this episode's receipts.
- The old `PracticalYieldEstimator` verified a completion proof; no controller input carries a proof (README 5 item 5): the proof is derived from the held receipts for the model and the report only.
- The old frozen closed credit has no counterpart: the system's `completion['credit']` is returned.
- `ctx.settings` must hold `resources`, `initial_information`, `coordinator_workspace`, `launch_instructions_directory`, `tool_knowledge_directory` and `task_yield_profiles` (`{'launch_local:<request class>': {'cutoff', 'parameters'}}`); the old A107 continuation setting `{'cutoff', 'parameters'}` per binding path is replaced by those profiles.
- `job_access` must be supplied as a service factory (`SERVICES = ('job_access',)`); none exists, so no real runner test can be written without a stand-in (forbidden).
- The old A107 NMF/RNA/Infernal local resource bindings cannot be validated (unplaced above).
