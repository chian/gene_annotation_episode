## CONTRACT MISFITS

- validate_form_not_built:A110: the old `validate_report` called `validate_form(form, original_schema, resolver)` first; A110 has no report-form validator, so `messages/final_to_parent.py validate_report` raises `NotImplementedError('validate_form_not_built:A110')` after its fact-agreement check, and `final_to_parent` raises every time it is reached (tested). The parent-written JSON-Schema form that call validated no longer exists.
- new field: hpc_work_planning scope (assignment, record; the old code took it from the request message); new field: hpc_work_planning input_refs (assignment, `record_refs`; the records the planned jobs read: the launch and execution children's forms take them).
- `commission_source_ref` of the assignment is a `record_refs` field holding exactly one reference (`read_from_parent` raises `hpc_commission_source_ref_must_hold_exactly_one_reference` otherwise); the old field was a single text (reference). `commission_id` is free text (the old `enum` came from the construction settings, which a form cannot read); exactly one of the two is required. `tool_request` must name all five keys (the old `obj` required all). The children's forms declare `commission_source_ref` as text, so it is passed to them as text and they do not hold the source.
- new field (sent to the prediction child): hpc_resource_prediction ledger_refs (blocked, above); (sent to the execution child): hpc_allocation_execution scope and input_refs; (sent to the launch child): execution_job_submission handoff_unit, handed_off_resource_ids, record_refs and hpc_execution_job (the launch child declares `hpc_execution_job`, so what `LaunchPreparation.initial_decision_context` supplied rides in the assignment); (sent to the results child): execution_result_acquisition resource_kind (`hpc`) and acquired_record_unit.
- the two children disagree on the ledger: `hpc_resource_prediction` reads the owner ledger as custody rows (`hpc_evidence`), `hpc_allocation_execution` opens the `HPCLedger` file by `settings['directory']`. This episode writes the file, so the execution child can open it and the prediction child cannot be given its records.
- the sources returned in `hpc_result_refs` are Store sources; a parent that reads their bytes also needs each source's artifact (decision T5, "name every record"), which the old fact did not name. No field is added.
- the results child's form declares no `known_result_refs`, so the old `known_result_refs` argument of a results commission is not carried.
- the tools are handed no summary, so `action` adds `scope` to the arguments of every model-selected tool call, and `decide` adds `commission_identity`, `scope` and the tool request to `hpc_initialize`. `hpc_initialize` is new: the old `state` and `initialize` ran as a side effect of the first decision context and of every `execute`; `decide` cannot write, so it offers `hpc_initialize` as the first unit (no model call). Credit before that unit is zero.
- the ledger a child writes is read again by the unit after a commission of it: `decide` offers `hpc_read` of the commission when the last unit was `hpc_prediction` or `hpc_execution` (the old decision context read the ledger live at every decision). `credit` has no ledger: it takes the ledger credit from the last tool outcome, or from the `hpc_credit` the execution child returned (its credit is that same ledger view; not a sum of children's credits). After an `hpc_prediction` unit the model credit it wrote shows only at the next unit, so that unit's marginal credit is understated.
- RETURN_FORM: `finding` is `FINDING_FORM` (accepted and remaining obligations, plans frozen and closed, results held, the credit components, a qualification). The four old fact sources `hpc_credit`, `hpc_model_ref`, `hpc_accepted_obligations`, `hpc_result_refs` are selectable (`required=False`).
- yield: `AccumulationYieldController('hpc_work_planning', initial_credit, settings['cutoff'], settings['parameters'])` (README 7b: `HPCContinuationEstimator` has no controller of its own; the credit is a signed measured gain with no maximum or target; the old commission `yield_bindings['hpc']` is the settings). The controller stops the Episode only when its cost (`cutoff`) is above zero and units stop earning. Completion credit does not apply, so "completion credit may pass its target" does not arise.
- `selection` of the commissions: `hpc_prediction` selects `hpc_credit` and `hpc_accepted_obligations` (its `hpc_model_ref`, `hpc_result_refs` and `hpc_ledger_refs` are required fields); `hpc_execution` the four facts; `launch` `execution_handoff_refs` and `execution_completed`; `monitor` the seven `monitoring_*` fields the monitor's form declares selectable (not `monitoring_dependency_handles`); `results` nothing (its three result fields are required).
- tool names carry underscores (`hpc_read`, ..., `hpc_monitor_wait`): `[a-z][a-z0-9_]*` does not admit the old dotted names `hpc.read`, `hpc.monitor.wait`.
- The remote project directory `/lus/flare/projects/BioReason-Aurora-Test` stays a constant of `hpc_discovery_run` (as in the old code and AGENTS.md).
- `a110/biochemical_feasibility_evidence/home.py` and `a110/biochemical_feasibility_evidence/tests/test_model_run.py` (the head's files, not this episode's) still import the deleted `hpc_work_planning/hpc_runtime.py`: they break until the head is carried. `hpc_work_planning/episode.py` of the earlier port (`HpcWorkPlanningEpisode(HPCRuntime, EpisodeRuntimeContract)`) is replaced by `HPCWorkPlanning(EpisodeBinding)`.
- the old tests of this episode ran shared old-root test classes and the deleted hook-era runtime and are retired; the new tests are structural and exercise the pure parts (forms, controller, credit, commissions, report, blocked primitives): no real-runner test is possible while the ledger handoff record and both services are blocked.
- `ctx.settings` holds the check that at least one `pbs_ssh` resource is configured (`resources_of`), raised at `hpc_initialize`.
