## Fourth source step: protein-feature coordinate attribution

Source review found a concrete region-use defect: the native sequence reader
expects a complete query sequence artifact, while a feature supplies its
sequence through parent extraction. Exact region identity already worked, but
a nonidentical protein feature could fail before its received parent alignment
was interpreted.

`component_sequence_attribution` now composes the actual received feature
segments into protein-symbol coordinates and projects the original native
pairs into the requested local coordinates. Nested and discontinuous extraction
paths retain their declared order; reversed protein traversal and noninjective
paths remain unsupported. Protein strand `.` follows the original reader's
nonreversed extraction convention and does not become a nucleotide-strand
claim. A translated product's nucleotide footprint is not substituted for its
protein-symbol frame.

A received native alignment to the exact reference-region sequence is used
directly. Only when that direct correspondence has no native witnesses does

Conflicting direct correspondence is not bypassed by selecting a parent map.
