## What was verified against the live service (and how)

All of it through the project's authenticated client (`BVBRCAnalysisClient`, `prepare_rpc_request`), one hand-made protein alignment (12 sequences in 3 groups of 4, plus a 13th sequence `T0` standing for the target, 23 columns) uploaded under `gene_annotation_episode_a101/metacats_probe/{a,b}`; probe scripts are outside the repository (scratchpad); results kept in `runs/metacats_probe_3c/out` and `runs/metacats_probe_4b/out`.

- The application list (`AppService.enumerate_apps`, `runs/metacats_probe_2/MetaCATS.json`) shows six parameters (alignment_file, group_file, p_value, alignment_type, output_path, output_file). The deployed script does NOT work with only those: `start_app2` answered HTTP 500, "Required parameter The input type. (input_type) missing" (`runs/metacats_probe_3b`). The job record of a finished job carries the application's own full definition (`runs/metacats_probe_3c/out/result`, the `job_result` file) and it is the one retained in the repository (`forms/application_service_specs/original/MetaCATS.json`): `input_type` (`auto`, `groups`, `files`; required), `alphabet` (`na`, `aa`; required), `alignment_file`, `group_file`, `alignment_type`, `p_value`, `output_path`, `output_file`. The source of the deployed script is public (github.com/BV-BRC/bvbrc_metacats: `service-scripts/App-MetaCATS.pl`, `scripts/metadata_parser.pl`, `scripts/Meta-CATS.R`); it was read for the group-file rules and the result table, and then every rule below was checked live.
- INPUT FORM: `input_type` `files`, `alphabet` `aa`, `alignment_type` `aligned_protein_fasta`, `alignment_file` and `group_file` as workspace paths in the account's own workspace (a path, not an upload: accepted at once), `p_value`. The alignment object was created with type `aligned_protein_fasta` and the group file with type `tsv` through `Workspace.create` with the text as the object's data: accepted. WHICH ONE: the input files are written under the job's own folder (the route entry's `uploads`), not taken from the MSA's folder, because the alignment the job takes is a derived one (below).
- GROUP FILE: TSV, one header line (`Seq_ID<TAB>group`), then `identifier<TAB>label` per sequence; the identifier is the first word of the FASTA header; the groups are the distinct labels of the second column.
- A SEQUENCE OF THE ALIGNMENT IN NO GROUP: NOT left out. Probe `runs/metacats_probe_3c` (alignment with `T0`, group file without it): the result table has a first group column `Unlabeled` that holds `T0` (`1 M`, `1 R` at the positions where it differs), the statistics change (degrees of freedom 3 instead of 2, chi-square 12.99 instead of 11.99 at position 4) and the group counts shift. So the TARGET WOULD BE A GROUP OF ITS OWN and would distort every statistic. Therefore code derives from the held alignment a second alignment without the target's row (and without any sequence of no group), with the SAME columns, and the job takes that one; the target's residues are read from the held alignment at the same columns. Probe `runs/metacats_probe_4b` (the derived alignment): no `Unlabeled` column, the statistics are the 12-sequence ones. The derived alignment and the group file are what the child builds (both equal the probe's inputs byte for byte: checked directly, `runs/metacats_check_2`).
- RESULT FILES (`<output_path>/.<output_file>/`): `<header of the group column>-chisqTable.tsv` and `<header>-mcTable.tsv` (here `group-chisqTable.tsv`, `group-mcTable.tsv`); the job's own record `<output_path>/<output_file>` (type `job_result`) holds the application's definition and parameters. `chisqTable`: the header `Position, Chi-square_value, P-value, Degrees_of_freedom, Fewer_5` and one column per group (in the order of the sorted labels), then only the positions whose chi-square p-value is below `p_value` (the service's own filter: the probe at 0.05 gave positions 4, 5 and 16 of 23); a group cell holds the residue counts `4 L` or `13 C, 9 S`; a gap is the residue `-` (`4 -`); `Position` is the 1-based alignment column (checked against the probe's alignment). `mcTable`: `Position, Multiple_comparison_p-value, Groups` (the pairs of groups that differ). Terminal gaps are replaced by `#` and not counted (script; not exercised).
- GROUP LABELS: the script cuts a label at its last `/` (script; a product name has one: `CspB/CspG/CspI`). So code writes the labels (`group_01`...) and the sheet keeps the product of each label.
- DURATION: the two accepted jobs took 7 s and 8 s of the service's own time (task record: submit to completed).
- NOT VERIFIED: the smallest group the service accepts (the child uses at least 2 sequences per group, 2 groups: the owner's requirement and what a chi-square test of a count table needs; the table flags `Fewer_5` where any count is below 5, which every probe group did); terminal gaps; more than 3 groups; a dna alignment (`alphabet` `na`); the `mcTable` is saved by the child but not read.

## The requirement and the records

- The commission's alignment is a row of `data_products` of kind `multiple_sequence_alignment` (the `bvbrc_msa` child's extract writes it). The head's commission check and the child's `estimate` and `launch` checks refuse it with the product's code (`data_product_not_held`, `data_product_kind_mismatch`; field `alignment_ref`). The head offers the slot only while a held alignment gives at least two groups of at least two sequences (`metacats_options`; each shows its groups and sizes).
- The groups are read from the held alignment's source (provider `bvbrc_msa`, whose alignment artifact the row labels) and its sheet: each reference sequence (`r1`...) with its candidate; the target's row is the sheet's `target_name`. Code builds one group per candidate, the target in none.
- The report: the child's extract records the service's chi-square table as a data product of kind `alignment_position_report` (EDAM data_0867, format_3475, TSV: the table is TSV): `generated_by` the job's handoff row, `used` the group file's artifact (its reference is the digest of its bytes, known before the launch), `derived_from` the alignment's product row, `settings` `{application: MetaCATS, p_value, groups: [sorted products]}`, `locations` the table's path in the BV-BRC workspace (from the job's result catalog). Before `launch`, `find_reusable` with the same kind, used and settings: a held report refuses the launch (`alignment_position_report_already_held`), and the head's commission check refuses the commission.
- The sheet (`metacats_sheet_v1`, kept as a grading artifact): the groups (label, product, size), the commission's p-value, and for each position the service reports as significant (its statistic, p-value, degrees of freedom and `Fewer_5`): the residue counts per group and the target's residue; per group its VALUE = the mean over the significant positions of the fraction of the group's sequences that carry the target's residue there (a target gap counts 0). No significant position: `no_significant_position` true and no value for any candidate (the test measured that the groups do not differ: a result that places no candidate). A source of provider `bvbrc_metacats`, a `reference` record per group (label = product) and a `comparison` observation per group with a value are kept.
- Direct check of the arithmetic on the probe's real table (NOT a real run, `runs/metacats_check_2`): target `T0` (M at column 4, S at 5, R at 16), groups of 4: significant positions 4, 5, 16; fractions group_1 0, 1, 1; group_2 1, 0, 1; group_3 1, 0, 0; values 0.667, 0.667, 0.333; a table with no row gives `no_significant_position` true.

## Run C1: prediction (written before the run)

Target CspA (P0A9X9, 70 residues; fetched from rest.uniprot.org), fresh workspace `runs/metacats_ws_C1`, run `runs/metacats_run_C1` (run id `metacats_C1`), local and BV-BRC resources, Pfam configured, the global ledger, the head's yield as in `a101_diff_ws_H1` (accumulation, cutoff 0.1). One `Homology`, one `MSA` and one `MetaCATS` submission (the fifth MetaCATS request).

1. The head's model: `hmmer_profile_scan` and `bvbrc_blast` as in H1; then `bvbrc_msa` with two to four candidates (probability 0.6), then `bvbrc_metacats` (probability 0.5 given an alignment: its description says what the test does and that it can differentiate candidates already listed); then reasoning until the estimator stops it. If it stops before `bvbrc_metacats`, that is the finding.
2. The child: `estimate` (basis `no_earlier_measurement`: the ledger has no MetaCATS row; the model's number 20 to 120 s), `launch`, `monitor` (first status after the 60 s minimum shows `finished`; the service's own duration 5 to 20 s), `extract`: 4 units, credit 4.0 = target. One ledger row of kind `bvbrc_metacats:bvbrc_metacats:MetaCATS:protein` with `{application, sequences, groups, columns}`, the measured duration the service's own.
3. The alignment of CspA with up to 4 candidates of 3 to 10 sequences has 70 to 75 columns; the groups hold 3 to 10 sequences each. I expect 3 to 20 significant positions at 0.05 (small groups of paralogues that differ at many columns), and fewer at 0.01. If the model picks 0.01 and the groups are close, zero is possible (probability 0.15): then no value, no credit from the test, and the sheet says so.
4. Values: the group of the CspA product (the target is itself a CspA) carries the target's residue at most significant positions: 0.8 to 1.0; the CspB/CspG/CspI group 0.3 to 0.7; the others between. Each candidate's score becomes the mean of its earlier test values and this one; the differentials of the pairs listed are |change of A - change of B| and I expect their sum 0.1 to 0.8, so the head's credit rises at the extract unit by that much (no admission term: every group candidate is already listed).
5. The returned candidates in order: the CspA product first; the gaps between the first and the others grow (the CspA product's value in the new test is high, the others' low, so the score moves of the first and the rest differ).
6. No file of the run directories contains the token's signature (`sig=` followed by hex).
