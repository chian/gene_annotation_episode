structure, experimental structure, or both. Experimental-only work can return
authenticated experimental numerical cells without creating a native alignment
or a comparative B score. Current selections no longer offer A111 as a separate
axis. Historical catalogs, A111 records, formula versions and results remain
readable with their original meanings.

qualifications, provenance and dependencies. Reusing a model or an experiment
does not create independent evidence. There is no invented combined scalar.
Scientific findings and controller progress remain distinct.
