  Prefix acquisition remains incomplete for a required full population.

  unavailable distances for the changed population. Failed projection retains

  committed outcome, context and target. Staged controls do not grant completed

  delivery, private duplicate-credit lineage, multi-hop forwarding prefixes,

  loss are checked. Historical facts remain valid; new saved operands require
  currently available exact bytes.
