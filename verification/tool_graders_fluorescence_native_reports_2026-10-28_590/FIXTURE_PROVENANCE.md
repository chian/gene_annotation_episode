The three `source_format_*_fixture` files were written as synthetic parser test
inputs after reading the complete original functions. They are not captured
native executions, laboratory observations, or proof of a compatible cytokine
deployment. Their numbers are chosen to exercise deterministic parsing and the
existing shared calculator.

The original source file and complete function hashes (including their final
LF) match proposal573/SOURCE_MANIFEST_001.json. The parser records the matching
profile source identity and always records supplied origin and unverified
upstream execution. It does not assert that these functions ran.

intermediate lower/upper counts2 and1. Cytokine fixtures: target50.0%, control75.0%,
native background-clipped0.0 percentage points. The shared calculator alone
