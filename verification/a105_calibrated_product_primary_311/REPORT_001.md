# A105 paired substrate and product audit 311

## Decision

**The selected primary paper does not meet the requested complete-assay criterion.** Zaman and Friesen, *Establishment of a New and Viable Assay to Characterize Wheat Germ Acid Phosphatase Using 31P NMR Spectroscopy* (2024), [original article](https://www.jyi.org/2024-may/2024/5/31/establishment-of-a-new-and-viable-assay-to-characterize-wheat-germ-acid-phosphatase-using-31p-nmr-spectroscopy), measures and identifies inorganic phosphate product and includes a no-enzyme NMR control. It does **not** report a numerical substrate concentration or substrate peak integral after incubation for the same active assay. Its product amount uses the starting substrate peak as a molar reference, without a demonstrated substrate/product response-factor or relaxation validation. Thus it cannot yield a verified same-assay substrate-loss/product-gain balance, even though an example product concentration is extractable. I stopped at this candidate as assigned.

The retained 142-group review was the starting point. The FolM 2010 packet at `research/experimental_scoring_review_2026-10-23/template_a105_reactions_001/09_folm_2010_001.md` was inspected first; it explicitly lacks a calibrated paired product amount. Gruber 2026, P205 and the retained PARP1 packet were excluded by the parent brief's specified calibration, identity and control gaps. Search then selected this original paper. No other original paper was inspected.

Experimental comparability: the NMR enzyme-concentration and time series share stated buffer, substrate, temperature and readout conditions, but differ in the intended variable. UV and NMR series have different enzyme concentrations and assay aliquots; their specific activities cannot be used as a same-assay stoichiometric pair. Effect isolation: a no-enzyme Pi nondetection addresses spontaneous phosphate production under that condition, but an unquantified control, a commercial enzyme preparation and no numerical final-substrate value leave the magnitude and molecule-specific account unresolved. These are distinct questions.

## Numerical sheet crosswalk and explicit arithmetic

Keep the assayed pNPP→Pi reaction account separate from target correspondence. Let (S_{A0},S_{At},S_{C0},S_{Ct}) be active and matched-control pNPP concentrations at zero and endpoint time; (P_{A0},P_{At},P_{C0},P_{Ct}) the corresponding Pi concentrations, all in mM. One pNPP yields one Pi in the stated hydrolysis model, so the proposed product-per-substrate coefficient is ν=1. These fields require actual source spans or dataset columns and matching assay/aliquot identities; the present source does not provide all eight.

| A105 numerical cell | Reader operands / calculation | This paper's population | What it evaluates |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Product example | (P_{At}=3.368(1.50\times10^6/2.67\times10^7)=0.1892) mM as reported, lines 262-264 | 0.1892 mM, source calculated; calibration qualification retained | Pi signal converted to concentration; not independent from the initial substrate reference. |

| Control-corrected parent loss (L) | ((S_{A0}-S_{At})-(S_{C0}-S_{Ct})) | **Unknown** | Substrate loss isolated to the active condition. |
| Control-corrected Pi gain (G) | ((P_{At}-P_{A0})-(P_{Ct}-P_{C0})) | **Unknown:** Pi control is nondetected, with no numeric limit; no paired starting Pi values | Product gain isolated to the active condition. |
| Stoichiometric agreement | (R=G-\nu L) mM, and (C=1-|R|/(G+\nu L)) for positive denominator and nonnegative operands | **Unknown** | Numerical mass-account consistency, where 1 means equality. (C) is not a product identity or annotation probability. |
| Measurement uncertainty | Joint replicate covariance of (L,G), plus calibration uncertainty | **Unknown** | Precision of the paired account; the UV/NMR group spreads do not substitute for it. |

The only arithmetic available from the reported example is (0.1892/3.368\approx0.05618), or about 5.62% of the stated initial substrate scale. **Do not label this a substrate-conversion fraction, coupling factor, mass recovery or A105 score**: it lacks the matching final substrate, quantitative control amount and validated response relationship.

For a future complete paired source, one *proposed* within-assay combination, with weights still to be reviewed, is (E=[w_L(L/S_{A0})+w_G(G/(\nu S_{A0}))]\times C), (w_L+w_G=1), after explicit validity and effect-isolation qualifications. It sums two normalized measured aspects and multiplies their stoichiometric agreement; it is not an adopted credit formula. (L,G,C) share observations and must remain one dependent assay account, not three independent experiments. Missing operands make (E) unavailable; no zero or one is imputed. Identity, calibration, conditions, controls, replication, uncertainty and molecular attribution remain separately represented rather than silently folded into (C). A different complementary assay could address each named weakness and contribute separately only if physically independent.

The current `literature_parent_depletion_*` reader is for a particular XLSX relative-removal row and does not read general quantitative NMR substrate endpoints. The current `literature_product_coupling_*` section accepts product-versus-**cofactor** rates and cannot relabel pNPP loss as cofactor consumption. `literature_a105_worksheet.py` can group these sections but does not authenticate a same-aliquot substrate/product mass account or compute the cross-section combination above. This is a concrete extraction/sheet capability gap, not permission to force this paper's product signal into an existing inappropriate cell.
