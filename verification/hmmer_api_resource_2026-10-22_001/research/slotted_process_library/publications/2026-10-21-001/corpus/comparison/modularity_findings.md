# Comparison research: what actually recombines?

This is a comparison of recorded investigations, not a library taxonomy or an episode design. The published outcomes were known before the live probes. No experiments, annotation runs, model episodes, alignments, docking, or database queries were performed for this comparison. References below identify the full trace and the case record used to check its controls and limits.

The records support **limited recombination of two different decisions**: which unsupported attachment to a claim is being reconsidered, and which comparison could expose that attachment. They do not support a complete, general set of middle slots. Some attractive apparent dimensions are merely the requested annotation scope, evidence provenance, or the biological parameters of a comparison. Several combinations require the same biological relationship to be specified in both decisions; that is a compatibility condition, but can also collapse the proposed separation if neither decision adds anything independent. The later held-out extension below preserves H1's original meanings: it finds substantial coverage failures rather than renaming those meanings to fit the new cases.

## Paired tests with one component held constant

### 1. Same HemK protein, same catalytic-core versus substrate question; change the comparator search

Held constant: do not copy a reference enzyme's substrate merely because a catalytic-region correspondence is plausible.

In live route A, Dam's DPPY and HemK's NPPY did not align in a meaningful broad region under the declared local-alignment settings. This led to a different DNA reference, M.TaqI, rather than treating one poorly chosen exemplar as a verdict on the family. The M.TaqI comparison gave a longer aligned region and a published active-site analogy, but still did not decide DNA versus protein substrate.

In live route B, a PrmB-versus-proteome score search made HemK the outstanding nonself candidate in that particular search. Inspecting the longer aligned core justified pursuing related chemistry. The decisive substrate comparison came from reported tests: YfcB/PrmB modifies L3 rather than release factors, whereas HemK modifies release factors.

Separate decisions are visible: which reference is worth comparing, and which property that comparison licenses transferring. The first can change while the second remains fixed. This is stronger than a comparison of differently named tools because both routes used the same protein and declared alignment settings. It does not establish that reference selection is a standalone middle slot: selecting a useful reference still depends on the property questioned.

Sources: `sequence/hemk_live_routes.json`, routes A and B; `sequence/verified_cases.jsonl`, `seq_hemk_prmc_ecoli`. Raw scores have no calibrated significance here. All modern function labels were visible.

### 2. Same catalytic-core versus substrate question; change from rival inputs to predictor-decoupling relatives

Held constant: a shared or conserved feature may support part of an annotation without identifying the exact substrate.

In TM0936 route 1, the useful discriminations are substrate activities: cytosine derivatives fail, while SAH/MTA/adenosine turn over and AMP/SAM do not under the authors' conditions. AMP is an explicit docking false positive. This asks what the same enzyme does with rival substrates.

In TM0936 route 2, the question changes to whether an arginine rule predicts SAH activity across relatives. Bc1793 retains an aligned arginine but lacks measured SAH activity; Tvn0515 lacks both discussed arginines but has SAH activity. This chooses relatives that separate a proposed predictor from the measured property. The modeled loop/contact explanations remain explanations rather than newly observed geometry.

HisN supplies a separate case of the latter comparison layout. A more globally similar Actinoplanes candidate lacking motif 5 failed complementation, while less similar motif-bearing candidates succeeded. Here the relationship under pressure is overall similarity as a predictor of HolPase activity, not the TM0936 arginine rule. Overexpression rescue by cg0911 does not establish its normal physiological contribution.

The TM0936 pair is an actual within-case recombination: the same inherited-specificity problem is attacked using two distinct comparison layouts, with different next choices. The HisN case shows that the second layout is reusable beyond the particular arginine story. These are not obligatory consecutive stages.

Sources: `structure/tm0936_traces.jsonl`, both routes; `structure/cases.jsonl`, `TM0936_Hermann_2007` and `BC1793_Hitchcock_2013`; `sequence/published_deep_traces.jsonl` and `sequence/verified_cases.jsonl`, `seq_hisn_impase_discrimination`.

### 3. Same rival-substrate comparison layout; change what earlier information must survive failure

Held constant: compare the same candidate preparation against chemically distinct candidate inputs, retaining conditions and product identity.

TM0936 and the archaeal GHMP-kinase investigation use substrate contrasts to determine how far a family relationship extends. The GHMP investigation additionally contrasts two paralogs: RMJ07785 has reported shikimate activity, RMJ01903 homoserine activity, without detected cross-activity in the reported panels. Family resemblance is not the substrate answer. The HemK investigation also has substrate comparisons, but the route summary does not report a HemK-versus-L3 negative; it must not be silently converted into a complete reciprocal substrate matrix.

QueF uses the same general comparison layout for a different update. Controlled cyclohydrolase negatives reject an inherited reaction assignment. Independent pathway association is retained, so the authors reconsider later reactions rather than treating the failure as evidence against all queuosine involvement. Archaeal distribution and the preQ0 branch boundary narrow the alternatives; absence of a putative B12-binding motif helps prioritize one reduction. NADPH-dependent product formation and product identification establish that selected reaction. A motif negative did not establish the product.

Thus the comparison layout does not itself specify what survives failure. One inquiry preserves common catalytic-family compatibility while resolving substrate; the other preserves pathway placement while replacing the proposed chemistry. This is a genuine partial recombination. It does not justify preserving catalytic chemistry in QueF merely because chemistry was preserved in the HemK investigation.

Sources: the HemK and TM0936 records above; `context/contrasting_investigations.json`, both entries; `context/verified_cases.jsonl`, `context_archaeal_shikimate_kinase_2001` and `context_quef_nitrile_reductase_2005`; `sequence/verified_cases.jsonl`, `seq_queF_not_folE`. Sequence and context QueF records describe the same published case, not independent replication.

### 4. Same proposed universal dependence; change the evidential contrast

Held constant: distinguish a restricted association from a claim that one component is obligatory whenever the relevant system operates.

OarX route B compares deposited organism profiles. FmnO is recorded in 22 of 87 OarX-containing rows and blank in 65. The child-like decision is whether to pursue an obligatory pair or conditional partnership; the result makes the former unsafe. However, a blank is no recorded assignment, not exhaustive gene absence, and the OarX-selected cohort cannot estimate association against OarX-negative organisms.

The Y-complex route B uses selective substrate behavior in perturbations. cggR/gapA ends are strongly reduced in Y-complex mutants, but the mature rnpB signal remains high compared with its reduction in an rny mutant. This selects a substrate that can separate loss of all RNase Y activity from a restricted accessory dependency. These track values are live descriptive extractions; the end-chemistry experiment supporting processing rather than an internal transcription start is the authors' work.

Both inquiries constrain an overbroad dependence rule, but their evidential force differs. Natural-profile gaps and measured retained function are not interchangeable thought-pattern values. Which comparison to seek can be modular; its epistemic status must stay with the record.

Sources: `context/oarx_actual_routes.json`, route B; `context/verified_cases.jsonl`, `context_oarx_thiamin_2024`; `cellular/detailed_traces.json`, `cellular_y_complex`, route B; matching `cellular/verified_cases.jsonl` record.

### 5. Same phenotype improvement; change the causal question

Held constant: an improved phenotype does not by itself identify the restored molecular relationship.

YabA selective interaction mutants remove DnaA or DnaN connections while preserving others. Complementary variants restore localization and initiation control; same-defect coexpression is a control. The informative contrast concerns whether two distinct connections can jointly supply the function.

YihI improves growth of derT57I under reported conditions, but the purified T57I protein shows neither detectable catalytic activation nor competition for YihI in the reported tests. The contrast asks whether endpoint rescue repairs the proposed catalytic defect. It leaves indirect compensation viable.

YhcB suppressors modify other envelope pathways and restore resistance under two challenges. That coherence supports an envelope-balance interpretation but does not isolate a unique physical connector. Multiple suppressors are not equivalent to the interaction-separation controls in YabA.

The word "rescue" is an assay/outcome label, not a reusable inference. A common process can ask whether improvement restores a named link, but it cannot choose a useful control until that link and competing explanation are supplied. Here causal relationship and contrast construction are conditionally coupled, and a slot containing only "rescue" fails.

Sources: `root/rescue_comparison.json` (checked against the case records, not accepted as an instruction); `cellular/verified_cases.jsonl`, `cellular_yabA`, `cellular_yihI`, `cellular_yhcB`; `cellular/detailed_traces.json`, YhcB route B. Full 2010 YihI paper was unavailable in the collection; the 2013 full results support the specific rescue/catalysis comparison.

### 6. Same rule not to assign an observation to the wrong entity; change whether attribution itself is the scientific uncertainty

In OarX route A, comparing the deposited sequence with the experimental-strain genome reveals nonidentical strain records and a boundary difference. Preserving that mapping is a provenance obligation. It prevents combining incompatible details but is not, by itself, a selectable scientific thought pattern.

In the CodA investigation, extract activity's source is an unresolved scientific question. Lack of expected thermostability and tag-purification behavior changes the live hypothesis from the intended archaeal construct to a host enzyme. Activity-guided purification, peptide identity, and independently prepared CodA then settle the attribution. The initial archaeal protein remains unassigned; insoluble expression is not a negative activity measurement for that protein.

In TM0936, recognizing that a bound methionine can occupy a peripheral recognition region rather than prove a catalytic substrate likewise changes the investigated hypothesis. A contact record alone does not perform the experiment needed to establish the relationship.

Therefore "check provenance" and "find the biological source of an unexpected signal" cannot be bundled as one middle thought unit. They share an obligation to track entities, but only the latter sometimes defines the scientific competition.

Sources: OarX route A; `sequence/published_deep_traces.jsonl` and `sequence/verified_cases.jsonl`, `seq_codA_isoguanine_failed_candidate`; TM0936 route 2.

### 7. Held-out check: same activity-attribution problem, change from host contamination to a coupled pathway assay

After H1 was drafted, a source from the chemistry lane supplied a useful challenge. The F420 study reports that changing the CofD/FbiA partner changes the apparent substrate preference of a CofC/FbiD-containing coupled assay. In particular, the apparent 2-PL activity of Msmeg-FbiD largely disappears with cognate Msmeg-FbiA instead of Mjan-CofD. The measured final product reflects both enzymes, so varying substrate with FbiD held fixed does not isolate FbiD's contribution.

Held constant with CodA: real activity must not automatically be assigned to the intended component. Varied: CodA follows activity through purification and identifies a host enzyme; F420 varies a downstream partner to expose filtering of upstream products. This is a reusable scientific attribution problem, rather than a generic record-ID check. It constrains H1: its rival-substrate layout can discriminate the **measured system's** behavior, but the proposed unit of attribution must remain explicit. Partner-swapping is a different useful comparison, compatible with separating component contributions. Neither case authorizes deleting a genuine aggregate observation merely because its original attribution fails.

Source inspected for this held-out check: `chemistry/raw/PMC8764529.paragraphs.txt`, complete "Role of CofD in substrate specificity" Results section, complete Discussion, and complete combined-assay Methods subsection. This is authors' experimental evidence, not an assay rerun or a systematic review of the chemistry collection.

## One small factoring worth retaining as a hypothesis

H1 is a **local** two-part factoring of the comparison decisions above. It is not a declaration of library slots. The first part chooses an update operation; the second chooses a comparison layout. They must share named biological variables and available evidence.

Part 1, possible values observed here:

* **Separate common catalytic machinery from copied substrate specificity.** The inquiry keeps evidence for a related core distinct and seeks the property that could warrant or defeat substrate transfer. This constrains which conclusion to retain after a mismatch and what remains unresolved.
* **Retain independently grounded pathway placement while replacing a failed reaction.** The inquiry explicitly asks which alternative reaction fits the surviving pathway constraints. This constrains which hypotheses are reopened after a negative result.
* **Separate endpoint rescue from restoration of the proposed molecular defect.** The inquiry asks for the missing molecular link rather than equating an improved phenotype with its repair. This constrains what a follow-up result must distinguish.

Part 2, possible values observed here:

* **Keep the candidate fixed and compare rival substrate or co-substrate identities.** This constrains the next contrast to distinguish substrate-specific chemistry; it does not dictate that a new experiment must be available or performed. The F420 check shows that the conclusion concerns the measured preparation/system unless component-specific attribution is separately supported.
* **Choose relatives that separate a proposed predictor from the measured property.** This constrains comparator selection toward cases such as feature-without-activity or activity-without-feature, rather than another concordant family member.
* **Remove individual connections while preserving others, then compare complementary and same-defect restoration.** This constrains the perturbation logic. The present collection supports it specifically through YabA, not through every suppressor or rescue case.

H1 passes a narrow recombination test for substrate and reaction investigations. It has not established a general middle-slot vocabulary. Part 1 risks becoming a description of annotation scope unless its values retain the specified update decisions; "chemistry," "substrate," or "cellular role" alone would fail. Part 2 risks becoming an assay manual unless its comparison logic is retained; "enzyme assay" or "sequence search" alone would fail. The final YabA comparison layout also has only one clear supporting case in this bounded read. No fixed order follows from these assemblies; the same case can use a different compatible comparison on a later decision.

## Failed and unresolved factorings

* **Similarity / exception / agreement as successive positions:** fails. HemK repairs a weak comparison, tests transfer, and revises substrate inference within each route; TM0936 can use substrate negatives and relative counterexamples in different investigations; QueF alternates distribution, sequence/cofactor clues, and biochemical comparisons. The records do not justify a prescribed itinerary.
* **Biological relation × counterexample choice as completely independent values:** fails without a shared typed relationship. A blank FmnO table cell, retained rnpB end, and no T57I activation challenge different implications. Recombining them without their entity, condition, and outcome variables produces no meaningful decision.
* **Rescue / binding / negative assay as thought patterns:** fails. YabA, YihI, and YhcB have different causal targets despite rescue results. ATP retained in MJ0577, peripheral methionine in TM0936, and a computationally annotated TPP riboswitch do not establish the same relationship. Negative activity with a positive control differs from failed expression and from a missing annotation.
* **Annotation level as a middle thought slot:** unsupported. Broad pathway participation versus exact substrate is the parent's requested scientific question/credit scope. The process is how the inquiry tests the disputed transfer or causal link, not the name of that scope.
* **Record identity or measurement origin as selectable thought:** usually an invariant provenance obligation. CodA shows a special case where source attribution becomes the scientific unknown; this does not make every strain-ID check the same process.
* **One process per tool:** fails. One local alignment supported both a poor motif comparison and a useful longer core comparison in HemK. The same processed RNA tracks answered end-loss and retained-control questions. Different tools can implement the same comparison layout, and a useful comparison can require evidence not obtainable by the available tools.

## Held-out extension: spatial, organismal, chemical and causal cases

The original H1 values do **not** generalize as written. EnvC is a direct failure of "preserve common catalytic machinery": the retained fold lacks the corresponding Zn ligands and a catalytic metal, and the authors report no EnvC-alone hydrolase activity. Retaining scaffold resemblance cannot rescue a metallopeptidase assignment. Conversely, stable EnvC variants that reach the septum but fail to activate AmiB support a functional regulatory surface. The proposed direct helix-binding/conformational mechanism remains a model; unsuccessful complex capture did not directly establish that binding event.

Three other exact failures are informative. MurJ's lost cleavage readout raises access-versus-reporter alternatives, not inherited substrate specificity. Ttr's systemic nonphenotype concerns a capability's conditional contribution, not replacement of a failed reaction. The Bcd feasibility route evaluates whether an energy account is sufficient; it is not an activity contrast at all. Calling these "specificity" or "rival inputs" would erase the relevant thought rather than demonstrate composition. CcdA is closer to H1's transfer limit, but "preserved catalytic machinery" still overstates what genetic and acceptor-redox tests establish directly; the supported shared transmembrane reducing task and its acceptor range must be stated at their actual levels.

The broader separation between an **inference move** and a **comparison construction** remains useful in the following bounded assemblies. The contribution-attribution move already appeared in CodA and the first F420 held-out check; it is not a broadened synonym for preserving catalytic machinery. Here it means: keep the aggregate measurement, distinguish which component supplies the reaction versus makes it observable/possible, and revise only the contributor claim contradicted by selective evidence. The comparison choice then specifies which contributions to separate experimentally. These are research reconstructions of compatible selections, not a new frozen list of slots.

These assemblies establish distinct constraints at a **bounded comparison decision**. They do not demonstrate that a whole child investigation can be generated from two values once. A child could instead select another inference move or another compatible comparison after a result. The source records supply no prescribed order, cadence or number of turns.

The strongest held-out crossing holds contribution-attribution constant across EnvC, FtsW, Bcd and F420 while changing the comparison construction. That move cannot itself tell a child whether to inspect stable localization-preserving variants, reciprocal catalytic inactivation, upstream bypass or downstream partner swaps. Conversely, the same selective-loss/retained-function logic can delimit the contribution of an activator (EnvC), distinguish catalyst from partner (FtsW), or test an additional route without erasing existing XoxF activity. These are related comparison logics, but they should not be claimed as identical complete layouts: nested background deletion and retained localization are different constructions with different controls.

Ttr has independent support for its resource-conditioned process beyond one case: the inspected EutC record links nutrient benefit to tetrathionate without a sufficient nutrient-concentration increase; the nitrate record couples host Nos2 perturbation to nitrate and bacterial respiratory fitness; Pdu instead retains a benefit without Ttr and depends on microbiota-derived substrate and broader respiratory capacity. These are compatible variations of asking which prerequisite licenses an already demonstrated capability to matter. The electron acceptor and nutrient cannot be copied between them. Their organismal question is parent scope; the choice to require a resource perturbation that separates correlated inflammation from resource use is the process constraint.

### What does not become a middle thought component

* **Parent scientific question:** "What does EnvC do?", "Which route supports methanol growth?", or "Where does Ttr matter?" supplies the scoped job. Simply placing those questions in a slot would store tasks, not processes. The moves in the table add a policy for interpreting conflicting/partial evidence and for selecting a next discriminating question.
* **Ordinary provenance/measurement metadata:** strain, metal concentration, construct, preparation, reporter definition, normalization and source ownership travel with evidence. The TrxA strain correction and lanthanide contamination can alter whether an observation applies, but "strain correction" and "glassware control" are not process values. In contrast, deliberately treating the signal's biological source as unknown can select a contributor-attribution investigation.
* **Task or tool operation:** purify, mutate, retrieve a PDB component, read a paper, compute a free energy or query a genome does not identify the inference. The Bcd genomic window repair remains ancillary identity work, despite adaptive query choices. Its useful arithmetic addresses redox feasibility; its biochemical controls address causal coupling. They earn different conclusions.
* **Annotation scope:** catalysis, spatial access, organismal benefit and resource-conditioned essentiality describe the requested support. A process can serve one of these aims without making it a new middle slot or a new credit axis.

### Exact remaining failures and unresolved questions

1. **H1 coverage fails.** Its three original update values and three comparison layouts cannot encode these held-out investigations without additions. This extension leaves their meanings intact and does not present the table as an adopted replacement taxonomy.
2. **Pure feasibility is a real hole.** Bcd's standard-potential calculation can reject an incomplete energy explanation while proving neither coupling nor physiological free energy. The provisional "comparison construction" position does not currently represent stoichiometric/thermodynamic accounting honestly. Adding "compare reaction sets" would require its own cases and a clear decision constraint; it has not been validated here.
3. **Attribution versus control may collapse.** EnvC's amount/localization checks are essential evidence that a functional defect is selective. Is "isolate contribution" independently selectable from "preserve those alternate functions," or does a useful definition of either already contain both? The contrasts show distinct choices, but do not establish a universally clean boundary.
4. **Prerequisite parameters versus a reusable move remain conditional.** A metal, partner, compartment, electron acceptor or community nutrient is a biological parameter. A reusable process must say why the evidence supports testing a missing prerequisite, and what would distinguish it from changing protein abundance or a reporter failure. "Try another condition" is insufficient.
5. **A contrast construction can have different evidential meanings.** FtsW's partner restoration establishes a required context for its assayed activity; XoxF metal rescue alone could have reflected regulation; only identity/metal/activity evidence supports catalysis. Sharing an addition/removal layout does not transfer a causal conclusion.
6. **Full process composition remains unverified.** No prospective child chose between these assemblies, discovered its own relevant alternatives, returned a parent-defined typed form, or had progress/continuation evaluated. The table is source-grounded evidence for separable decision constraints, not proof that the requested complete one-per-slot process library exists.

The Bcd and F420 chemistry case records were read. ## Capabilities omitted and what remains unknown

This bounded comparison does not cover a matched OarX-negative cohort, independent ortholog search, calibrated homology inference, phylogeny, new motif screens, structure prediction, new docking or superpositions, raw sequencing processing, replicate-based statistics, extract fractionation, protein assays, new mutants, microscopy, or direct physiological substrate tests. It also does not demonstrate automated construction of a child, a complete typed parent return, measured credit, duration estimation, or continuation behavior.

The records are unusually informative about some explicit published pivots (QueF, MJ0577, CodA), but other question transitions are our retrospective reconstructions. A published experiment is not a live query, and a live source/data extraction is not a reproduced experiment. Missing author deliberations remain unknown. The later PxpR/MalR comparison and chemistry case records beyond Bcd/F420 were not examined for this bounded comparison. They may provide better crossings or invalidate the apparent separation.

The next empirical need is not another renamed inventory. It is a new case in which a child actually chooses between the two comparison layouts while preserving the same update operation, and a different case in which the same layout is selected under a different update operation, with a constrained parent question and recorded alternatives. The present records supply retrospective examples of that partial crossing, not execution evidence for a complete compositional library.


## Three-slot follow-up: selecting predictor/function counterexamples

The user's current working hypothesis is annotation-credit question × thought pattern × tools. Earlier comparison layouts are candidate content for the thought-pattern slot, not a declaration of extra slots.

**Concrete candidate:** seek comparisons that separate a proposed predictor from supported function. Feature-present/function-absent observations challenge sufficiency; function-present/feature-absent observations challenge necessity. The next choice is a potentially discordant comparator, rather than another concordant example.

Tools can implement feature mapping or retrieve a phenotype table, but the pattern supplies the choice to seek a discordant comparison and the rule for revising the claim.
