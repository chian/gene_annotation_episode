## Implemented boundary

The existing selected status/collect action owns its locked intent, original
practical handoff/start evidence, actual native observation and reservation,
closed-writer capture, bounded C3 worker records, and publication. A completed
publication must reconcile those controls with its original runtime episode,
selected action and exact saved result. A staged receipt does not establish
completion. No new authority database, episode, scheduler or observation is added.

`sheet-evidence.json` is built from actual captured ContactSheet bytes and freezes
its request digest, PopulationExtraction records and ordered TableDescriptors.
Initial creation validates the complete C1 sheet, C2 component joins/selected
projection and exact C3 output table keys. Historical reads use only frozen
controls and Store metadata. Identical bytes keep every original table-key and
population association. The whole sheet-plus-ordered-tables bundle is the saved
operand; the ledger may deduplicate reference handles without changing the bundle.

New acquisition is one original run-plan identity with authentic acquired
bins/contact observations (or a validated complete empty sparse read). Repeated
collection and saved calculation add no new acquired population after frozen
baseline subtraction. Practical completion requires complete original acquisition
and requested delivery, not a positive scientific value. Saved repair may use a
complete original acquisition whose earlier projection was unavailable; it cannot
repair partial native acquisition merely by changing projection parameters.
