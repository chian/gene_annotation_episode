# Lifecycle membership proof, additive 403/004

2026-10-27. Clarification from406's implementation question, approved by root.
No public type, scientific permission or publication shape changes.

ValidatedProteinWorker copies prepared/plan/execution/result. It identifies the
retained handle but does not copy every request/intent/control body. After those
artifact bytes are lost, their complete membership cannot be independently
reconstructed from that closed body alone. Do not claim that it can.

At initial publication407 obtains lifecycle members from the actual retained
native handle and its exact worker-directory paths, checks their retained
artifact metadata, and records ordered deduplicated membership under the403/001
rule. It performs no generic recursive reference expansion and invents no member.

Historical406 proof reconstructs every member whose identity follows from the
copied body. For remaining observed lifecycle members it checks actual artifact
metadata and exact equality/order across immutable publication, receipt and the
completed runtime outcome. A change to a member in one persisted copy rejects.
The remaining membership is original recorded metadata, not a new assertion that
the vanished control bytes were reread. Private staged copies never establish
completed authority. Membership only classifies protected artifacts; no member
list, by itself, grants a scientific value or permission to use that artifact.
