# Controlled reaction examples and saved replay

These three isolated ATGGCT Workflows use scripted decisions and fixture
controllers. They are mechanical execution demonstrations of the root-reviewed
fixtures in tests/test_literature_reaction_integration.py, not configured-model
scientific interpretation, another source review or live host-restart recovery.

The primary CsiD retained extraction preserves its reported product-pair ratio
of1. Sampling moments, named alternative comparison and resource budget remain
unknown. Its logical resource-comparison upper bound of1 does not establish a
point score or measured sufficiency. The original supplemental PDF and its
retained text representation have separate hashes and source witnesses.

The complete synthetic example has ratio mean0.8, mean squared claim error0.05,
proposed relative comparison1/3, resource coverage0.6 and comparison upper0.2.
The incomplete synthetic example retains the same ratio calculations and
resource upper bound while leaving nominal coverage unknown. These values are
synthetic calculator fixtures, not measurements from the CsiD study.

Each case retains the immutable full sheet, exact frozen child form, exact
five-cell admitted parent subset, source hashes and full own-action/observation
histories. Each has three completed actions and observations, one population
operation and one parent receipt; all source-bookkeeping action credit is zero.

The fresh --reopen process disables28 defining-module parser/calculator/
identity helpers and imported aliases, permits only the saved sheet/request
artifacts, and sets SQLite query_only. All three saved outcomes, forms, admitted
subsets and histories compare equal. Original source/PDF/text/dataset bytes are
not read. EXAMPLES_RECEIPT.json and REOPEN_RECEIPT.json record the actual checks.

No measurement correction, network/isotope calculation, whole-axis compilation,
cross-axis credit, target correspondence or annotation credit is established.
