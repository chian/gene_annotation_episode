## Proposed A105 controlled-reference TASK insert

Read the assigned experimental material to determine what the studied molecule
changed in each selected condition and what those measurements establish about
its possible molecular activity. Establish the actual organism, genotype,
molecule, construct, preparation and experimental system. Distinguish the
expression host, the reference molecule and the supplied annotation target.
Identify whether molecules replace one another or coexist, the intended changed
factor, additional differences, endpoint, normalization, units and comparison
denominators. A preparation signal does not by itself isolate the protein's
activity or identify the reaction product.

For each condition, establish the protein-present arm and the actual applicable
protein-free or empty-vector control. Determine whether the controls are distinct
physical observations or the same physical reference with source-supported
applicability and stated invariance. Equal values, labels, image locations or
paper identity do not establish a shared physical owner. A missing or disagreeing
control number does not by itself establish conflicting physical identity or
control applicability. If the source instead makes that scientific relationship
unknown or conflicting, preserve that distinction explicitly.

Preserve complete reported numeric tokens and source locations, actual units,
limits, approximation, reported SD/SEM/CI and confidence level, repetition and
fit basis, and any source-owned joint covariance and observation-model support.
Technical repeats are not biological experiments. Count and spread do not imply
independence, a joint covariance matrix or known variance. Nondetection is not
zero. Keep raw measurements and valid local comparisons when another component
is missing, censored, ambiguous, conflicting or unsupported.

If a selected linear bar supplies a numerical reading, nominate the actual
image, bar region and terminal edge, calibration ticks/pixels, physical unit and
witnessed axis scale. The image label and axis association are your source
interpretations; pixel decoding does not recognize the experimental design.
Graph bounds describe conditional digitization, not SD, SEM, confidence intervals
or additional replication. Do not infer an activity-unit definition from a label.

Read neighboring passages, legends, methods, tables or permitted narrower
literature investigations when they can resolve a concrete uncertainty. Revise
your source nominations or scientific meanings from actual outcomes. These are
alternatives, not a prescribed itinerary or a requirement to read every source.
When multiple representations concern an active measured owner, preserve every
alternative and inspect source authority, rounding, normalization, revision and
graph-selection assumptions. Do not average alternatives, choose the favorable
effect or fabricate a midpoint, uncertainty or canonical reading. Return a
specific unresolved representation question when the source/model does not
settle it.

Nominate the source-backed experimental meanings and operands through the
declared closed request. Deterministic readers parse source literals and pixels,
validate original source covariance and convert units; the separate calculator
populates the numerical cells. Do not supply fabricated scientific numbers,
target-transfer weights, an overall reliability category or annotation credit.
Return only the parent's requested typed results with their necessary scientific,
owner-cancellation, uncertainty, model and unresolved qualifications. A second
model call to restate saved results is unnecessary. Use the ordinary episode
loop; the configured controller owns continuation.
