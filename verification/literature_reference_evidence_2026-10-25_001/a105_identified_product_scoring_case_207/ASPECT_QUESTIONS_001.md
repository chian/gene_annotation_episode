# Proposed aspect-specific interpretation contract

This is a design proposal, not an executable declaration or a new goal. The full scientific TASK is in TASK_PROPOSAL_001.txt. Original source excerpts/figures and actually possessed component rows supply evidence. Interpretation identifies experimental meanings and selectors; deterministic calculators own arithmetic. These finite alternatives are semantic facts, not ordinal quality categories or numbers.

| Question | Proposed declared meanings | Supporting material | Unknown/conflict behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| What endpoint does this scalar measure? | `cofactor_consumption`, `identified_product_formation`, `peroxide_formation`, `calculated_reaction_residual`, `other_reported_endpoint`, `unknown`, `conflicting` | Actual column header, Methods, source cell and chemical identity passages | Retain native scalar; do not promote an unresolved endpoint into functional activity |
| What is the observation's numerical meaning? | `reported_positive_point`, `reported_signed_estimator`, `reported_nondetection`, `reported_upper_bound`, `reported_lower_bound`, `reported_approximation`, `unknown`, `conflicting` | Native number, full comparator and nearby qualification, explicit detection limit | Printed0 plus nondetection is preserved as a native entry; no exact point0 or invented upper limit |
| How is product identity supported? | `authentic_standard_chromatography_and_MS`, `authentic_standard_chromatography_only`, `mass_signal_only`, `source_claim_without_selected_identity_measurement`, `other_selected_identity_measurement`, `unknown`, `conflicting` | Source's identified standard, chromatography and spectrum with actual compound/ion meanings | No numeric1/0 identity grade, no ppm error unless both actual measured and expected mass/ion are available |
| What pairs the product and cofactor rates? | `author_assigned_same_comparison`, `explicit_same_aliquot_time_course`, `explicit_distinct_matched_assays`, `different_system`, `unknown`, `conflicting` | Actual paired arm/table plus preparation/assay passages | Source-assigned pairing supports a qualified descriptive comparison; do not infer stronger physical pairing |
| Are the relevant non-target conditions matched? | Separate status for subject/construct, preparation, partner ratio, substrate, cofactor, matrix/buffer, temperature and time regime: `supported`, `different`, `unknown`, `conflicting` | Actual per-arm conditions; preserve intended change separately | Do not collapse these into an average score or silently borrow a condition from another assay |
| What normalization and correction stage applies? | `unscaled_molar_rate`, `already_per_enzyme`, `already_per_total_protein`, `already_product_coupling_adjusted`, `other_declared_basis`, `unknown`, `conflicting`; separate applied-correction witnesses | Units/denominator and source calculation | Avoid duplicate enzyme normalization/calibration/product coupling; don't inventE=1,k=1or background0 |
| Where does the reaction stoichiometry come from? | `source_stated_reaction_account`, `independently_measured_stoichiometry`, `chemical_model_assumption`, `unknown`, `conflicting` | Explicit equation, stated chemistry or independent measured account | A ratio cannot supply its own stoichiometric coefficient; preserve qualification and no invented certainty |
| Is a secondary endpoint independently measured? | `directly_measured`, `derived_from_selected_endpoints`, `other_shared_measurement`, `unknown`, `conflicting` | Methods/footnote and original dependencies | A water residual and algebraic closure do not add another measured endpoint |
| What uncertainty and replication support this exact statistic? | Explicit source-selectedSD/SEM/CI/covariance/other kinds; unit/level identifiers; `unknown`/`conflicting` separately | Relevant row/legend/Methods and preparation identities | No per-point triplicate inheritance toTable3, no variance from rounded printed digits, no independent-mean/normal model inferred |
| Which effect can be assigned to the studied molecule? | Specific supported control relationship and endpoint; `partner_only_control`, `no_studied_molecule_control`, `contaminant_control`, `other_specific_control`, `unknown`, `conflicting` | Actual measured control, its conditions and intended change | A nonproductive catalytic mutant is not a generic background subtraction; missing control remains missing scientific evidence |

Every supported or different interpretation needs the actual source witnesses specifying that relationship. A conflict needs distinct opposing witnesses; image-label evidence can point to the original figure artifact and region rather than pretend its printed letters are HTML bytes. Unknown requires no fabricated witness. This should be implemented within an existing selected literature operation and original scoped parent forms; it does not introduce an interpretation episode or additional report-composer call.

For the present case the positiveT252N product identity has source-standard GC-MS support; detailed same-aliquot/time/condition matching andTable3 covariance are unknown. The original double-mutant Fig4label remains conflicting with the text/table identity. Coefficients and reported ratios remain explicit rather than becoming generic relevance weights.
