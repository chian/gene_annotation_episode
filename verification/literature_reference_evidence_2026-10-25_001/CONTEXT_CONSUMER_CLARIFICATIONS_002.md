   known weights. If frame is unreported, pairing is not paired, no contexts
   exist or any weight is missing, all four sample mass cells stay unavailable
   with specific reasons. Per-context measurements, logical events and known
   weights remain usable in saved results. A partial sum cannot silently become
   the biological denominator. Unpaired fractions have no invented sample mass.

   conditions for positive functional compatibility. With multiple necessary
   predicates and unresolved/conflicting joint_support, lower remains0; upper
   retains known necessary-condition exclusions. Established joint-domain
   fixtures retain the declared Frechet arithmetic. No marginal product.
3. Decimal sampling enclosures round intermediate positive division, logarithm
   and square-root steps outward before final float rounding. SD/SEM never
   enters the admissible model domain merely because it is reported.
4. Absent-dataset preparation occurs on the original selected operation only.
   Persist prepared_inputs in the immutable payload. Read/replay compares saved
   inputs, never calls empty_context_inputs or another calculator helper.
