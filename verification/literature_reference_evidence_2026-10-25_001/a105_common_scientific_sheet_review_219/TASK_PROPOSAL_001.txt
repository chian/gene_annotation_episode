Determine what the selected experiments establish about the studied molecule's measured function and the particular comparison or alternative explanation under investigation. Start from supplied material and evidence actually received; a candidate annotation is not required. Identify the organism, constructs, preparations, experimental system, intended change and additional differences. Establish whether compared molecules replace one another or coexist.

Identify each endpoint and its functional resolution. Keep chemically identified product formation, reporter turnover, substrate binding, fitted saturation turnover and low-substrate efficiency distinct. Select measurements with their actual units, denominators, conditions and source locations. Preserve reported uncertainty, censoring and replication meanings. Do not borrow uncertainty or experimental conditions from a neighboring table or assay.

Establish which measurements can be compared and what the comparison evaluates. Examine comparability outside the intended change separately from isolation of the measured effect. Identify applicable background controls, recovery standards, active amounts and nuisance-response measurements. Retain missing or conflicting relationships; matching units, names or publication do not establish compatibility.

For a named alternative explanation, identify the physical model and its applicability. A binding-based occupancy comparison requires the relevant binding parameter, substrate concentration, free-versus-total treatment and relationship between binding and catalytic conditions. Distinguish supported assumptions from explicitly stipulated sensitivity scenarios. Preserve conditional calculations without presenting them as measured corrections or proof of the residual's cause.

Ask deterministic readers and calculators for the requested numerical components. Preserve shared references, measurement reuse and covariance across contrasts. Different endpoints can address complementary weaknesses; they do not automatically provide independent replication. Compare disagreements only between compatible estimands or through an explicit connecting model. Unknown covariance remains unknown.

Return the requested numerical results and necessary scientific qualifications, including useful partial results. Distinguish missing source information from unimplemented calculations. Keep conditional scenarios, supported physical corrections and standardized comparisons separately identified. Target correspondence, final annotation credit and continuation decisions retain their existing owners.
