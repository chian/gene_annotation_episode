The changed live code is:

  separately populates ready final sheets, representable partial sheets, explicitly
  source-supported conditional assignments, local results and conservative

Companion representations remain representations of an actual measurement.
The implementation does not generate Cartesian assignments, average alternative
readings, turn them into independent experiments or choose by favorable effect.
Source-designated reported mean/display reconciliation can use a companion as
well as a primary reading. Known shared-owner cancellation preserves science and
retained readings. Unresolved active effects/profile/index remain conditional.
Mixed origins are retained separately when a truthful whole partial group is
unrepresentable. Absent primary arms retain null source kind and raw origin;
selected missing measurements retain their actual arm, source kind and origin.

The local calculator never calls the legacy or owner whole-sheet populator or
validator on fabricated other roles. It uses the two actual operands and exact
arithmetic primitives. Full supplied covariance lexemes, complete variable
records, symmetry and PSD are validated before every marginal; a bad unused row
cannot be repaired by extracting a favorable submatrix. Pair variance uses both
actual unit factors and binding/owner scope. Unknown owner or covariance can
leave variance unavailable while a meaningful difference survives. Estimated
covariance is allowed and qualified. Zero variance is retained; independently
representable standard uncertainty survives a variance display underflow.
Digitization intervals remain separate from sampling covariance. Reported
SD/SEM/CI/count values are retained in their original meanings.

Actual saved local and population records are validated against their full
closed output schema, then compared with canonical JSON reconstruction. Explicit
regressions reject a boolean in place of a numerical zero. Shared089 likewise
validates the actual complete saved source extraction schema.
