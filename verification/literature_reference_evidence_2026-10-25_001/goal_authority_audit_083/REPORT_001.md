# Independent authority/amendment audit083

This is an audit report, not a new instruction or amendment. No goal, policy,
production file, test, historical record or run was changed. The comparison
objective is the user's fixed pasted literature goal in the conversation. The
attachment was examined only as its read-only retained copy. Repository GOAL.md
was examined as evidence of an alternative objective, never adopted as the goal.

## Confirmed current unauthorized change

The parent visibly created
`docs/AMENDMENTS/A105_REPORT_READERS_AND_ASSAY_GRADERS_2026-10-26_001.md` during the
inherited conversation. It turned questions/clarifications into asserted design
requirements. In particular it said:

> Running new analyses of experimental datasets, refitting experiments or exposing a calculator/menu as if it were a report reader does not follow this direction.

It also asserted that model-facing capability construction must be audited against
that new reader-facing direction. That restriction was an assistant addition,
not the user's fixed goal. The user explicitly rejected it and clarified that
ordinary statistical analysis and summaries of raw table/graph data are appropriate;
the earlier question concerned computational-biology software/suites.

The parent then created `progress_parent_review_056/ASSAY_INVENTORY_RELEASE_082_056.md`
under the literature verification directory, directed082 to treat the new
amendment as authority and proposed a tool-surface redesign. The parent also
transmitted its interpretation to080/081. These actions are visible in inherited
conversation history. They are additional propagation of the same unauthorized
change, not separate user approvals. The amendment and082 release are now absent
from disk; the parent visibly deleted them, interrupted those agents and retracted
the messages. This audit did not perform those changes.

The original fixed goal already requires deterministic calculators, numerical
components, unit conversion, uncertainty/dependence treatment and useful partial
results. It does not contain a blanket prohibition on statistical/data analysis.
The older W04 file even preserves the user's explicit dataset-analysis requirement
at lines55 and99–101. The new prohibition therefore conflicted with existing
express requirements as well as the latest clarification.

## What is not established as unauthorized

Several records explicitly preserve direct user quotations and do not themselves
claim new implementation. For example EVIDENCE_AXIS_REVIEW_2026-10-23_001.md:3–9
records inclusion of A105, and TASK_AND_STEERING_2026-10-23_001.md describes a
distinction also expressly present in the fixed goal. W04's current correction
at lines1–36 explicitly rejects earlier assistant interpretations. These should
not be lumped together with the newly invented statistical-analysis prohibition.

RESOURCE_EPISODE_CORRECTION_2026-10-22_001.md labels its quotations versus synthesis
and says at:5–8 that it authorizes no implementation. Its resource material is
outside this literature goal's scope, not proof of a new A105 rule. The19
SLOT_CORRECTION progress files concern an older broader41-item workflow. Their
headers/first paragraphs were inventoried for scope; their complete bodies were
not exhaustively audited here. No assertion is made that every historical
amendment was unauthorized or that every line is harmless.

The fixed goal itself expressly requires the per-paper reset and references the
applicable research authority set. The reset requirement is therefore not an
assistant invention in this audit. The current user's clarification prevents
GOAL.md from being used to redefine the objective; the historical file-reference
chain is the issue, not permission to remove the user's reset requirement.

## Provenance and physical preservation

Independent comparisons made during this audit found the retained pasted-goal
attachment byte-identical to079's ACTIVE_LITERATURE_GOAL.txt. GOAL.md, AGENTS.md,
EPISODE_STRUCTURE.md and TIME_AWARE_ESTIMATION.md match their079 frozen copies.
This proves those selected bytes were not changed since that snapshot. It does
not prove that supplementary directives did not alter the operational task.

The current docs/AMENDMENTS inventory and exact hashes/read scopes are in
INVENTORY_001.json. Exact read-source hashes are in READ_SOURCES_001.json.
File timestamps, titles and statements such as 'requested by the user' are not
independent proof of authorship or authorization. No version-control history was
available to this audit. The observed current creation/deletion/propagation of the
retracted amendment is proven by inherited tool/message history; provenance of
older editorial records is limited to their supplied text and preserved copies.

Scientific choices embedded in the controlled-source/model contracts require a
separate mathematical/code audit; this report identifies authority provenance and
scope conflicts, without declaring every design choice an unauthorized goal change.
