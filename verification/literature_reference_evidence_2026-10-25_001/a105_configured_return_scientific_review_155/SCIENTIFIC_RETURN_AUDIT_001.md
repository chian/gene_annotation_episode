## Usable numerical components were available

Table 2's NADH oxidation block reports 7 U/mg with ferredoxin plus hydrogenase and 4 U/mg with ferredoxin without added hydrogenase, under its 25 C protocol. Their difference is 3 U/mg. The fractional contrast is (7-4)/7 = 3/7, conditional on that denominator and preparation. This evaluates the response to adding the accessory hydrogenase within this assay; it does not isolate an intrinsic catalytic contribution of Bcd alone or establish an independent probability of function.

Table 2's H2 formation block reports 5 U/mg for the complete system and less than 0.01 U/mg when crotonyl-CoA is omitted, under its distinct 37 C protocol. Using the existing native-rate bound route, the difference is greater than 4.99 U/mg and the fractional loss (5-control)/5 is greater than 0.998. The reported bound is not an exact zero. This evaluates dependence of the measured H2 endpoint on crotonyl-CoA in the coupled preparation. It does not by itself identify or quantify butyryl-CoA formation.

The general same-comparison identity is attributable contrast = reference rate - comparison rate, with fractional contrast = attributable contrast/reference rate. Multiplying the reference rate by that fraction reconstructs the same contrast; this is not an extra independent evidence term. A second transformation of those same rates must not count as a second experiment. These components are local experimental contrasts, not final annotation credit.

NADH oxidation and H2 formation use different endpoint-specific unit definitions, amounts, concentrations, temperatures and protocols. They cannot be divided to establish productive coupling efficiency. A parenthetical NADH rate at 37 C does not remove the other protocol differences. Fig. 2's reported completion-amount slopes, 2.2 NADH/H2 and 1.4 crotonyl-CoA/H2, are separate measurements and must retain their measured values rather than be replaced by an ideal inferred stoichiometry.

## Legitimately unavailable corrected-model operands

The source's per-mg activities cannot silently fill unnormalized net concentration rate q or molar active enzyme concentration E. Bradford total protein with a BSA standard is not an active-site concentration. The article text does not provide the selected sample/blank slopes, molar active amount, recovery and covariance needed to calculate all corrected-model cells. Null corrected activity remains appropriate when those operands are absent. A MALDI mass change supports a complementary product assignment; it supplies neither quantitative formation rate nor calibration error.

The original return's lack of selected operands is not proof of omission from the paper. The adapter repeats the first label for q-g and u/v. This is a demonstrated semantic defect in prospective output, separate from the scientific selection failure. Historical values and labels remain historical facts.

## Extraction relationships needing repair

Several literal labels use an entire caption or reaction-mixture passage for distinct roles such as organism, molecule, measured entity and asserted input/output. Literal custody validation does not establish those meanings. A long protocol can be a valid contextual witness, but its presence cannot substitute for identifying each role. Exact source spans should identify C. kluyveri separately from C. pasteurianum accessory components, NADH loss separately from crotonyl-CoA input, and H2 measurement separately from MALDI product identification.

The five records are not demonstrated to be five distinct fully specified assays. Some concern overlapping NADH protocols, and one concerns a product-identification observation. No assay coverage or completion count follows from their number.

The existing scientific instructions correctly prohibit putting per-mg values into q and missing controls into zero or one. They do not establish that the parent selected an appropriate return projection for the available native measurements.
