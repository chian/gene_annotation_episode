# Coupled-assay interpretation limitation for the existing A105 case

This adds a qualification to SCIENTIFIC_RETURN_AUDIT_001.md from the same
already-read PMC2223550 case. It is not a new goal, adopted scoring policy,
new grader or claim that a proposed extraction field is implemented.

The assayed entity is the purified C. kluyveri Bcd/Etf complex. Added ferredoxin
and hydrogenase from C. pasteurianum coexist with it. The measured H2 endpoint
depends on the hydrogenase reaction used in this coupled system. Removing that
enzyme can eliminate the measured gas without establishing loss of an intrinsic
Bcd/Etf reaction or a physiological requirement for that partner in C. kluyveri.
Native partner presence and coexpression remain separate later work.

The printed 7-versus-4 U/mg NADH comparison evaluates the response to added
hydrogenase under that protocol. The printed 5-versus-<0.01 U/mg H2 comparison
evaluates dependence on crotonyl-CoA under its distinct protocol. Their
differences/fractions retain those experimental meanings. Neither becomes a
universal reliability multiplier, an independent function probability or a
cross-endpoint productive coupling efficiency.

The interpretation task must distinguish the measured species, asserted
substrate/product, primary assayed preparation and the roles of added reaction
participants, carriers and reporter/coupling enzymes. Unknown roles remain
unknown. Such relationship fields support interpretation of numerical cells;
they are not coded numerical categories. A required reporter must not silently
become a required physiological partner.

Product identification can complement a reporter endpoint without supplying
the reporter's missing quantitative attribution or calibration. Repeated
representations and several transformations of the same rates remain dependent
evidence. A scoped comparison can retain a useful native rate or control bound
while causal isolation, biological interpretation or uncertainty remains
unresolved. Those distinctions still require scientifically reviewed populated
examples before the A105 template can be called adequate.
