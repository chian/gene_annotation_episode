Reactions were conducted in 100 mM MOPS, 70 mM NaCl and 20 mM KCl pH 7.2 in the presence of 400 µM Ascorbat and 4 µM Fe(NH4)2SO4. KM for glutarate and α-ketoglutarate was determined in the presence of 1 mM α-ketoglutarate and 5 mM glutarate, respectively. Five millimolar substrate was used for the C2–C7 analogues of glutarate. 180 nM CsiD were used for the reactions. Reactions were conducted at 30 °C in the sealed reaction chamber with magnetic stirrer. v0 was determined and plotted against the concentration. The plot was fitted with Michaelis-Menten equation with in GraphPad Prism5 (HYPNOS).
