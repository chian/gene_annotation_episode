## A canceled numeric conflict does not become a scientific identity gate

003 incorrectly required disjoint canceled numerical readings, or a canceled
arm's status=conflicting, to block e even when actual owner/context/applicability
remained known. Remove that requirement. It confuses an unused reading with the
scientific identity required for the owner expression.

For genuinely established owner B and actual applicable shared-reference
invariance, e=A-B-C+B=A-C. This remains independent of B's scalar or interval
even if B is printed as4 in one representation and5 in another, has a disjoint
graphical domain, or its numeric status is conflicting. Preserve
canceled_source_reading_conflict/canceled_reading_domain_conflict and the exact
source alternatives as QUALIFICATIONS. Do not select or average B and do not
require a nonempty intersection of B readings in order to cancel its coefficient.
Reading conflict roles are retained in contrast_dependencies; canceled numeric
conflicts do not enter scientific_reasons or active_reading_reasons.

If the episode concludes that a discrepancy challenges physical-owner identity,
actual control applicability, endpoint/normalization or other scientific
correspondence, it must mark that SCIENTIFIC meaning unknown/conflicting in its
source interpretation. That does block the claimed cancellation/comparison.
The pure calculator may not infer this scientific downgrade automatically from
an unused scalar disagreement. Known incompatible scientific contexts or actual
source-observation/physical-owner binding contradictions remain failures.
Active, noncanceling conflicts still block or require an explicit reading model.

Contrasting shape fixtures004 include disjoint canceled graph/text readings,
canceled numeric status=conflicting, and the SAME case with actual conflicting
physical-owner meaning. The former two retain e=2 conditional on the known
science; the latter cannot cancel and leaves the cross component unavailable.

with unit=null does not require a guessed conversion. Explicit incompatible

qualification, not a new automatic acceptance gate. Existing explicit endpoint,
normalization, subject/construct, condition/control applicability and intended
factor requirements retain their scientific meanings. No numerical requirement
on a canceled term may be silently added to those scientific requirements.

## Exact dependencies, covariance and model eligibility

contrast_dependencies.groups partitions ALL four role names exactly once in
primitive order. Present known same-owner roles share a group; absent/unknown
roles each have an unresolved singleton with null owner. known_owner is true
iff its owner is established, and coefficient is the exact sum of original
[1,-1,-1,1] role coefficients. canceled_roles contains precisely roles of known
groups with coefficient0. active_roles is the ordered complement. Reading
conflict roles may include canceled roles; that alone does not block effect.

All declared canceled reading qualifiers must follow actual retained roles.
required_covariance_owner_ids is the ordered unique known active owner roster;
an unknown active owner prevents source-owned covariance inference. The provided
owner roster is the normalized original RoleCovariance or unique OwnerCovariance
roster. covariance_state is scientific_blocked when applicable science prevents
the contrast, unavailable when C is absent, incomplete_active_coverage when
provided C omits a required active owner, otherwise available for its valid
explicit scope. No unprovided B entries are zeroed or inferred independent.

Validate an ENTIRE supplied matrix before active projection. A provided invalid
B variance or other invalid full matrix remains invalid; do not cherry-pick A/C.
A legitimately supplied unique-owner C over[A,C] is explicitly smaller evidence,
not an automatic repair of bad full C. Both unit factors and actual owners remain
required. Full original source-N validation remains the reader's responsibility.

V/SE require valid active reading/statistic support as well as explicit source C.
Preserve006's unresolved_censoring_model restriction for an ACTIVE reported bound
whose point is unknown. Canceled bound/graph/conflicting readings do not impose
an active censoring/reading-model restriction. Positive-V precision and the
explicit known-versus-estimated status remain unchanged. Optional normal S/B
evaluates source model support, known positive V and exact uncensored non-graph
non-approximate ACTIVE readings only. Canceled source reading problems remain
visible as qualifications; they do not fabricate uncertainty for A-C.

The derived numerical origin/approximation/bound-kind refer to ACTIVE numeric
dependencies; canceled source/graph qualifications are retained separately.
Raw/local cells preserve their own original reading values, bounds and unknowns.
Do not obtain e by subtracting those representation-specific local summaries.
