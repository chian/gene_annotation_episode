conflict, uncertainty and model qualifications. They must not expose unrequested

or a durable digest alone is not possession of all child scientific cells.

observation. No private episode loop, new episode/axis type, fixed child order,
controller intervention, A101 transfer or W05 aggregation is proposed.

Active (noncanceling) distinct source representations still require the actual
source reading/reconciliation model identified in001. The first pure tranche
retains that limitation explicitly, not as the permanent intended final state.
Actual primary cases and configured-model selected literature execution remain
required. Synthetic expected sheets and declaration checks establish none of
that scientific or native coverage.
