| Boundary | Constructed fixture and expected result | Responsibility established |
|---|---|---|

| Inclusive channel limits | In a row, place pixels exactly equal to rgb_min and rgb_max, plus pixels just outside each individual channel. Choose a threshold with an analytically known count/run. | Every channel is tested inclusively; no luminance, averaging or color distance. |

| No/multiple runs | Empty threshold match→no_run and[]; two separated retained runs→multiple_runs and both in order. No favorable run is chosen. | Missing pixels never become zero; ambiguity is preserved. |
| Counter does not interpret an edge | A run touching ROI.top or ROI.bottom still appears unchanged. CountSelection rejects terminal_edge, tolerance, calibration, value or unit. | Counter is pure acquisition of row ranges. |
