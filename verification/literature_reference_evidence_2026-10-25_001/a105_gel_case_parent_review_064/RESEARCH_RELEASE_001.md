Establish actual organism/genotype/construct, comparator, expression arrangement
(replacement/coexistence/other), lane/band identities, biological question,
endpoint and normalization, loading/background/exposure/calibration/saturation
controls, quantitative values and uncertainty/repetition basis. Distinguish
paper-reported values, graph-derived values, proposed arithmetic and synthetic
illustrations. Do not estimate an intensity from unauthenticated/noisy pixels
without a declared justified measurement model.

Explain exactly what each gel result can and cannot establish for A105 evidence
on the SOURCE molecule, or why it belongs only to perturbation/restoration or
another later literature responsibility. Protein abundance, purity and direct
activity are different measured quantities. Distinguish comparability outside
the intended change from isolation of the measured effect. Different formats
can contribute to the same experimental sheet; a new plot/gel format is not a
new axis or automatically a new scientific grader.

Develop paired source fields and actual numerical component proposals together.
For every proposed number define actual inputs/locations, units/denominators,
equation, purpose and explicit role in sums/multipliers with existing components.
Preserve unknowns and alternative explanations. If there is no defensible
numerical conversion, identify precisely the missing measurement/relationship
and which complementary experiment would supply it. No default0/1, category
ladder, intuitive reliability, citation/reputation score, inferred independence,
gel-intensity-to-activity equivalence, target homology or W05 credit.

Deliver a concise source-grounded experimental account, paired extraction/cell
proposal with worked actual or clearly marked synthetic arithmetic, necessary
aspect-specific interpretation question(s) with finite allowed meanings and
unknown/conflicting conditions, and precise later reader/calculator needs.
Do not code or adopt a scoring policy. Root must read the primary evidence,
perform its own reset and print concrete component/instruction examples before
any new gel implementation or broad replication.
