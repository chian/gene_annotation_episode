# Proposed recurring TASK insert

Draft text for root to print and obtain running feedback before implementation.
This is aspect-specific investigation guidance, not the numerical sheet or a
mandatory series of actions. The enclosing episode retains its full parent
assignment, scope, constraints and requested result on every iteration.

```text
Determine what the reported signal comparison establishes about the studied
molecule and the experiment you were assigned. Identify the actual sample,
recipient genotype, tested construct and reference construct before interpreting
a ratio. Establish whether the proteins replace one another or coexist. A WT
label can refer to tagged plasmid complementation rather than an unmodified
organism or endogenous protein. Keep intended experimental changes separate
from expression, loading or other differences that could explain the result.

Identify the measured signal and its relation to the claimed function. A band
or antibody signal can qualify expression comparability without measuring active
protein amount, catalysis or the functional endpoint. Growth recovery and
survival are different endpoints. Do not borrow the mutant panel, repetition
count, loading control, exposure or calibration from another experiment.

When a printed figure value is useful, examine the actual selected native image
view available to your own episode. Identify its frame, numerator and reference
regions and the source's normalization. Nominate the complete printed literal,
including its percent sign or inequality, and the applicable source witnesses.
Choose a printed normalized ratio, percentage or an explicit pair of printed
signals according to what the figure reports. Raw band darkness is not a
printed number; this reader does not perform densitometry, OCR or calibration.

Keep your reading exact, ambiguous, unreadable or conflicting according to the
actual evidence. Preserve alternative readings and visible fragments/regions.
For a pair, nominate joint numerator/reference alternatives and establish a
common signal scale; different exposures do not automatically share one. Do
not invent a value, positive reference, calibration or detection limit to finish
the sheet. A printed0 is a reported signal value, not proof of zero molecules;
ND and n.d. do not by themselves define a numerical limit or its meaning.

Check whether a prose statement and figure concern this same comparison and
physical measurement. Preserve the actual reference, conditions, endpoint and
uncertainty. Equal numbers, repeated figures or a common paper do not establish
independence. Retain conflicting source representations; do not select the
favorable one, average them or silently discard an unreadable alternative.
If source association or physical identity is unknown, preserve that unknown.

You identify experimental meanings, source locations and literal candidates.
Deterministic readers validate custody and parse numbers; separate calculators
perform division and transforms. Ratio, difference and log ratio are alternative
descriptions of one comparison, not independent quality scores. An amount or
functional-response correction requires its own supported calibration/model
and cannot be inferred from a band ratio. State the applicable limitation rather
than claiming the paper lacked an input merely because this reader does not
implement it.

Return the parent's requested numerical components and necessary experimental
qualifications through its declared typed form. Unknown, censored, contradictory
and valid partial results remain useful information. Do not create a target
transfer weight, annotation probability, extra scientific observation, new
episode type, fixed reading itinerary or report-composition call. The episode's
ordinary choices and configured continuation controller remain in charge.
```

No separate model call is introduced to ask these questions. They are parts of
the existing child decision that chooses source work and nominates the operation.
Text quotes establish retained words; image witnesses establish viewed pixels
and geometry. Neither mechanically proves the interpretation.
