## Aspect1: actual mean versus graphical display and source authority

### TASK — constructed concrete A105 assignment

Determine what the assigned experiment establishes about the studied molecule's
molecular activity, starting from the supplied experimental material. Read the
provided selected experimental passages, tables and figure region, and use
permitted source reading or narrower literature investigations when they can
resolve an identified uncertainty. A candidate annotation or publicly searchable
name is not required. Possible molecular functions can emerge from the evidence.
Keep the assayed reference molecule separate from the supplied annotation target;
you are evaluating experimental reference evidence, not sequence/structure
correspondence or final annotation credit.

Establish the actual organism, genotype, molecule, construct, preparation and
experimental system. Determine whether other molecules coexist or replace one
another. Identify the intended changed factor, conditions, actual protein-present
arms and applicable protein-free/empty-vector controls. Distinguish background
subtraction, experimental comparability outside the intended change and isolation
of the effect. A preparation signal alone does not establish isolated action of
the studied protein or unique chemical identity of a reaction product.

Resolve the specific source question: does the printed A response10 nmol/min
and the graphical A response10.05 with conditional reading bounds9.95 to10.15
nmol/min describe the SAME measured mean? The supplied readings and bounds are
retained results; do not recalculate them in prose. Inspect surrounding methods,
legends, table headers, normalization definitions, calibration and any correction
or revision. Establish physical observation, endpoint, normalization,
subject/construct, condition, preparation and arrangement separately. Equal values,
shared paper/image identity, or a printed point lying inside a graph domain do
not establish those scientific relationships.

Determine whether the source explicitly designates a reported quantitative mean
and describes the graph as a display of that SAME mean, whether the two represent
alternative estimates/calibrations, or whether their relation/authority is unknown
or conflicting. Source authority concerns the source's explicit relationship
between these values; it is not a reputation ranking, favored publication type,
vote of matching values or your overall impression. Primary means the original
nomination and need not be the source-designated reading. Return the exact source
statements supporting any asserted authority and any normalization/calibration/
revision/model-scope relationship. Keep all selected alternatives and their
original source kind, reported/graph/synthetic origin, units, limits, approximation,
reported SD/SEM/CI/confidence/count/repetition/fit meanings and source locations.

Keep graphical reading bounds separate from sampling uncertainty. A graph domain
is conditional on its selected region, terminal edge, calibration and axis scale;
it is not a sampling confidence interval, independent replicate, covariance or
observation model. Do not choose a favorable value, average representations,
invent a midpoint or precision, intersect graphs into a new measured value, or
convert SD/count into independence. Determine whether original source-C and
source-model statements concern the SAME source means and experimental scope;
do not nominate a different scope merely because a covariance submatrix is valid.

Use the declared closed source nominations to report the aspect-specific meanings.
For each reading group return relationship as same_reported_mean_and_display,
exact_duplicate, alternative_estimates, unknown or conflicting. Authority is
source_explicit with the exact existing designated reading, not_established, or
conflicting. Each normalization/calibration/revision/model-scope relation is same,
explicit_compatible, not_applicable, unknown or conflicting and needs the actual
relevant source witnesses. Exact duplication additionally requires established
identity/context and canonical quantities; numerical calculators check equivalence
and domains. Unknown authority is appropriate when the source supplies no
relationship, selected material omits the needed context, or scientific identity
cannot be established. Conflicting requires distinct incompatible source facts;
different numerical representations alone need not be a biological contradiction.

Choose useful further reading, a repair of a source nomination, a permitted
narrower child or a typed return based on actual outcomes. These are alternatives,
not an itinerary or fixed number of readings. Deterministic readers authenticate

## Aspect2: simultaneous reading assignments and coupled source scope

### TASK — constructed concrete A105 assignment

Determine which retained readings can be used TOGETHER to interpret the studied
molecule's response under the two assigned conditions. Start from the supplied
source material and the retained experimental readings. The job is to establish
experimental reference evidence for possible molecular activity; no candidate
annotation or searchable protein name is required. Preserve the actual assayed
molecule and its organism/genotype/construct/preparation/system separately from
any supplied annotation target. Do not calculate target correspondence, final
annotation credit or an overall experimental-quality category.

Establish each condition's protein-present arm, its actual applicable protein-free
or empty-vector control, endpoint, normalization, units, intended changed factor
and nonintended differences. Determine physical control identity and stated
invariance independently of numerical agreement. The same physical B may appear
as printed4 and graphical4.05[3.95,4.15] nmol/min; those are two representations,
not two experiments. Missing or disagreeing canceled control numbers do not alone
make its scientific applicability conflicting. Unknown or conflicting OWNER or
application remains scientifically material even when algebra could cancel B.

Resolve this specific question: source bundle P uses printed A10 and original
C8 nmol/min; bundle Q uses graphical A10.05[9.95,10.15] and C8.1 nmol/min. Which
complete four-role assignments does the source actually permit? Examine the
legend, data links, calibration/normalization definitions, reference reuse and
source revisions. Values can be coupled by a common calibration, normalization
factor, source revision or observation-model scope. Finding an A alternative and
a C alternative separately does not permit combining them arbitrarily. Return
only the explicitly nominated full assignments whose simultaneous applicability
is established; do not generate a Cartesian product, discard an active reading,
choose the largest effect or multiply conditional likelihoods.

For each nominated assignment, identify the actual source statements establishing
its common endpoint and normalization, calibration compatibility, source revision
and covariance/model scope. Retain the exact original complete covariance source
nomination, original variable roster and owner bindings for that assignment;
changing normalization or calibration cannot reuse covariance units or model
scope by assumption. Known source support for an original full joint-means model
can support its explicit marginal; reported SD and a repetition count do not supply
that support. An invalid supplied full covariance remains invalid even if a
selected A/C submatrix looks usable. Preserve measured scientific quantities and
limitations when model or variance support is absent.

Return each joint assignment's admissibility as compatible, unknown or conflicting
with exact relevant source witnesses. Compatible means simultaneous scientific
use under its actual source scopes; it does NOT mean independent observations,
preferred readings or resolved final reconciliation. Unknown means the source
material does not establish the connection, the relevant scope is absent or a
required relationship remains unresolved. Conflicting means source facts show
the nominated simultaneous use is incompatible; retain both conflicting facts.
For normalization/calibration/revision/covariance/model relations use same,
explicit_compatible, not_applicable, unknown or conflicting with their own
witnesses. Not_applicable is only for a genuinely absent model/C or irrelevant
revision/calibration relationship, not a replacement for unknown information.

Evaluate an independently assigned two-arm local comparison using ONLY its actual
two readings, condition target/application and pair-specific scientific scope.
A supported C/B local comparison need not wait for an unrelated A group. Its
physical dependence can remain unknown, leaving variance unknown while the
background-controlled magnitude remains available. Do not infer four-role
compatibility from a pair. Preserve every original raw reading, graph domain,
source origin and reported uncertainty/repetition meaning. Distinguish numerical
availability from scientific applicability; a parsed number is not automatically
a suitable functional measurement.

Use actual outcomes to choose further permitted reading, source-nomination repair,
