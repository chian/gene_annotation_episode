## Existing numerical combination

The within-assay section explicitly computes additional supported contaminant
subtraction q-g, nuisance response u/v, amount normalization q/E, calibration
and amount q/(kE), and full correction (q-g)v/(kEu). Its source relationships,
correction stages and units determine which calculation applies. Already
applied corrections use documented algebraic identities, not newly inferred
measurements. Applicable joint covariance propagates through J Sigma J^T;
smaller supported operand blocks preserve partial uncertainty results. Neither
an enzyme spike nor an endpoint fraction is automatically reporter recovery.

The common-assertion branch explicitly compares identified product,
reporter turnover, productive use, saturation turnover and low-substrate
efficiency. A declared one-site occupancy model can supply an alternative
explanation: compare product ratio with occupancy ratio, or divide them to
obtain a conditional residual. Its log residual has a signed full-covariance
propagation and standardized form when the required model/inputs are available.
These are numerical components bearing on particular assertions; native rates,
correction fractions and model residuals do not become a universal quality
average. Different endpoints may address complementary weaknesses.

## What remains unconnected

The inspected common received-component selector currently admits product
coupling, original kinetic parameters and calibration-recovery rows. It does
not admit new summary-precision rows or finite-endpoint fraction rows. The
independent probe accepts the existing kinetic selector and rejects the actual
endpoint namespace. This is a limit of this particular assembly interface,
not proof that all other library functions lack useful comparison arithmetic.

Connecting new numerical sections requires source-bound scientific meanings,
not adding namespaces indiscriminately. In particular:

- A mean variance estimated from a sample SD must retain its sampling unit,
  applicable count, model conditions and estimated status. It cannot supply
  known population variance or zero shared calibration covariance.
- Source and explicitly stipulated scenario results remain separate. Their
  repeated SD/n/variance/SE/interval representations share one contribution.
- A finite-trace fraction requires its actual correction role and stage. A
  fraction already incorporated in a reported outcome must not be applied a
  second time. Image-reading uncertainty is distinct from physical variation.
- The relevance of each quantity to the actual assertion, molecule, endpoint,
  conditions and denominator remains explicit. Mere matching units or a common
  paper does not establish comparability, causal isolation or independence.

The current common sheet explicitly declares general compilation and
same-estimand contradiction compilation unfinished. Its named occupancy
alternative is useful but does not implement a general matched-assay
consistency assessment. Investigating a source-grounded extension is required;
an arbitrary variance weight, assay count or categorical reliability grade
would not close this gap.
