1. **Partner public subject can contradict the actual measured arm subject.** Reproduced with constructed source text and the real native reader/repository. Population succeeds, all numerical components return, and the typed `subject_ref` labels `PartnerP`. The measured subject and published subject therefore disagree. 2. **A108 requirement and route meanings become anonymous.** The retained inputs have requirement keys, units and domains, but no per-requirement source-backed biological/chemical meaning. Public `_metadata` removes requirement keys and returns only unit/domains; routes refer to integer indexes. Two predicates with the same unit cannot be distinguished by a scientific parent. A global function/biological-context binding does not identify each predicate. Units or interval values cannot supply the missing meanings.
3. **A110 alternatives become anonymous numerical predictions.** The retained ratio input records `{key,prediction}` alternatives; public `_metadata` returns only `alternative_predictions:[number...]`. There is no typed source-backed binding from each number to the corresponding competing reaction or functional hypothesis. A signed comparison cannot tell the parent which alternative was contrasted.

Direct report measurement readers for A108 and A110 are **separate missing functions**: current parsers consume a versioned, already prepared retained JSON extraction. Literal source witnesses support interpretation, but do not parse numerical domains, context measurements, ratios or budgets from the paper itself. This is not fixed by preserving names in the typed return.

## Exact implemented numerical scope

**A108:** `sample_weight`, `supported_weight`, `unresolved_weight`, `incompatible_weight`; `coverage_lower`, `coverage_upper`, `coverage_point`; `capability_lower`, `capability_upper`, `capability_point`; `selected_lower`, `selected_upper`, `selected_point`; `sampling_lower`, `sampling_upper`.

Requirements use explicit supported/incompatible interval unions and units. Observations preserve relation/spread/repetition information rather than treating SD or technical repetition as biological domain coverage. Complete routes require joint support; genuine alternative routes combine by logical OR. Coverage is supported/possible weight divided by the stated sample-frame weight. Unpaired marginals use Frechet bounds, not an independence product. Capability is existential and differs from sampled-context coverage; unexamined remainder remains possible. Optional Hoeffding outer intervals require the declared paired, defined-scope/frame, independent equal-weight, known distinct cluster and alpha conditions. This is a conditional numerical compatibility framework, not an intuitive overall plausibility grade.

**A110:** `ratio_mean`; `claim_nominal_squared_error`; `claim_mean_squared_error`; `reaction_comparison` and its lower/upper; `resource_capacity` and its lower/upper; `resource_coverage` and its lower/upper; `resource_comparison` and its lower/upper.

Nominal error is `(mean-claim)^2`; full mean squared error includes the independently observed variance contribution when available. Relative reaction comparison uses `(alternative_error-claim_error)/(alternative_error+claim_error)` against the specified alternatives, with explicit zero/zero behavior. Capacity is `min(U_j/a_j)` under the fixed-consumption model; coverage is `min(1,capacity/demand)` and resource comparison is `2*coverage-1`. Product-pair, formed-per-consumed, formed-per-supplied and signal ratios remain different. Budget basis, units, roster completeness and logical ranges are explicit. The implementation does not itself establish product identity, consumption, physiological availability or pathway flux merely from a positive number.

**Partners:** `reference_rate`, `omitted_rate`, `addback_rate`, `partner_loss=W-O`, `partner_loss_fraction=(W-O)/W`, `addback_gain=A-O`, `addback_fraction=(A-O)/(W-O)`, `restored_reference_fraction=(A-O)/W`.

Contrasts require compatible measured subjects/partners/endpoints/normalizations and explicit nonpartner conditions, while preserving partial native rates when contrasts are invalid. Shared measurements remain shared; reused reference/addback measurements do not fabricate restoration. Upper-bound domains are propagated through common primitive variables rather than pretending repeated denominator occurrences are independent. Negative effects and overshoot are preserved. No universal partner necessity, target correspondence, genomic presence or coexpression score is inferred.

## Remaining limits distinguished from defects

Partner native source numbers currently support exact points and `<`/`<=` upper bounds, not arbitrary interval/lower-bound/approximate report grammar. Retained native evidence distinguishes `<` from `<=`, but the numerical/public typed arm domain currently collapses both to the same closed outer domain. This is conservative enclosure, yet loses literal comparator meaning at the typed boundary. Preserve the reported relation separately if this route is extended.

Partner spread input retains kind and magnitude but has no independent sample n, count/replicate basis or covariance sufficient for new inferential uncertainty calculations; public `statistical_uncertainty` is explicitly `not_evaluated`. Missing n in a source is missing data; no supported input for actually available n/design is missing implementation. Avoid adding a generic CI calculator without the requisite experiment-specific design. Basic statistical arithmetic is within the goal; computational biology suites are exceptional and none are called by these three calculator paths.

A108 positive native organism/sample measurements and A110 named alternative/resource primary contrasts are not supplied by the engineering integration cases inspected. These are limits on demonstrated scientific coverage, separate from the definite typing/parser defects. The fixed goal does not demand implementing every imaginable experiment or historical proposal feature. Additional targeted experiments should follow actual unresolved annotation questions, after the required full paper reset.
