## What can affect an annotation

This experiment can substantiate a particular preparation-level oxygen-rate
response. Its magnitude and reported spread are usable quantitative components.
To support an asserted catalytic conversion, the reader must separately find
evidence identifying the product and attributing the productive reaction to
the studied molecule. A product assay elsewhere can address identity without
quantifying simultaneous electrode coupling. The scientific relationship must
be retained rather than converted into a blanket confidence multiplier.

No alignment, structural similarity or target-transfer number enters this
source evaluation. A future consumer can connect this characterized molecule
to a target using the separate correspondence evidence.
