## Numerical contribution

The compiled-activity route reads exact possessed corrected-activity rows, retaining the already-applied corrections and their ownership. It calculates the preferred/alternative activity ratio and its logarithm. Optional supported joint 2-by-2 covariance supplies first-order variance and standard uncertainty. It does not silently assume independence, a confidence distribution, a new normalization or a conversion from charge flow to ion flux.

Separate reported-ratio and reported-threshold routes retain their original meaning, scope, approximation, censoring and derivation. Reported, estimator, nominal and physical domains are distinct. A nominal threshold ratio is not a rigorous physical bound. Explicit zero and signed estimates remain available: zero numerator with positive denominator has ratio zero and undefined logarithm; zero denominator has no finite ratio; signed estimator arithmetic is not promoted to a nonnegative physical ion rate. A bound reaching zero retains both its qualified positive-denominator branch and the possible undefined zero case.

The targeted local number reader preserves original source bytes, whole grouped tokens and JATS integer powers. Same-field following-spread and unit-continuation guards reject clipped units, percentages presented as fold ratios, physical dimensions presented as dimensionless ratios and convenient unrelated unit headers. It retains native spread as unimplemented uncertainty reading rather than manufacturing standard deviation or covariance. Shared lexical readers were not modified.

Known whole-activity aliases are checked from complete source and dependency ownership, formula and correction stages; common subsets or a shared normalizer do not establish whole-activity identity. Actual mock/synthetic operand origins are preserved and cannot silently become primary-source discrimination. Unknown derivation and quantity kind remain unknown rather than becoming author inference or ion flux. Source adjustment claims must link to actual already-applied compiled controls.
