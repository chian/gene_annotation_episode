Numerical definitions and combinations

For compatible point source activities, R=a_preferred/a_alternative and L=ln(R). Each a is already the existing (q−g)v/(kEu) result, so R=[(qP−gP)/(qA−gA)](kA/kP)(EA/EP)(vP/uP)/(vA/uA). This explains how the specificity component combines measured signal, attribution, calibration, amount and applicable nuisance effects. Correction identities come only from the established stages. An exact common factor cancels algebraically; it is not an extra independent measurement. This wrapper does not claim to implement new primitive-level uncertainty cancellation from hidden data.

Synthetic supported example: aP=0.9/min, aA=0.15/min gives R=6 and L=1.791759469228055. If no joint covariance is supplied, R/L remain usable while their variance/SE remain unknown. A separate synthetic supplied covariance [[0.0081,0.000675],[0.000675,0.000225]] in per_min_squared gives Var(R)=0.36, SE(R)=0.6, Var(L)=0.01 and SE(L)=0.1. These are conditional first-order uncertainties, not confidence levels, probabilities or a second experiment.

For A=aP and B=aA, J_R=(1/B,−A/B²) and J_L=(1/A,−1/B). Use J C Jᵀ with the full ordered2×2 covariance. The original covariance source must declare actual selected activity keys, units, complete dependency keys, variance status, origin and covariance-of-estimator basis. Require finite symmetric PSD data. The source’s own complete matrix is validated before use. Unknown covariance never means diagonal covariance; individual SE/SD values do not supply the missing joint model. Point A/B must have positive supported activities for this transport interpretation; a signed negative estimator or B=0 remains native evidence without clipping or infinite ratio. Reusing the identical selected activity for two purported ions is not discrimination; reject it as reused_activity_not_ion_discrimination.

The internal sheet contains preferred_activity, alternative_activity, activity_ratio, log_activity_ratio, their separate ratio/log variance/SE cells, reported_selectivity and nominal_threshold_ratio. Route-inapplicable cells remain explicitly unavailable. These are different descriptions/contributions, not independent proofs. Component names must agree with their map key; units/formulas/meanings and finite reasons are code-owned, not model prose.

Four domain fields have constrained, different meanings:

- reported_domain: the domain the source assigns to its reported estimate/limit. It is not a sampling confidence interval.
- estimator_domain: a selected or calculated estimator’s numerical domain, including a point value. A point estimator does not establish an exact physical parameter.
- nominal_domain: the mathematical domain obtained by treating reported threshold numbers literally. Its conditional arithmetic must remain visible even when it cannot substantiate the selected construct’s assertion.
- physical_domain: a conditional physical parameter domain only when source-supported quantity/basis, nonnegative parameter meaning, compatible subject/arrangement and the necessary bound/approximation derivation are established. An unquantified approximation cannot be promoted to a rigorous physical bound.

Domains store finite nullable endpoints, explicit unbounded sides, exact rational endpoints and nullable inclusivity. “Lower limit” with unstated strictness is retained as lower_limit with unknown inclusivity; it is not silently ge or gt. Nonnegative physical parameters and signed measured estimators are separate interpretations. A nonnegative rate bound does not justify clipping a measured negative estimate. For a nonnegative denominator interval touching0, ratio bounds must state the B>0 condition and retain the zero-denominator case; never substitute an epsilon or output numerical infinity. Approximate inputs may support nominal threshold arithmetic while the physical domain stays unavailable.
