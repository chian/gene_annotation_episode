The paired extraction binds source numbers, endpoint-specific unit definitions,
actual molecular subject, partner state, normalization and physical measurement
identities. It selects ten reported nonpartner protocol factors for each
comparison. Coverage here means the selected reported protocol, conditional on
those supplied facts; it is not a claim that preparation identity, purity and
all possible confounds are known. The intended component omission is kept out
of nonpartner condition matching.

The numerical components are W, O, W-O and (W-O)/W. Their explicit arithmetic
measures condition-specific endpoint dependence. W multiplied by the fractional
loss reconstructs W-O and is not another independent contribution. The two
contrasts within each endpoint reuse the same reference measurement. They
cannot be summed as independent evidence. No separately observed addback is
selected; all four restoration/addback components remain unknown.

A unit is endpoint-specific: one micromole NADH oxidized or H2 formed per minute,
respectively. The supported reader's canonical pmol/min/mg conversion multiplies
these U/mg numbers by10^6 after binding the actual source definition. Equal
rate dimensions do not make the biological endpoints interchangeable. The NADH
and H2 protocols differ in temperature, concentrations, enzyme amount and volume;
there is no valid productive-coupling ratio from these two Table2 rates.

Hydrogenase participates in the H2 reporting system. Dependence of that readout
on hydrogenase does not alone establish a physiological partner requirement.
The omission contrasts do not identify all individual protein contributions,
exclude contaminating activity or by themselves prove a mechanism. Product
assignment and completion stoichiometry are complementary questions. They must
not be fabricated from an omission fraction.

The paper reports preparation-dependent rates, lags and imperfect proportionality
to enzyme added. This increment supplies no SD, sample count, covariance,
between-preparation precision or calibrated reliability probability. Those
quantities remain unknown. Gel appearance is not a quantitative contaminating
activity correction. These missing facts do not justify categorical grades.
