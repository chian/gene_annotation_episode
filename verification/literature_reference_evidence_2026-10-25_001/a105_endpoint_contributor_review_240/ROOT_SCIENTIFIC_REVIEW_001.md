The experiment and the scope of its assertion
----------------------------------------------

The native MvhADG/HdrABC complex comes from M.marburgensis DSM2133. Six subunits
coexist; no replacement genotype or engineered expression construct is
established. The alternative ferredoxin partner comes from C.pasteurianum.
The MTZ assay uses an artificial electron acceptor rather than a chemically
identified product of one isolated subunit. These experiments bear on the
complex's coupled redox activity; the suggested individual HdrA bifurcation
site remains an inference rather than an isolated molecular measurement.

Fig4A uses approximately0.1unit complex,90nmolMTZ and72nmolCoM-S-S-CoB in0.75mL,
1.6M potassium phosphate,pH7,60°C andH2. The Methods specify1.2barH2 and
anaerobic sealed UV cuvettes. Reduction is followed at320nm. Although the source
gives an extinction coefficient, it does not state this trace's path length;
no newly measured absolute amount is inferred from the ordinate.

Contributors coexist: complex-mediated reactions, stock thiols/DTT/FAD,
product-dependent disulfide regeneration, and potential alternative MTZ
reduction. The intentionally varied disulfide amount does not establish
selective removal of these contributions. The authors correct a continuing
late component by extrapolation. This supplies a quantitative correction
model; it is not a separately measured no-enzyme control or initial rate g.
