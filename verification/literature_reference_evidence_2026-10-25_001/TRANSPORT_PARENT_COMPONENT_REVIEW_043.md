## How the experimental components compile

For an actual matched whole-cell experiment, fit an intercept and initial slope
per identified preparation over a justified window. Form each matched difference
d_i=vB_i−vEV_i. Its uncertainty uses the actual pairing/covariance; an independent
paired-sample variance requires actual independent pairs, not time points or a
caption count alone. Total-cell-protein normalization never supplies BtsT amount.

For the four-arm controlled response, the explicit sum is e=l*x. Its full
covariance propagates as lClᵀ. No missing arm is zero and no off-diagonal entry
is silently independence. The original measured endpoint, normalization and
protocol scope remain attached to the result. Native signed noisy measurements
survive; a log requires an applicable positive endpoint, not a pseudocount.

This is a contrast of experimental responses, not proof of complete causal
isolation. The intended gradient is part of the signal. Buffer/ionic cochanges
outside that intended variable remain qualifications. A separately measured,
applicable response mapping could correct its effect once; neither the gradient
ratio nor an unrelated Km can supply that mapping. A correction already made
must not reappear as an extra reliability multiplier.

Where an actual supported known-normal model and positive known variance are
available, the existing proposed source normalization is
S=e*abs(e)/(2V), B=tanh(S/2). Estimated variance needs its supported model.
The retained case does not supply the necessary matched empty-arm numbers and
joint uncertainty. S/B remain unknown. They are not zero, one or a confidence
probability, and the future model/covariance reader remains implementation work.

A synthetic engineering illustration, explicitly not paper data, is
x=(12,2,5,1)nmol/mg/min and C=diag(1,.25,.36,.09) in squared units.
It gives e=6 and V=1.70. This defines the requested arithmetic boundary without
manufacturing the missing source observations.056 owns only a proposal/reuse
audit for targeted population of these cells until root reviews its interface.

Do not multiply the sixfold increment, competition reduction, gradient ratio,
relative Km spread and repetition count as a reliability formula. They mix

Complementary reconstitution/metabolic controls address named limitations;
they do not become independent repeats of the same whole-cell measurement.
