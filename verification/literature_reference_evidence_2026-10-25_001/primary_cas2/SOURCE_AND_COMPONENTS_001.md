## Experimental definitions and actual comparisons

Nuñez JK, Kranzusch PJ, Noeske J, Wright AV, Davies CW and Doudna JA. “Cas1–Cas2 complex formation mediates spacer acquisition during CRISPR–Cas adaptive immunity.” Nature Structural & Molecular Biology 21:528–534 (2014), published online 4 May 2014. DOI 10.1038/nsmb.2820, PMID 24793649, PMC4075942. Identity is established by article XML identifiers/title/authors and cross-checked against the publisher/PubMed web result, rather than inferred from its retained filename.

The donor genes are E. coli K12 MG1655 cas1 and cas2. The in vivo assay recipient is E. coli BL21-AI, explicitly described as lacking all cas genes (P5); this is not a constructed single cas2 knockout in an otherwise native K12 CRISPR machinery. The proteins are co-overexpressed from pCDF-1b (P31). Figure 1b compares empty vector, Cas1 alone, Cas2 alone and Cas1+Cas2, each induced/uninduced. Figure 3c compares the Cas1+Cas2 WT construct with Cas1 tail/point variants and Cas2 C-terminal Δβ6–β7, with an empty-vector control. Figure 3e compares WT, empty vector and Cas2 E65/D84 interface variants in the Cas1-containing system. Figure 4d compares WT, empty vector and Cas2 E9A/N10A/R14A/R16A/R18A/R27A/E9R variants with Cas1. The Cas1 partner remains a contributor; “WT” refers to the plasmid protein pair in this recipient, not native WT-organism performance.

The Cas2 variants replace the plasmid WT Cas2 in their respective constructs; the recipient has no endogenous Cas2 according to P5. Therefore a WT+mutant Cas2 mixture is not the experimental design. Cas1 and Cas2 coexist as distinct required components. Unknown expression fractions, oligomer mixtures and cell-level contributor fractions remain unknown. The exact amino-acid boundaries of the Cas2 Δβ6–β7 deletion are not specified in the inspected methods/labels; do not invent them from strand names. P12 says this deletion removes the Cas1-backbone contact and D84 contact. The Cas1-tail deletion is explicitly P282–S305 and is a different construct.

There is **no matched native-WT / single-cas2-deletion / deletion-plus-WT-complement triple with quantitative same-endpoint values** in this paper. Empty-vector BL21-AI removes both Cas1 and Cas2 expression relative to the WT pair, so that comparison does not isolate Cas2. Cas1-only versus Cas1+Cas2 is the more focused add-back comparison, but still reconstitution in a cas-free recipient. Figure 3c's “deletion” is a Cas2 protein-domain truncation, not a native cas2 gene-deletion arm followed by an independently measured repair. These distinctions must survive extraction; the presence of labels WT, empty and Δ does not supply the requested restoration operands.

## Endpoint, conditions and measurement validity

P31: overnight LB with streptomycin 50 µg/mL; 1:300 inoculation into 10 mL with 0.2% L-arabinose, 0.1 mM IPTG and streptomycin 50 µg/mL; assay at 20–24 hours after induction. Cultures are normalized by OD600 for mutant comparisons. Boiled supernatants serve as PCR template; products are separated on 1.5% agarose. The measured endpoint is presence/pattern of expanded CRISPR-I PCR products, not calibrated acquisition events per cell, a direct catalytic rate, or organism survival. A +1 locus adds a typical 33-bp spacer and 28-bp repeat, 61 bp (P5). Figure 1b shows induced pair-specific expansion products labeled +1/+2/+3; qualitative band visibility cannot provide a numerical fraction of cells or a zero acquisition rate in other lanes.

PCR intensity has no supplied amplification-efficiency calibration, linearity validation, exposure saturation check, molecule/cell denominator, clone-screen denominator or limit of detection. Normalize neither bands nor visible expansions into a frequency score. Equal OD supports approximate template-input matching, not equal viable-cell number, protein abundance or quantitative expanded-locus fraction. The source's phrase “frequencies similar” for E9 variants (P16) is not numerical equivalence, and a nonsignificant difference was not supplied as an equivalence test.

Supplementary Table 1 lists 32 selected newly expanded-clone spacer entries: 30 length 33 bp and 2 length 34 bp; 26 labeled plasmid and 6 genomic. The denominator is **listed selected spacer entries**, not all eligible cells/clones assayed. Rows 16 and 26 have the same reported sequence; a repeated string does not prove either independent acquisition or duplicate biological observation. No sampling-block IDs, total clones screened or independent-lineage denominator are supplied. The list qualifies the endpoint's composition only.

## Comparability outside the intended change

Same recipient, vector family, induction protocol, time window and OD normalization provide specific comparability facts. Cas2 E65R has explicitly low expression in the Figure 3f caption; no quantitative ratio is reported. Input blots in Figures 3f and 4g/h and Supplementary Figure 5 retain visual expression information without a calibration or densitometric estimate. Identical induction instructions do not establish equal intracellular Cas2 dose. The assay's 20–24 h window is a reported range, not exact shared sampling times or ±2 h uncertainty.

For point mutations, expression differences are an unintended condition that could affect interpretation. For an intentional protein deletion, absence of the deleted domain is the intended change; do not score that same absence again as a nuisance mismatch. Cas2 amount/folding/stability may additionally differ and remain unquantified. Tag controls in Supplementary Figure 1a show that FLAG/HA constructs can support acquisition; they do not demonstrate that every tagged variant has quantitatively WT activity or expression.

## Effect isolation, claim relevance and complementary evidence

Figure 3f shows loss of co-immunoprecipitation for Cas1 R252E and Cas2 Δβ6–β7, alongside acquisition defects. This supports an assembly-associated contribution, but a truncation changes more than a single electrostatic contact and its effect is not isolated into a catalytic mechanism. The purification/gel-filtration check for Cas1 R252E in Supplementary Figure 2d addresses one Cas1 mutant's stability/oligomer behavior; it cannot be borrowed as a quantitative folding control for Cas2 truncations or all other variants.

Figure 4d establishes detectable spacer acquisition with E9A/E9R variants in this reconstituted system. P16 cites catalytic defects from other Cas2 homologs and earlier papers; this paper does not provide a matched quantitative nuclease activity measurement of these E. coli E9 variants. The substitution is known; a catalytic-null state, residual capacity fraction or physiological dispensability is not thereby measured here. Do not convert the authors' catalytic-dispensability interpretation into P_effective=1 or a necessity-counterevidence score. This packet opens no cited second paper.

Figures 4e/f/h and Supplementary Figure 3 measure retention of tagged Cas1 on CRISPR versus control biotinylated DNA in **cell lysates**, not purified-protein binding alone. P17 reports lack of sequence-specific DNA binding with purified proteins and suggests host factors. P18 detects no Cas2 in elution and explicitly offers washing-related loss as an explanation. These are materially different experimental systems; failure to detect Cas2 is not proof of no interaction. DNA probes are 186 bp, 100 nM (P33); the leader-scrambling supplementary panel uses six 125-bp probes (supplement page 4 caption). The main text describes a minimal 60-bp leader, while the supplementary schematics mark 64 bp. Keep exact probe identities/lengths rather than collapsing all comparisons to one leader length. Five washes and 400 mM KCl in IP can affect retention; pull-down amount is not Kd.

These assays address named alternative explanations (assembly, tag compatibility, DNA recognition), not independent copies of acquisition. Claim resolution is conditional contribution to acquisition/recognition in the reconstituted recipient. It is not direct demonstration of molecular nuclease turnover or native organism necessity.

## Uncertainty and repetition ownership

P31 says all acquisition assays were replicated at least three times; P32 says the same for FLAG IP. Store count>=3, exact biological-unit count=null, technical repeat count=null, pairing IDs=null. These words alone do not identify independently started cultures, clone lineage or sample covariance. Figure 4g and h explicitly use the same lysates: assembly and DNA precipitation share a physical material source. Supplementary Figure 3e's +/- annotations repeat Figure 3 results. Supplementary Figure 5 uncropped blots are representations of the original experiments, never additional experiments. Mutants within one gel share displayed WT/empty controls; those controls must be single canonical observations.

P29 separately reports three ITC Kd values (308,212,351 nM), average about 290 nM, with reported SD 58 nM; no independence beyond the paper's “three separate experiments” is invented. These describe purified WT pair association, not acquisition frequencies or mutant activity. Arithmetic yields mean 290.333 nM, population SD 58.104 nM, sample SD 71.163 nM. Thus the published “SD 58” matches the divisor-n spread; retain it as author-reported SD with convention qualification rather than silently using it as sample SD or SEM. This numerical audit does not add an A106 likelihood.

### Explicit combination proposal and its unavailable published total

Retain sheet003's descriptive effect/restoration meanings for a **verified three-arm same-endpoint comparison**: theta=(rWT-rD)/(rWT+rD), restoration_agreement=clip(1-abs(rC-rWT)/abs(rWT-rD),0,1). For the **reference experiment alone**, propose q_reference=s*theta*restoration_agreement*C*I with exponents one, C=product of distinct applicable measured nuisance-comparability terms, I as above. This is a bounded proposed conventional index, not an empirical probability or independent-factor likelihood. Propagate the actual joint admissible set through this one function. Do not multiply an uncertainty factor twice. Published q_reference=null here.

This is an explicitly proposed source-only projection of sheet003, **not setting H=1** and not modifying that historical sheet. Its M/O/H target-correspondence fields are outside the latest adopted literature scope; preserve assayed identity/conditions for later consumers but calculate no target-transfer scores. Likewise the old A105 sheet's sequence/structure/system correspondence rows are read for reset context, not reproduced as literature responsibilities. No conditional likelihood S or tanh(B) is calculated from the listed spacer fractions, gel visibility or an unknown acquisition error model.

Missing native WT is distinct from missing quantitative readout: even a future calibration of these exact gels would not create a native WT/single-deletion/complement experiment. A two-arm reconstitution difference can be retained as a two-arm descriptive effect after calibration; it cannot silently become the three-arm restoration score. ### Checked synthetic illustration, not Cas2 data

To make the proposed arithmetic reviewable, stipulate an unrelated synthetic matched triple of validated events per 100,000 eligible cells: rWT=100,rD=20,rC=90. Then loss=80, rescue_raw=70/80=.875, theta=80/120=2/3, restoration_agreement=.875. No uncertainty interval, probability, molecular correspondence or target coordinate is supplied. If rWT is absent the rescue fraction and q_reference are null; if rWT=rD their restoration denominator is singular. The arithmetic checks preserve these unavailable states instead of assigning zero.
