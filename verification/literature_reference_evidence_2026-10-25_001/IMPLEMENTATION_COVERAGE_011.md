## What the new reaction path establishes

The selected literature.reaction.populate operation reads an explicitly retained
numerical dataset and exact source witnesses. It does not interpret native NMR
spectra or turn arbitrary prose into experimental quantities. Fifteen numeric
cells implement reported ratio means, nominal squared residuals, per-independent-
unit or supported summary errors, fixed named-alternative contrasts and simple
resource capacity/coverage with logical bounds. Native ratio data, source
measurement limitations and proposed score conventions stay distinguishable.

Unknown source summaryn/SD leaves error and score unknown. Missing alternatives
are not favorable comparisons. Incomplete resources can leave nominal capacity
unknown while known explicit uppercaps constrain coverage; no unlimited missing
resource is invented. Coefficients, units and budgetbasis remain declared model
inputs. Current bounds do not model arbitrary dependence, network feasibility,
regeneration or demand uncertainty. Signed scalar ratios remain signed.

The original CsiD supplement reports succinate:L2HG=1:1; its nominal residual
against1:1 is0. Independentn,SD, quantitative recovery and a named comparison
remain unknown, so no uncertainty-qualified score follows. PurifiedLhgO UQ
reduction, membrane oxygen consumption and artificial DCPIP reduction have
different endpoint meanings; no measured productive oxygen coupling is inferred.
The HQNO text/figure discrepancy is retained without invented graph values.

The scalar comparison convention is s=(e_alternative-e_claim)/(their sum),
doublezero=>0, minimum over fixedalternatives. Resource comparison is2f-1.
These are bounded source comparison components, not confidence probabilities or
adopted annotation credit. ExistingA110026 compilation remains a proposal and
is not implemented by this subset.
