The 14 implemented optional submission parameters all exist under `SearchRequestSchema.properties`. Its defaults for numeric search controls are `E=1`, `domE=1`, `incE=.01`, `incdomE=.03`, `T=7`, `domT=5`, `incT=25`, `incdomT=22`, `popen=.02`, `pextend=.4`; other defaults are `threshold=evalue`, `mx=BLOSUM62`, and empty taxonomy include/exclude arrays. The exact typed fragments are in `supported-parameter-schema-extract.json`. The client exposes a narrower explicit subset and rejects null values at the adapter boundary; API fields such as `input_type`, `with_taxonomy` and `with_architecture` are not exposed. That scope restriction is not a wire-name mismatch.

The official searches page explains reporting/inclusion E-values and bit scores, gap penalties and substitution matrices. Its domain-E-value range tables contain an impossible bound, so those tables cannot establish current service validation. No compatibility or new option is inferred from those older entries.
