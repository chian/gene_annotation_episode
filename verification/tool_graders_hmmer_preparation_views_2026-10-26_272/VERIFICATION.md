## Validated boundaries and limits

Verified exact ordered row windows/keys, absent keys/artifacts, same-byte separate
roles, no whole-sheet disclosure, preserved nonzero partial/failed values,
measured zero versus missing/nonfinite/incompatible states, native repeated check
instances including contradiction, E1 distinct aggregate identity, S1 explicit-key
precedence, F1 null-key name/scope partition, exact variable units, wrong schema,
operation, occurrence, model direction and malformed structures. Unknown binary
content/ABI remains unknown. Recorded issue lists are not blanket failure flags.

and Store access plus all scientific parser/extractor/calculator/grade functions
while performing projection across all six families. Projection leaves supplied
grading and occurrence inputs unchanged. Returned schemas use the existing
closed communication grammar with exact receipt/identifier namespaces.
