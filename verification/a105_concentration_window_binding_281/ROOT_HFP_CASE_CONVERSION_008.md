# A105 HFP case conversion after root post-paper reset

The retained HFP paper packet was read as one publication. The root then reread
the immutable user goal and complete applicable authority set, current common
A105 worksheet, assay input reader, paired extraction/numerical proposal and
source corrections. The reset receipt is ROOT_HFP_POST_PAPER_RESET_007.json.

The A. aeolicus HFP preparation was produced in E. coli, so HFP and host proteins
coexisted in the assayed sample. HFP versus IMP replaced the added preparation
in the one-pot comparison; multiple other enzymes remained present. The NMR
isomerization endpoint reported Ru5P 0.397 and R5P 0.603. Their ratio 603/397
is 1.5188916876574308; the reported normalized spectral-integral ratio
152/252 is 0.6031746031746031 R5P. These are alternate representations of the
same chemical endpoint, not two independent experiments. The approximately
5/7 HFP/bulk mass fraction is nominal mass, not active fraction or the portion
of isomerase activity attributable to HFP.

For a compatible initial-rate assay, the existing A105 arithmetic combines
sample and blank by subtraction, subtracts a measured same-endpoint additional
contributor g, and multiplies by separately measured recovery, amount and
nuisance-condition-response corrections. Joint covariance is required for the
resulting uncertainty. This paper supplies no matched initial-rate q or g for
the isomerase attribution, so attributed rate, isolated share, corrected activity,
uncertainty and source-level evidence strength remain unavailable. The reported
triplicate NMR assays do not establish an independent preparation count or
estimator variance; the 512 instrument scans are not assay replicates.

The source identifies E. coli RpiA in the preparation, and a nonselective
purification/heat comparison slows approach to the endpoint. This is evidence
of a plausible alternative contributor and a challenge to HFP attribution.
It is not a measured contaminant activity. Construct, purification, heating,
target recovery and contaminant amount are not held quantitatively constant
between the compared preparations, so the completion-time ratio cannot populate
a matched comparability or causal-rate component. The source's HFP phosphatase
readout must retain its distinct endpoint; this isomerase limitation does not
erase it.

The paired extraction need is a constrained contributor relationship with
separate meanings for molecular identification, preparation perturbation and
matched same-scale measured activity or bound. A deterministic assembler may
feed g only from the last kind when endpoint, scale, conditions, preparation and
additive model are supported. Existing q-g arithmetic is already implemented;
the missing g here is source information. This case does not justify a numeric
confidence multiplier from peptide counts, 4/5 other preparations, gel
appearance or timing. The full A105 reliability/validity/relevance combination
remains unimplemented and is not adopted by this note.
