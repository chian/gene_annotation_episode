GenomeComparison scientific tool. The independent full native report reader
already exists. Its output is currently not consumed by numerical calculators
or attached to the complete proteome sheet. Do not replace that gap with more
legacy first-HSP averages or claim that descriptive native tables complete it.

The planning worker owns only a new verification directory, not production,
existing schemas, source-retention copies, tests, prompts or another candidate.
Its deliverable is the exact next parser-output -> numerical-sheet -> calculator
contract, grounded in actual existing reader output and BLAST grader code.

Read complete bvbrc_proteome_directional.py, directional_contracts.py and the
actual schema; inspect all three retained native examples and filled projections
from worker172. Read the actual BLAST pairwise/database components for reusable
mathematics. Read the producer-retention proposal170 and current B2/B3 validators

formula, overlap treatment, absent/contradictory/partial behavior and uncertainty

without loss of native fields or missing-state meanings. Overlapping HSPs must

give those different quantities explicit names and conditions.

files, downstream B2/B3/provider/return consumers and independent implementation
workloads. Give a small filled numerical sheet with checkable answers. Identify
which quantities can be calculated now and the exact missing witness fields for
