```text
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
{one row per returned, permitted finding; preserve measured values, units, bounds and uncertainty; distinguish an experiment from a reported prediction}

{Only for other originally requested information:}

| Requested information | Actual returned value and its declared meaning |
| --- | --- |
{requested constrained judgments, partial results, access/retrieval limitations, pending work, or other facts; preserve unavailable information explicitly}

{Only when originally requested and actually returned for approach steering:}

| Requested approach information | Recorded closing value | Meaning or availability |
| --- | --- | --- |
{the requested local credit, completed-own-action count, saved next-gain expectation/band, or continuation comparison; retain its actual units and unknowns}

Use these returned findings in your annotation decision. Preserve the actual assayed or reported subject and conditions when assessing applicability to this gene. An unavailable retrieval or passage supplies an access limitation; a scientific negative requires an actual source result. The publication citation supplies attribution.

Each candidate annotation keeps one global identity and fixed scoring operands through its revisions. Retain competing candidates separately with their own supplied support values. Integrate the returned evidence using your current received state; child local credit is separate from annotation support and from your received-information credit. You own the next scientific choice, and the configured controller owns continuation.
```

This is the complete proposed receiver message for **literature child → annotation root**. Braced fills are deterministic selections of actual requested information, not instructions for another model to compose a report. Remove optional blocks when unrequested. If no requested scientific finding was returned, replace the finding row with “No requested scientific finding was returned”; do not invent a result. The internal typed JSON form stays unchanged. The rendered message contains no opaque audit IDs, hashes, versions, revision numbers, schema, raw record, provenance envelope, article body or child-private history.

## DECISION trace: this parent and boundary

The annotation root asks what published findings change this candidate annotation, qualify its support, preserve an alternative, or require further investigation. It needs the distinctions it requested, including the actual source proposition and the source's subject, organism, construct and relevant conditions where those are declared return information. Family/domain support, activity, physiological role and localization remain distinct claims. An assay on a reference construct and a reported predicted label retain their actual meanings when the root evaluates transfer to ybhP/b0790.

The child returns the original parent's requested scientific information. The root interprets and integrates that contribution; a literature interpretation account does not itself replace the root annotation. A source-grounded negative, nontransferability finding or correction may improve the investigation while reducing annotation support. The return does not select the next root action, require another investigation, or introduce a scientific completion gate.

The root may consider an approach's local outcome only if it originally requested available closing quantities. This literature boundary supplies no private query/reading trajectory, automatic process-comparison record, inferred trend, or newly composed recommendation.

## Inspected producer → admission → root consumer path

1. The complete `LiteratureEpisodes` binding uses the common loop and scientific credit. Its `fact_sources` and `facts` return empty mappings: there is no independent literature summary source or automatic literature process-results record. Source custody and own-outcome metadata in its decision context belong to the literature child's own iteration, not this return.
2. `DecisionSource.apply_response` validates exactly the nonsourced `report_update` fields and records their actual prior information basis. `DecisionSource.finish` obtains `Workflow.facts`, fills only the original `x-source` fields through `form_with_facts`, freezes `runtime_forms`, then releases the closed source. No closing report model call is needed. `Workflow.facts` preserves actual committed/changed records, latest interpretation basis, later observations, local credit/yield and only the configured extra facts.
3. The detached path reconstructs the original contract in `returned_request`; `episode_admission._admit_form` compares sourced values with frozen child facts and admits the original closed form. `admit_return` records the attempt, invokes `receive_form`, and projects the root's cumulative received information. One original commission completes; the child's internal turns do not become root observations. Replay preserves the admission, and a closed receiver is not repriced. The blocking child projection follows the same original typed-contract and `receive_form` boundary.
4. `receive_form` selects typed-reference positions through `_references_in` and records receipts. `EpisodeReceipts.receive` follows existing forward references, while `Store.reasoning_view(record_selection=...)` can later present received immutable versions. This machinery records knowledge and dependencies; it is not field-level permission to render an inherited record closure or read artifact bytes.
5. The root's `DecisionSource.next` passes admitted `view.child_reports` through `model_inputs`; `method_loop._model_inputs` accepts only reports admitted for that parent. The root's existing received state and separate `annotation_context` provide integration context. The current provider serializes context as JSON rather than producing this proposed text/table message.

## Omissions and gaps exposed for human steering

- Omit literal child queries, search hit lists, navigation/reading order, byte coverage and child-private decisions unless a specific original requested returned fact needs one of those values for the root's decision. No retrieval completeness claim follows from those activities.
- Omit raw source text, quoted anchors, artifact/source custody envelopes, full inherited dependency closures, internal identities and schemas. Keep only permitted scientific distinctions and bibliographic attribution.
- There is no current literature-to-root text renderer. Parent purpose and field meanings currently accompany the child commission rather than each rendered return. The proposed message needs deterministic selection after admission and before root presentation; JSON transport, validation and durable snapshots stay internal.
- Shape/namespace/scope validation does not declare exact displayable fields of a returned assessment/reference. A future renderer needs an explicit allowed-field interpretation of the original requested meaning. It cannot enrich an assessment-only or generic changed-record form with undeclared article content. Missing source proposition, conditions, evidence class, native values or access-failure meaning must remain visible as gaps.
- The literature binding exposes no dedicated search-access report, per-candidate support decomposition, initial local-credit fact or approach-trend record. Closing `episode_yield` exists, conditionally requested; it supplies only its saved local quantities. This draft adds none of those missing facts and changes no scoring or continuation behavior.
