## Actual assay and positive contrast

The assayed protein was Pa0142 from *Pseudomonas aeruginosa* PAO1, expressed from pET-30b(+) in *E. coli* BL21(DE3), then fractionated by ammonium sulfate, size exclusion and anion exchange. The SI reports a single SDS-PAGE band and matching first five amino acids. It reports average 0.25 Zn and 0.05 Fe equivalents per subunit; neither number measures active enzyme fraction. The construct's retained tag state is not explicitly established. [Article p. 1762](https://www.chem.tamu.edu/rgroup/raushel/publications/P245-JACS-8-Oxoguanine%20Deaminase-2009.pdf); [SI pp. S1–S2](https://ndownloader.figshare.com/files/4485787).

More than 50 candidate aromatic bases, nucleosides and nucleotides were screened. Ammonia formation was monitored through a glutamate-dehydrogenase/NADH/α-ketoglutarate coupled assay at pH 8.0. The article says product formation in each active case was confirmed by mass spectrometry and UV spectroscopy. Activity was found for 8-oxoguanine (8-oxoG), guanine, isocytosine and ammeline. The paper identifies the 8-oxoG deamination product as uric acid and the guanine deamination product as xanthine; the coupled assay measures ammonia. The article does not provide spectra, chromatograms, mass errors, product yields, or chemical standards for the confirmations. [Article pp. 1762–1763](https://www.chem.tamu.edu/rgroup/raushel/publications/P245-JACS-8-Oxoguanine%20Deaminase-2009.pdf).

These values are from article p. 1763, left column, opening paragraph. “±” is printed, but the article and SI do not define SD versus SEM, replicate count, independent preparation count, or fit covariance. Guanine `kcat/Km` is compatible with dividing rounded `kcat` by rounded `Km`; it is **one kinetic estimate**, not an independent confirmation. The 8-oxoG `kcat/Km` is a source-reported slope/efficiency while individual `kcat` and `Km` remain unavailable.

## Control and alternative limits

The >50-compound screen and “activity only” list are useful alternatives, but the complete substrate identities, tested concentrations, exposure, blank/background, lower detection limits and per-substrate traces are not supplied. Thus the other compounds are qualitative non-detections, not zero rates or calibrated negative score operands. 8-oxoG limited solubility is a stated assay restriction; no saturated `kcat`/`Km` or within-assay concentration-response profile can be reconstructed from this paper. Product confirmation is a source assertion whose analytical selectivity cannot be numerically scored from the absent MS/UV data. No no-enzyme, heat-inactivated, matrix-only or coupling-enzyme interference control is tabulated. These are **unreported inputs**, not evidence that the controls failed.

There is **no usable quantitative method-validity or effect-isolation operand** beyond the source efficiency estimates. In particular, a blank-corrected signal or no-enzyme rate (with its matched enzyme/substrate conditions) is absent; calibrated product recovery/response and product-versus-ammonia stoichiometry are absent; active-site titration or active enzyme amount is absent; and variance across independent protein preparations is absent. The reported 0.25 Zn equivalents per subunit is a bulk purification measurement, not an active-site titration, assay control, or matched blank. Consequently, no numerical blank-normalized effect, product-recovery fraction, stoichiometric agreement, active-enzyme correction, or preparation-level variance can be calculated from this article/SI.

Pa0134 (*P. aeruginosa*) and b2883 (*E. coli* K12) were separate purified guanine deaminases. Their guanine efficiencies were (4.2 ± 0.9) × 10⁴ and (8.1 ± 0.4) × 10⁴ M⁻¹ s⁻¹, respectively. The paper gives a maximum 8-oxoG deamination rate constant **<5 × 10⁻³ s⁻¹** in a sentence following both proteins; it does not disaggregate a per-protein threshold or report its detection conditions. Preserve this as a paper-level censored comparator `kcat` statement; it is neither zero nor a Pa0142 measurement. Do not compare that `s⁻¹` bound directly to Pa0142 `M⁻¹ s⁻¹` efficiency. [Article p. 1763, left column, second paragraph](https://www.chem.tamu.edu/rgroup/raushel/publications/P245-JACS-8-Oxoguanine%20Deaminase-2009.pdf).

The Pa0142 8-oxoG/guanine efficiency ratio is a **point contrast of two reported point estimates**. A same-parameter ratio involving a censored alternative could at most give a bound if the subject, conditions and denominator orientation were established. The comparator `<5 × 10⁻³ s⁻¹` is a censored *different-enzyme `kcat`*, so this paper supplies no valid bounded Pa0142 efficiency ratio from that statement. The paper does not provide a numerical denominator detection limit for the other screen-negative substrates; their ratios are likewise unavailable rather than infinite or lower bounded.

The separately purified Sargasso Sea homolog (43% sequence identity) has reported 8-oxoG, guanine and isocytosine efficiencies of (2.7 ± 0.1) × 10⁵, (5.2 ± 0.6) × 10⁴ and (3.9 ± 0.5) × 10⁴ M⁻¹ s⁻¹. Its structure is **not** a Pa0142 structure: Pa0142 crystallization failed, and the homolog was crystallized separately. No native-organism in vivo function test is reported; the authors explicitly say such tests were in progress. These records can support a separate within-homolog contrast but cannot be silently pooled with Pa0142 or transferred to an unseen annotation target. [Article p. 1763](https://www.chem.tamu.edu/rgroup/raushel/publications/P245-JACS-8-Oxoguanine%20Deaminase-2009.pdf); [SI pp. S2, S6](https://ndownloader.figshare.com/files/4485787).

## Decision and testable reader gap

This is a **source-positive case** for a measured Pa0142 substrate-efficiency contrast with a real alternative substrate. The paper permits numerical descriptive A105 cells but does not permit the current kinetic sheet's model-based source profile/index, an independent-replication claim, an assay-wide false-negative rate, or target annotation credit. Reliability remains limited by undefined ±, absent preparation/replicate/covariance and raw curves; analytical validity is supported by reported MS/UV confirmation but cannot be quantified from underlying data; relevance is direct to the purified Pa0142 enzyme under the stated assay, with native in vivo relevance unresolved. These are separate interpretations, not a categorical grade.
