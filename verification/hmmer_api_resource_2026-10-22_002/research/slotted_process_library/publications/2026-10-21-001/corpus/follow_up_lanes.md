## Annotation distinctions to check against the structured credit form

These entries identify distinctions that the form may need to express through its declared fields and allowed values. They do not assign credit, introduce axes or weights, or add a freehand assessment:

- **F01:** preserve separate support for broad chemistry, exact specificity, and native responsibility. A positive assay cannot silently establish all three. Preserved affinity for one target also does not establish preserved discrimination against alternatives; a protein-level substrate claim does not establish all modification sites.
- **F02:** distinguish compatibility/feasibility from demonstrated reaction and coupling. An unresolved partner or electron acceptor remains unresolved.
- **F03:** support belongs to the demonstrated component contribution. System output cannot be assigned independently to every participating protein. Environmental response, direct input action, binding and productive control are different links; binding-site evidence does not assign regulatory sign.
- **F04:** distinguish catalytic capacity from the spatial prerequisites of a physiological claim. Predicted access and measured access carry different support. A processing readout can support cleavage compatibility while leaving native assembly unresolved or contradicted.
- **F05:** replacement can support a pathway contribution without establishing identical chemistry or universal necessity. Functional overlap and conditional demand matter. Short-term recovery and prolonged survival may change in opposite directions; the supported physiological consequence must retain its time window and population context.

A correction can advance investigation while reducing annotation support. The same underlying observation may inform multiple questions; these cross-links do not make it independent evidence or justify counting it repeatedly. No score formula, saved observation, or credit assignment has been changed.

## Cross-lane reuse to keep testing

- **Measured capacity versus native responsibility:** F01 and F05 share this question through YihU/SuhB/YigL and FolM. It can also matter in F04 when a protein is experimentally relocated. The [YihU/SuhB branch comparison](specificity/detailed_traces.json) keeps the shared pattern while changing the discriminating evidence: YihU needs substrate/native-pathway discrimination, whereas SuhB needs an explanation of responsibility that its measured phosphatase capacity does not supply.
- **Attribution within a measured system:** F01 HFP, F02 F420, and F03 EnvC/FtsW address different ways that system output can be assigned to the wrong contributor. The [Cas1–Cas2 case and two routes](causal/cas2/case_trace.json) extend this inquiry beyond metabolism: distinguish signature-site, assembly and donor-DNA-positioning contributions, then seek positive evidence for the retained role. E. coli E9-mutant acquisition/integration does not independently establish an E. coli nuclease-null mutant. Their discriminating controls are different; a shared pattern must preserve those biological differences.
- **Separating associated properties:** F03 stable/localized but defective EnvC and ATPase-active but poorly coupled TsaE connect with F04's distinction between export, targeting and activity.
- **Alternative routes versus absent demand:** F05 replacement and conditional necessity can motivate F02 reaction accounting or F03 component attribution. The actual [BioZ/ThyX bookkeeping](replacement/raw/route_bookkeeping_probe.json) distinguishes changed carbon entry from changed folate-regeneration demand. Identical final products do not establish interchangeable inputs or enzyme requirements; projected balances do not establish native flux or a particular native electron donor.

These are candidate reusable patterns, not four additional lanes or extra slots. Mixing entries must preserve the biological question, available evidence, and conditions. For example, a general substrate-comparison tool cannot by itself decide whether growth rescue restores the proposed molecular defect.

- **Predictor/function counterexamples:** F01 HisN and deaminase comparisons challenge overall-similarity or residue rules; F05 asks whether function persists without its usual gene. [Actual table calculations](comparison/counterexample_calculations.json) preserve the different force of complementation results, censored kinetics, and genomic missingness.

## Tools: actual work versus candidates

All biological experiments cited above were performed by the published authors. Our use of their evidence does not make those experiments available execution tools.

Actual research work is recorded in the source files linked below. It includes primary-text/table inspection; HemK sequence comparisons; YihU motif scanning and reported-kinetics unit conversion; Bcd redox arithmetic; UbiUV isotope-expectation arithmetic; and deposited record/coordinate checks for EnvC, QueH, MurJ and F420. Follow-up work additionally extracted HisN Table 3 and calculated rank inversions, and rechecked deaminase substrate-ratio bounds. The Cas1–Cas2 inquiry additionally calculated deposited-coordinate distances to distinguish central donor-DNA contacts, the Cas1 interface, and donor-end placement; these are geometric observations, not new binding or catalytic measurements. YihU mutant-table arithmetic is embedded in its [existing trace](specificity/detailed_traces.json); it addresses substrate-dependent perturbation rather than native flux. These checks have their stated local limits and are not new biological experiments or blind annotation discoveries.

Named tool candidates for the growing tools slot include:

- **Sequence/profile search:** [HMMER](https://github.com/EBI-Metagenomics/HMMER-web-docs/blob/master/docs/api.rst) and [MMseqs2](https://www.mmseqs.com/latest/userguide.pdf). Candidate use here does not establish installation, a selected search database, or a completed run.
- **Structure search:** [Foldseek](https://github.com/steineggerlab/foldseek), followed where relevant by deposited-residue/ligand inspection. A structural match alone does not establish specificity.
- **Spatial prediction:** [SignalP 6.0](https://services.healthtech.dtu.dk/services/SignalP-6.0/) and [DeepTMHMM](https://services.healthtech.dtu.dk/services/DeepTMHMM-1.0/). These have not been run for this follow-up collection; prediction is distinct from measured export, orientation, or transport. The archaeal cases specifically require documented class III leader recognition. Their partial motif/length audits do not establish a SignalP result, a FlaFind rerun, or a universally valid cleavage rule.
- **DNA motif scanning:** [FIMO](https://meme-suite.org/meme/doc/fimo.html) accepts supplied motifs and FASTA sequences and returns candidate site positions and scores. This is a tool candidate, not a completed scan or a prediction of regulatory sign.
- **Numerical and record analysis:** explicit arithmetic, coordinate measurements, kinetics normalization, reaction bookkeeping, and analyses of supplied expression, proteomic or time-course data. Some bounded calculations were run as noted above; broader analyses remain candidates and require suitable inputs.
- **Published-evidence retrieval/extraction:** shared across all five lanes. It supplies the reported conditions, controls and outcomes needed to interpret other tool results; it does not predetermine a scientific conclusion.

The table does not prescribe a task sequence. A thought pattern should constrain the child's alternative explanations and subsequent decisions; a tool supplies an operation or evidence. The three-slot hypothesis remains open while more cases fill its entries and expose gaps.
