## Paired extraction and numerical cells

The measured subject is purified Pa0142 from *Pseudomonas aeruginosa* PA01; the original paper says it was expressed and purified, and the SI describes the expression/purification protocol. The proposed annotation-relevant claim is that this assayed reference enzyme deaminates 8-oxoG and that its reported catalytic efficiency exceeds the two named alternative substrate measurements. The experiment monitors ammonia with a glutamate-dehydrogenase/NADH/α-ketoglutarate coupled endpoint at pH 8.0; the authors say they confirmed products by MS and UV, without providing those primary traces here. Substrates are separate assays. In the original Scheme 1 and p.1763 text, 8-oxoG is (1), guanine is (2), and isocytosine is (3); the p.1763 `For 2 ... respectively` and `For 3 ... corresponding values` clauses assign the reported parameter order. These labels and clauses are exact selected witnesses in the test.
