## Exact inputs, outputs and limits

### C01

**Parent inputs / explicit tool dependencies:** Exact HemK sequence, the failed short Dam comparison and the recorded reference comparisons.

**Tool return:** Sequences, alignment positions and native scores; existing published substrate results remain separate inputs.

**Limit:** A useful match does not assign RF1/RF2 or glutamine specificity. These known-outcome comparisons were actually made in the research; no blind annotation is claimed.

### C02

**Parent inputs / explicit tool dependencies:** Published HisN complementation and similarity rows, including motif-deficient HisN2.

**Tool return:** Selected sequence/motif correspondences; supplied source-table scores are retained as published results.

**Limit:** The selected rank inversion does not validate a motif classifier or turn failed complementation into purified-enzyme inactivity.

### C03

**Parent inputs / explicit tool dependencies:** The broad amidohydrolase relationship and published substrate/condition/product records.

**Tool return:** Condition-indexed apparent kinetic values, ratios and censored bounds.

**Limit:** In-vitro substrate discrimination does not establish the dominant cellular substrate. No new assay, docking or kinetic fit was run.

### C04

**Parent inputs / explicit tool dependencies:** Independent queuosine-pathway placement and the authors' biochemical rejection of the inherited reaction.

**Tool return:** Exact source windows containing the reported cofactor, product and alternative-reaction observations, with source identity.

**Limit:** Reading returns source evidence; the thought pattern supplies the decision about which constraints survive. No biological assay is performed by this selection.

### C05

**Parent inputs / explicit tool dependencies:** Measured mature Q in the specified QueG-lacking organism and the relevant pathway inventory.

**Tool return:** Recorded component distributions, neighborhoods and fusions for selected candidates.

**Limit:** An absent annotation is not proven gene absence. The QueH cellular result does not establish isolated productive catalysis or a native electron donor.

## What recombines

C02/C03/C13/C22 hold target specificity (A02) while changing the thought pattern and evidence operations. C06/C20 retain contribution attribution (P06) while changing annotation scope and tools. C08/C12 retain native physiology (A08) while changing the resource-versus-time question. These crossings depend on explicit biological identities, conditions and available evidence. They do not establish that every A × P × T combination is meaningful. Input repair or later selection revision remains an episode decision; no fixed order follows from the examples.
