**3. Candidate recurring numerical cells, and what they cannot mean**

The common object should be a **measured result paired with a particular inference**. A single paper can provide a strong number for transport and no number for activation; a claim may emerge after investigating supplied sequence, selected file material or snippets. The sheet does not require an initial annotation candidate.

The following are proposals to test across lanes. They deliberately distinguish native measurements from evaluative cells.

| Proposed numerical cell | Calculation or grading anchors | Interpretation and counterexample |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Attributable signal / performance contrast | Blank-corrected reaction rate; mutant/parent rate; log2 competitive index; or a normalized physical difference, with native operands and units retained. Example: growth-rate ratio = parent doubling time / replacement doubling time. | Measures size of this assay outcome. A large growth effect can be indirect; a small biochemical rate can sustain native function. BioY has different affinity and capacity directions. No conversion to confidence is justified by magnitude alone. |
| Measurement precision | Report numerical SE/SD/CI and biological/preparation n separately. A descriptive SE/normalized-value ratio is possible, but not a new confidence probability or CI without the correct model/covariance. | XyGUL has n=3 biological cultures and SEM. FolM's three replicates are not shown to be independent protein preparations. Many inspected fit uncertainties remain null. |
| Claim-specific resolution, proposed ordinal 0–3 | 0: no measurement of the relevant claimed endpoint. 1: a responsive association or indirect proxy. 2: the relevant endpoint measured but subject-specific causal attribution unresolved. 3: relevant endpoint plus a discriminating subject intervention/control/rescue. Each grade must name the precise endpoint and rivals. | ComF native conditional contribution can receive proposed grade 3; purified ComF sulfur-transfer chemistry was not measured here. An EMSA cannot receive grade 3 for activation merely because its binding signal is excellent. These are resolution classes, not evenly spaced confidence levels. |
| Named weakness resolution, proposed 0–2 per weakness | 0: threat demonstrably untested by the available design; 1: indirect constraint; 2: a control or complementary experiment directly distinguishes that threat. If the protocol is unknown, use null rather than 0. Keep each threat and experiment link. | A HemY positive control addresses assay competence; heat-treated fractions address spontaneous oxidation. Neither alone repairs a missing HemJ electron donor. Do not grade every metadata field or count redundant controls as additional strength. |
| Mechanistic equivalence indicator | 1 only when the compared transformations, substrates/co-substrates and products match to the claimed level; 0 when demonstrated different chemistry restores the same downstream output; null when rescue chemistry is unresolved. | BioZ versus BioH has equivalence 0 while BioZ's pimelate-production evidence is strong. This cell measures sameness, not evidence quality. CobY replaces one activity of bifunctional CobU. |
| Consistency of genuinely comparable datasets | Retain numbers supporting, opposing and noninformative; a concordance fraction is calculable only after dataset identity, endpoint, direction and conditions are aligned. Always retain the denominator and exclusions. | Do not pool short ex-vivo urine nonphenotype with prolonged animal fitness, or glucose growth with photoautotrophy. Two papers and multiple readouts do not establish independent replication. No lane-wide consistency fraction was calculated. |
| Demonstrated system transfer | Quantify the *same endpoint* in source and target systems where measured, e.g. rescue performance by homolog/host/condition. Retain target correspondence separately; use null if the actual target system is unspecified or not assayed. | BioZ homologs at approximately 70% identity can differ in purified assay behavior. FolM Pseudomonas follow-up tested folX, not a native folM deletion. Neither identity nor species name supplies an applicability weight. |

A useful complementarity cell is the **change in resolution of a named weakness**, not the number of additional experiments. It can record a transition such as indirect constraint (1) to direct discriminating test (2), with the remaining weaknesses still shown. Repeated representations of the same dataset have no second transition.

**4. Worked contrasts with actual numbers**

**A. Strong conditional physiological evidence: ComF.** In standard medium containing 0.4 mM sulfide, the deletion grows at approximately 70% of the parental rate without CoM: an approximate rate deficit of **1 − 0.70 = 0.30**. The reported error type is SD across three cultures, but its numerical value was not extracted; no confidence interval can be filled. Removing sulfide and passaging exposes CoM auxotrophy; gene complementation and 1 µM CoM rescue distinguish a specific physiological contribution. Raising sulfide to 1.5 mM masks the defect. The 137 µM fitted growth-yield EC50 is a medium-response measurement, not enzyme affinity. A proposed claim-resolution grade is **3 for native conditional contribution**, while isolated mechanism remains unmeasured. High sulfide is not opposing replication of the sulfide-free claim. The backup L-ASST mechanism is still uncertain. [Source: Figures 3–4 and sec007](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12068725/).

**B. Misleading adequacy of endpoint accumulation: BioY.** BioY alone accumulates biotin approximately 1,000-fold over 3.5 hours at 4 nM external biotin, apparently making BioMN dispensable. Initial rates instead give BioY apparent half-saturation **250 nM** and Vmax **60 pmol min−1 mg-protein−1**. BioMNY has a reported **50-fold smaller half-saturation constant**: a derived approximate 5 nM, with fit uncertainty unknown. Its exact Vmax was not taken from a plot or invented from the phrase “an order of magnitude lower.” Affinity gain is thus **50**, while the substrate-specific transport advantage remains null without both fitted rates. These are different outcomes of the same construct family, not inconsistent repeats. The linked correction concerns Figure 4 printing; the online figure is corrected. [Source: sec3–5 and uptake Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1815280/), [correction](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1829295/).

**C. A complementary experiment localizes the weakness: XyGUL.** Table ED2 defines no growth as **ΔOD600 < 0.05**. ΔGH5 fails on polymer, but GH5-digested substrate permits **70 ± 9%** WT final density and **0.6 ± 0.2** WT rate (SEM, three biological replicates). Thus the filled performance cells are **0.70 with SEM 0.09** for density and **0.60 with SEM 0.20** for rate; they are correlated endpoints. This is partial downstream competence after removing a polymer-access bottleneck, not complete gene rescue. ΔGH9 on polymer gives **119 ± 11%** density and **1.2 ± 0.2** rate despite demonstrable purified activity, defeating an inference that every active/induced hydrolase is individually required. The same ΔGH5 reaches **139 ± 17%** density and **2.2 ± 0.2** rate on dp14 substrate, so substrate preparation changes the apparent defect. Do not compute a new rescue fraction from unlike substrate baselines. [Source: main Results and Table ED2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4282169/#T2).

**D. Functional replacement requires a metabolic partner: ThyX.** The narrative minimal-medium experiment gives doubling times of **103 min** WT, **108 min** ThyA+FolA and **310 min** ThyA alone. Relative exponential rates are therefore **103/108 = 0.954** and **103/310 = 0.332**. DNA-normalized de-novo dT labeling is **70% lower** for ThyA alone, or **0.30 of paired replacement**. The isotope endpoint addresses the nonspecificity of growth; it is not a second replication of growth or a direct folate-pool measurement. Table 2 uses enriched MPYE values **122/133/287 min**: its separate ratios, **0.917/0.425**, cannot silently replace the minimal-medium figures. Biological n and effect uncertainties remain null. [Source: sec9–10, Table 2 and Methods sec4](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2168944/).

**E. Capacity is real but its physiological interpretation changes: FolM.** At matched 50 µM substrates, 100 µM NADPH and pH 6, FolM dihydromonapterin reduction is **16-fold** faster than dihydrofolate reduction. This fills a discrimination-ratio cell; it does not erase the original DHFR capacity or alone establish native flux. The new assay's Vmax/Km values are **5.99 µmol min−1 mg−1 / 147 µM**; older dihydrofolate values quoted in the 2010 text are **0.083 / 9.5**, and are not a new matched assay or independent replication. Genetically controlled PhhA/PhhB rescue supplies complementary physiological context. A native Pseudomonas folX result must not be relabeled a folM deletion. [Source: sec13–15 and Figure 4](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2805310/).

**F. A physical effect and a contextual tradeoff: IsiA.** Visually inspected Figure 3 gives amplitude-weighted lifetimes **1.95 ns** oxidized WT, **3.04 ns** reduced WT and **4.25 ns** oxidized C260V. An explicitly conditional apparent quenching contrast is **1 − 1.95/4.25 = 0.541**. It assumes the mutant is a suitable unquenched reference with comparable other decay pathways; fitted uncertainty is unknown. PSI-transfer controls address the alternative that the mutant simply lost antenna function. At 800 µmol photons m−2 s−1 under severe iron limitation, the initially advantaged mutant declines after approximately **30 hours**; 200 µmol photons m−2 s−1 avoids the bleaching phenotype. Its physiological score must retain condition and time. [Source: Figures 3–4, Figure 7 and growth Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8545134/).

**5. What compilation must preserve, and the precise remaining gap**

Compile only after matching **inference, subject, assay endpoint, conditions and dataset identity**. Keep native effect, uncertainty, claim resolution, named validity threats, comparable-result consistency and system correspondence as separate numerical inputs. Complementarity should improve the resolution of the specific weakness it addresses. It must not create independent replication, erase an opposing result, or make a growth endpoint identify an unmeasured catalytic mechanism. Normalized rates, lifetimes, molecular masses and growth EC50 values cannot be added as if they shared units.

For example, ComF's approximately 0.30 deletion effect, three cultures and proposed resolution grade 3 support a conditional native role; none supplies a numerical probability of the proposed sulfur-transfer mechanism. For BioZ, a mechanistic-equivalence indicator of 0 coexists with strong alternative-route evidence. For HemJ, pathway requirement plus a controlled negative isolated assay leaves a specific unresolved missing-partner problem, not a single intermediate confidence average.

A candidate compilation operation is to deduplicate experimental representations, retain the strongest directly applicable resolution **per named weakness**, and retain every opposing or context-limited result beside it. This is a proposal for how component records relate, **not a total reliability formula**. The precise unresolved step is calibration: claim-resolution classes, native effect magnitudes, heterogeneous uncertainty and biological transfer do not currently share a justified cardinal scale or probability model. Averaging the ordinal classes or multiplying a 16-fold substrate ratio by a rescue fraction would invent such a scale. A finished part-2 design must show which numerical reliability/credit output it wants, how opposing and correlated results change it, and how unresolved transfer remains visible. This lane does not fill that gap with arbitrary weights or claim completion from the component list.

**6. Newly exposed follow-up lanes**

- **Conditional necessity, assay duration and carryover.** ComF's sulfide/passage contrast, IsiA's approximately 30-hour reversal and DsdA's six-hour versus 96-hour models require more examples with absolute time series and explicit detection limits. Next sources: ComF S9 numerical spreadsheet; IsiA growth/fit source data; original DsdA per-organ CFU/competitive-index panels.
- **Replacement topology and metabolite burden.** BioZ, CobY, FolM, FolA bypass and ThyX require separately scored chemical equivalence, downstream output and native role. Next reads: BioZ carrier-specific product spectra and inactive homolog preparations; the full FolM 2003 Methods; ThyX raw labeling/replicate information and direct folate-pool follow-ups. Do not infer that one rescued gene supplies every function of a multifunctional original.
- **Source-to-target transfer with matched endpoints.** BioZ homologs and FolM cross-organism tests show why identity, heterologous rescue and native genotype evidence cannot be collapsed. Next sources: matched donor-native deletions and protein-expression/stability controls for BioK/BioG; XoxF metal blanks/native occupancy; complete LysU 1990 and 2017 growth methods to align the conflict.
- **Assay product recovery and spontaneous chemistry.** QueH substrate loss without product and HemJ spontaneous oxidation need controls with quantified product recovery, blanks and limits. Next primary reads: QueH 2021 assay chromatograms and standards; HemJ assay/raw positive-control rates. These experiments can narrow an annotation without producing a positive reaction score.
- **Community and host resource access.** Ttr, EutC, Pdu, SdhA and nitrate suggest matched competitive-index interactions with producer/host perturbations. Next reads: absolute metabolite concentrations and donor-mutant pleiotropy, source/consumer fitness and host-stratified n. Per-gene chemistry cannot be obtained by assigning a whole-locus host phenotype to each member.

These are proposed research lanes for root ownership reconciliation, not additional episode levels, mandatory experiment sequences or runtime gates. Part 3 must wait for settled part-2 cells and then populate those exact cells; no reader/scorer was designed or coded in this lane.
