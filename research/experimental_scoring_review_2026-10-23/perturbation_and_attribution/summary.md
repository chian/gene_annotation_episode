## What the cases make worth scoring

Every row below refers to the matching `case_group` in [case_reviews.jsonl](case_reviews.jsonl), which retains exact original locators, sources, experimental boundaries and missing operands. These are inference-specific weaknesses; they are not a checklist every experiment must pass.

| Assay family / cases | Misleading inference or weakness | Comparison that changes what is established | Numerical implication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Processor deletion, cleavage and assembly: archaeal Hvo/PibD–FlaK | Cleavage or deletion phenotype identifies native assembled machinery | Native deletions, rescue and an expressed/processed PilA1 hybrid that still fails assembly | Record cleavage and assembly as different endpoints; 9/9 PibD leader lengths versus 6/9 FlaK is **tested-panel coverage**, not relative catalytic efficiency, because membranes/temperature differ. |
| Activation versus enzyme chemistry: ActS, EnvC, NlpI | Hydrolase-like fold or partner binding implies direct cleavage/activation | Protein-alone activity; enzyme addition; stable/localized variants; ternary-complex and partner-specific activity tests | Affinity, catalytic rate and rescue need separate magnitudes and claim-link scores. Count of variants is not independent replication. |
| Contributor attribution: Cas2, FtsW | Every required component performs the same chemistry | Defined products and selective catalytic/interface perturbations | Attribute output to a component only at the specificity supported by controls. Signature-site tolerance is not proof of directly measured catalytic null. |
| Turnover and coupling: TsaE, YihI | ATPase strength or growth rescue proves productive biochemical repair | Single versus repeated turnover; free-product measurement; growth rescue despite unrepaired Der GTPase defect | Product/complex ceiling and repeated-turnover output differ. A 3.5-fold initial-velocity difference cannot be averaged with binding Kd or growth rescue. |
| Spatial association and necessity: Cdv, CetZ, DolP, PopZ, MinJ | Colocalization or co-IP establishes physiological mechanism | Perturbation, addback, elongated-cell controls, localization hierarchy, direct binding | Direct binding may score strongly for binding and weakly for the broader cellular mechanism using the **same measured Kd**. |
| Tunnel/contact versus flux: LetB, YhdP | Lipid contact and architecture establish net transport | Pore/length mutants, sensitized physiology, redundant depletion and lipid partitioning | No direct flux means no fabricated transport rate or flux precision. Multiple convergent endpoints do not become replicated flux measurements. |
| Rescue and pathway relation: UgtP, Y-complex, YejM, YqfG, YneA | Rescue restores the proposed molecular lesion or uniquely identifies its mediator | Single-gene controls, bypass, turnover tests, matched molecular readout | Score rescue capacity separately from mechanism repair; positive bypass can coexist with an unrepaired original defect. |
| Composite substrates/state dependence: YbeY, YjgA | Degraded product or associated subunit is the original damaged component | Reciprocal subunit reconstitution; assembled versus isolated particles; state-resolved structure | Count preparations rather than cells/particles; state occupancy does not supply a reaction rate. |
| Complex membership: YfgM, YhcB | Co-migration/co-purification identifies an obligate catalytic component | Independent purification/kinetics; detergent-aware crosslinking; activity comparisons | Preserve the original association result and the negative stable-membership result. New cellular endpoints are not inconsistent repeats of oxidase kinetics. |
| Export/access: AmiA/AmiC, CcdA/DsbD, DsbD topology, MurJ | Reporter fails because substrate is absent; inaccessible cysteine means opposite face | Functional reporter, solubilization, membrane disruption, orientation markers, alternate acceptors | Readout competence and physical access are numerically distinct from the biological effect. Unknown labeling/vesicle-orientation fractions stay unknown. |
| Dose and compartment: exported TrxA | More protein monotonically increases function; same protein means same native task | Induction series, direct redox kinetics, corrected genotype and trapped partner intermediate | Dose-qualified signed effects and system correspondence are required. Identical protein sequence does not erase engineered compartment differences. |
| Timing and endpoints: YeaG | Early induction proves later survival; fast regrowth equals better survival | Early expression, 24-h recovery, 72-h-and-later CFU plus rescue | Different outcome signs across lag and survival are not failed replication. Time window belongs to the comparison identity. |

## Candidate numerical sheet, with explicit limits

These proposals are designed to be challenged by the cases. Native magnitudes alone are not a reliability score. Conversely, an ordinal judgment must not erase them. A result can fill numerical cells from literature or a dataset without giving a score to an entire paper.

| Cell | Proposed calculation / grading anchors | What it evaluates; counterexample |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **L, claim-relative link, 0–3** | 0: endpoint cannot discriminate this inference. 1: compatible association/proxy. 2: controlled perturbation establishes dependence/sufficiency while proposed mediation remains unresolved. 3: endpoint directly instantiates the precise inference and its entity/component is identified. Null if insufficient information. | A relation between measurement and a named emerged inference. PopZ SPR can give L=3 for binding, L=1 for SPR-alone native anchoring. A cell-rescue assay can give L=3 for rescue capacity without L=3 for catalytic mechanism. |
| **V, result/inference validity, 0–3** | 0: demonstrated compromising error/artifact for the inference. 1: identified critical confound remains untested or perturbation/readout competence is compromised. 2: appropriate controls support an interpretable endpoint, with component/mediator specificity unresolved. 3: endpoint identity plus a discriminating comparison with retained competence disfavor the named main alternatives in scope. Null when inspection is insufficient. | Validity depends on the assay and claim. The wrong TrxA strain genotype compromises the DsbB-independence inference. YeaG K116A at 64% WT abundance leaves a confound for catalytic necessity; WT-abundance K426A removes that abundance confound but not every folding/catalytic-specificity issue. |
| **Δ or ratio, signed native signal** | Matched treatment–control difference in native units; ratio=treatment/control only when denominator and scale are meaningful. Log ratio may be reported as a transparent transform, not a probability. No fold-change score for arbitrary-offset reporter scales. | Effect magnitude. The TrxA AP and OmpA endpoints yield different fold values and must stay separate. Concentration needed for matched endpoint cleavage is not kcat. |
| **U, uncertainty operands** | Retain SD, SEM, CI or fit uncertainty under its actual label, with n and hierarchy. CV=SD/mean only if the quantity is SD on a meaningful positive scale. A mean ratio has no manufactured CI when sample pairing/covariance is unknown. | Precision within an assay. YeaG's explicitly stated 36±2.3 SD-fold induction must not be relabeled SEM because the paper uses SEM elsewhere. PopZ Kd=20 nM with unreported uncertainty stays unqualified numerically. |
| **W, named-weakness outcome, −1/0/1/2** | −1: comparison reveals a compromising weakness. 0: required comparison untested. 1: tested but unresolved. 2: reported discriminating comparison disfavors that specific alternative. Store one cell per named alternative; null if source coverage cannot determine test status. | What complementarity actually repairs. DsbD membrane disruption and vesicle orientation address different alternatives. Repaired access does not add another transport rate. These codes are not summed or averaged. |
| **C, matched consistency counts** | For the same entity, endpoint, direction, intervention, system and relevant time/dose scope, retain numbers of supporting, opposing and inconclusive comparisons. Retain reported independent-experiment counts separately from established independent preparation/culture clusters. If identity or dependence is unknown, do not compute an effective-independent count. | Reproducibility at a defensible comparison level. YeaG faster regrowth/slower survival, YihI growth/GTPase, and FtsW access/polymerization are different endpoints, not opposing replicate signs. |
| **T, target-system compatibility, provisional 0–3** | 0: required target feature incompatible. 1: subject correspondence established but a required context differs or remains unresolved. 2: relevant determinants match with a scoped residual system difference. 3: required molecular/system features match the narrow claim. Null without target material/correspondence or sufficient context. | Transfer to the annotation target. Native TrxA sequence identity cannot by itself establish engineered periplasmic function in its natural cytoplasm. All actual annotation-target T cells here are **null** because no target material was supplied. This ordinal compression needs particular scrutiny. |
| **E, provisional ordinal inference tier** | **E=min(L,V)** when both are known; optional target-specific cap **min(E,T)** only if T is known. Missing cells leave the corresponding compilation unknown rather than zero. | A transparent, conservative compilation proposal across assay families. It makes a beautiful binding measurement weak evidence of activation without modifying the binding measurement. It is **not a settled experimental-reliability formula**, nor does it yet turn native signal/precision/consistency into a scalar. |

The control anchors are inference-relative assessments, not deterministic arithmetic on arbitrary metadata. For example, assigning V=3 requires reading a discriminating contrast and what it rules out; having three named controls is insufficient. These choices must be settled in part 2 before part 3's targeted readers/graders are specified. No reader code or independent generic reader design is supplied here.

The bottleneck candidate can underreward informative but indirect evidence and does not yet give a principled scalar bonus for replication or improved precision. That is a material open issue, not permission to make up weights. A reviewable next sheet should show E alongside native Δ/ratio, U, C and W, and explicitly decide whether calibration data justify any further compilation. If a single scalar is demanded, this lane does not claim to have validated such a scalar.

## Worked contrasts on common cells

Grades below are **provisional researcher applications of the proposed anchors**, not adopted scoring policy, confidence probabilities or grades of the papers. Fifteen explicit L/V/E entries are also retained in the six primary case rows. Target-system compilation remains null.

### 1. Same strong binding result, different inference: PopZ

[Results Fig. 3D and main SPR Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2745220/) report PopZ–ParB Kd **20 nM**. FtsZ and BSA controls, reverse immobilization, heterologous CFP/DivK controls and ParB/parS EMSA constrain different artifacts. Kd uncertainty and independent-preparation count remain unknown.

| Result and inference | Native cells | L | V | E=min(L,V) |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| SPR supports direct PopZ–ParB binding under tested conditions | Kd=20 nM; U=null | 3 | 3 | 3 |
| The same SPR alone establishes native origin anchoring | Same Kd=20 nM; U=null | 1 | 3 | 1 |
| Loss/addback plus origin labeling, dynamic imaging and elongated-cell controls establish PopZ dependence of stable polar origin residence | No numerical rescue fraction retained; 6-min imaging frames; origin/FROS controls | 3 | 3 | 3 |

The last row repairs cellular necessity and filamentation confounds; it does not retroactively turn affinity into an anchoring-force measurement. The imaging and binding data address distinct weaknesses and are not automatically independent replications. A broader claim about the complete force-bearing mechanism would still have lower L.

### 2. Large reporter effect, engineered context and dosage reversal: TrxA

[Fig. 1 and Tables 2–3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC94335/) support these exact arithmetic contrasts:

| Matched comparison | Native reported values | Transparent numerical cell | Uncertainty limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| dsbA AP, exported versus cytoplasmic TrxA | 80±8 SD versus 22±6 SD, reported AP activity units unspecified in inspected main Methods | ratio=80/22=**3.636**; difference=58; CVs=0.100 and 0.273 | Triplicate assay hierarchy unspecified; no ratio CI |
| dsbA OmpA oxidation half-time, cytoplasmic versus exported | 60 min versus 5 min | half-time acceleration=**12-fold** | No half-time CI or sample count stated in inspected account |
| AP at high versus intermediate induction | 95±12 SD at 200 µM IPTG versus 224±20 SD at 20 µM | ratio=95/224=**0.424**; difference=−129 | Cannot infer DsbB saturation probability or ratio CI |
| dsbB AP, exported versus cytoplasmic | 5±4 SD versus 20±8 SD | ratio=**0.25** in this background | Different genotype and redox context from dsbA comparison |

Direct OmpA oxidation with exported TrxA supports conditional oxidant activity, **L=3, V=3, E=3**. The specific explanation that high TrxA saturates DsbB and directly reduces AP remains a mechanistic interpretation, **L=2, V=2, E=2**. Native TrxA export is not established. The reconstructed dsbA/dsbB genotype and reducible TrxA–DsbB complex repair a named historical weakness; the original erroneous DsbB-independence comparison is not counted as a still-valid opposing biological replicate. Neither 3.636-fold AP nor 12-fold oxidation acceleration is averaged into the other endpoint.

### 3. Complementary experiment changes component attribution: FtsW

[2019 Figs. 1–2 and PGT Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6430707/) use **0.5 µM StFtsW, 1 µM bPBP and 10 µM Lipid II**, typically **30 min** for StFtsW. FtsW alone produces no detected polymer under tested conditions. Cognate bPBP restores polymer formation. FtsW catalytic-Asp substitution removes activity; TP-inactive bPBP retains stimulation. TM swaps and a compensatory interface substitution further localize the compatibility requirement. Figure 1d–e reports **three independent experiments**, 1f **two**; these do not supply product-amount dispersion or preparation lineage.

For the narrow inference that FtsW supplies polymerase catalysis in the tested complex, **L=3, V=3, E=3**. The catalytic and inactive-partner contrasts repair the weakness “the additional component might itself supply the measured chemistry.” Growth/depletion rescue adds physiological relevance and its own expression/partner limitations; it does not multiply the number of biochemical proofs.

The retained [2011 access study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3102273/) measured a different endpoint. This review does not newly grade its V, because it was not reread. Its access change cannot alone establish that FtsW is *exclusively* a physiological flippase (**L=1, V/E unknown here**). The 2019 paper expressly leaves additional flippase capacity formally open. A compile step must keep both results and constrain their inferences rather than call them discordant repeated polymerization assays.

### 4. Composite substrate: YbeY

[2013 Fig. 4B and main Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3570609/) combine reciprocal 30S/50S preparations at **3.8 µM each**, with the combined RNase assay using **5 µM YbeY and 5 µM RNase R**. Mutant 30S plus normal 50S yields susceptible 70S; normal 30S plus mutant 50S does not. Neither RNase alone nor isolated subunits give the same degradation behavior. Thus the **susceptibility determinant** maps to mutant-derived 30S within assembled 70S (**L=3, V=3, E=3**). The normal 50S partner can be degraded too; its destruction is not evidence that it began defective.

Figure 2's approximately **10,000-fold** mutant/wild-type concentration requirement for comparable RNA cleavage is an endpoint-matching concentration contrast, not a catalytic-rate ratio. The naked-RNA nuclease result alone does not establish the exact final physiological 16S cut (**L=1, V=2, E=1**). Subunit mixes share preparations; counting all lanes as independent replication would overstate precision. No raw band fractions or confidence bounds were reconstructed.

### 5. Strong induction is a weak survival inference: YeaG

[2015 Fig. 1A and Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4664914/) report **36±2.3 SD-fold** induction. That is strong expression evidence but only **L=1, V=2, E=1** for a survival-function inference. The physiological comparison shows deletion recovery lag **32 min shorter**, yet faster CFU decline becomes apparent after **72 h** of continued starvation. Wild-type addback repairs survival; this supports the narrower tested survival contribution, **L=3, V=3, E=3**.

Failed K116A rescue occurs at **64% WT abundance**: catalytic-necessity **L=2, V=1, E=1** because reduced abundance remains a confound. K426A at **100% WT abundance** supports STK-domain functional necessity with **L=2, V=2, E=2**; abundance is controlled, but an isolated catalytic-null/folding claim is not directly supplied by that number. **10,000 cytometry events per sample** are not 10,000 independent biological experiments. The lag/survival directions are different endpoints and time windows, not an inconsistency count.

## What compilation must preserve

1. Each numerical row belongs to one measured outcome and one inference. Keep high-quality binding and weak activity extrapolation as separate rows.
2. Complementarity updates a named weakness or supports a newly narrowed joint inference. It is not an additive control-point bonus. Reuse of a preparation, mutant panel or coupled reporter remains visible.
3. Compile **supporting and opposing same-scope results separately**, together with inconclusive results and provenance. Do not average away a negative or let a new different endpoint cancel a valid old measurement. A corrected genotype can invalidate an attribution without rewriting the activity that was recorded.
4. Match entity, intervention, endpoint, dose, time and relevant system features before computing consistency. “Different paper” is neither a necessary nor sufficient independence criterion.
5. Keep native signal, its precision and missing uncertainty separate from ordinal inference tiers. Unknown does not equal zero; no reported error bar does not mean zero error. A representative gel does not provide a product-rate estimate.
6. Keep assay target/construct correspondence and system constraints explicit. Compartment, membrane composition, partner compatibility and dosage can be decisive even when sequence is identical.
7. No scalar weight, confidence probability, citation/laboratory reputation weight or global annotation-credit change is adopted. The root review still needs to decide and show how signal, uncertainty and matched replication contribute to any requested combined reliability score. E alone is a deliberately limited stress-test candidate.
