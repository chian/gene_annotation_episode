# Additive qualification of research-work provenance

This qualifies the provenance wording in paper_01_nlpi_001.md, paper_02_rbkr_001.md and reconciliation_001.md; those files remain unchanged.

At startup, the source-discovery command printed the selected case_records.json entries for all eight assigned groups together. Those entries included retained experimental-observation summaries, not only source URLs. Although the command's purpose was an index inventory and no scores were derived from it, calling it an inventory does not establish compliance with the user's instruction to reset after each retained paper-specific packet. That initial multi-case output did not follow the required one-paper-at-a-time reset order. It must not be represented as fully compliant per-paper handling.

The subsequent detailed work was limited to two primary papers, NlpI and RbkR. Each was separately read, followed by the complete authority-set reread before its scoring summary. Their primary-review/reset provenance and the stated two-paper detailed coverage remain accurate. The other seed groups received no completed task-specific scoring interpretation, and their primary papers remain unreviewed in this assignment. The initial index observations were not used to assign numerical component values or to claim the absence of A105 binding experiments in those remaining papers.

The correction is to the scope of the compliance claim; it does not erase or retrospectively cure the initial ordering mistake. Future source inventories should print only identifiers/URLs, or treat any displayed experimental packet as a paper read and perform the full reset before the next packet. No new runtime gate, reader, scoring rule or production change is introduced.
