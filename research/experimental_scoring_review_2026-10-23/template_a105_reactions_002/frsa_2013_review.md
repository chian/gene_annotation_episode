## Definitions before comparison

The assayed molecule is recombinant wild-type *Vibrio vulnificus* FrsA expressed in *E. coli*, not an *E. coli* FrsA homolog. Preparation A uses the original authors' pQE-FrsA plasmid and JM109 F′ cells. Preparation B uses a codon-optimized insert, pET15b, BL21(DE3), and a cleaved N-terminal His tag. There is no stated mixture of a mutant FrsA and wild-type FrsA. Other proteins can still be present in a purification; their enzymatic contribution was not quantified.

For cross-paper replication the intended question is whether the reported pyruvate-to-acetaldehyde-plus-CO2 activity recurs. The intended variable is replication of the preparation/measurement, not a biological mutation. Host, construct, culture, purification, tag treatment, buffer and assay need separate comparison. A shared plasmid is unusually useful correspondence evidence, but it does not measure equality of protein state, contamination or activity.

For within-paper cross-method comparison, the intended changed variable is the detection method. Concentrations, duration, solvent and temperature also change and are nuisance differences, not evidence of three identical trials. The paper's computational barriers and structural occupancy are not measured reaction rates and are not A105 replicates.

## Proposed numerical cells and explicit combinations

The following calculations are proposals fitted to the user's scoring-template requirements. They are not formulas claimed by the paper.

**1. Experimental comparability outside the intended variable.** Define a reference rate response `g(c)` measured on the same molecule or an experimentally calibrated bridge under condition vector `c`: temperature, pH/pD, isotope solvent, salt, substrate and protein state. For assay `i`, `K_i = g(c_i)/g(c_0)` converts its rate to common conditions `c_0`. The reference rate must be measured, or its transfer uncertainty measured; a ratio of nominal temperatures or salt concentrations is not `K_i`. A multiparameter response surface is needed if effects interact. Do not automatically multiply independent pH, salt and temperature responses.

For a quantitative rate estimate, `a_i = (v_i-b_i-h_i)/(E_i*K_i)`, where `v_i` is total observed rate, `b_i` is matched nonenzymatic/assay background, `h_i` is independently measured contaminant activity, and `E_i` is assayed target-protein concentration. The uncertainty from the nuisance bridge belongs in `Var(a_i)`, using the full covariance when shared. A candidate *comparability precision multiplier* is `C_i = V_base/(V_base + V_bridge)`, where `V_base` is variance of the common-condition rate excluding the uncertain bridge, and `V_bridge` is the bridge's incremental variance. It evaluates information retained after nuisance uncertainty. Once the final variance includes `V_bridge`, do not multiply `C_i` again: the exact identity `C_i/V_base = 1/(V_base+V_bridge)` places it in a combined weight once. This is not a point reward for counting matched protocol fields. Here `K_i`, its variance and therefore `C_i` cannot be calculated.

**2. Isolation of the chemical effect.** For the coupled assay, a numerical spike-recovery cell would be `r_spike = observed NADH consumption / added acetaldehyde`, both in molar units, after matched blank correction and within the linear range. Its calibrated response and uncertainty enter the assay conversion. It does not establish that FrsA, rather than another catalyst, produced acetaldehyde. The separate activity fraction `I = (v-b-h)/(v-b)` would quantify that attribution only if `h` is independently measured under the same assay. Protein purity is not an estimate of `I`. Neither `r_spike` nor `I` is obtainable here. Do not correct for `h` and then multiply the corrected effect by `I` again.

**3. Nondetection with numerical meaning.** If the calibrated product-rate detection threshold is `L_i` and the matched rate uncertainty is `s_i`, a nondetection contributes the likelihood `Pr(Y_i<L_i | a) = Φ((L_i-E_i*K_i*a)/s_i)` under an explicitly justified Gaussian measurement model. Here `a` is a candidate per-enzyme common-condition rate, not a final annotation grade. If an endpoint product threshold `P_lim` is available and linear accumulation/recovery is demonstrated, `a_lim = P_lim/(E_i*t_i*K_i)` gives the corresponding rate bound. For the MS design the denominator is `36*K_i µM·s`; for NMR it is `27,000*K_i µM·s`. Because `L_i`, `s_i` and endpoint limits are absent, this paper alone cannot supply numerical upper rates or a calibrated contradiction score. Nondetection must not become an observed rate of exactly zero.

**4. Combining independent measurements.** Positive common-condition rate estimates can combine by `a_bar = (1ᵀΣ⁻¹a)/(1ᵀΣ⁻¹1)`, with variance `(1ᵀΣ⁻¹1)⁻¹`; `Σ` contains shared preparation, calibration and condition-bridge uncertainty. Their numerical consistency component is `(a-a_bar 1)ᵀΣ⁻¹(a-a_bar 1)`, with its degrees of freedom retained. For distinct independent preparations, detection and nondetection likelihoods multiply, and log likelihood ratios between explicit activity models sum. Orthogonal detectors on one preparation enter a joint likelihood, not three independent products. This gives explicit sums/products for a common scoring contribution without treating assay count as evidence strength. A bounded axis-score transform remains the parent's common-sheet choice; these inputs do not alone choose it.

## What would complete these cells

Needed: raw traces/blank distribution and calibrated thresholds; matrix-matched acetaldehyde and labeled-CO2 recovery series; a same-preparation split across detector methods; temperature/pD/salt response measurements or a calibrated matched reference; preparation-specific rates and purity/activity profiles. These measurements answer different weaknesses. A known ADH response does not validate MIMS transfer, and a crystallized fold does not validate activity sensitivity. Without them a numerical cross-paper discrepancy is unresolved, even though the independent failures and orthogonal readouts remain important scientific qualifications.

The reported preparation correspondence provides evidence about the actual experimental subject. It supplies no correspondence to the project's ybhP target, which remains unknown. Genetic growth, rescue and deletion conclusions are outside this A105 review.
