# PROPOSED A111 numerical scoring sheet

Status: a complete arithmetic proposal for the bounded structural-evidence measurements below, prepared for user judgment. It is not an accepted scoring policy, an implemented reader, a completed evidence axis, or a change to annotation credit. A105 remains first; 0/10 sheets are marked complete. Investigation prompt text, this numerical sheet, and future functions that populate its exact cells remain three separate pieces.

**Goal:** calculate how much experimentally observed occupancy, contacts, coordination or architecture in an actual measured molecule supports a specified structural feature of the supplied target, through an explicit feature correspondence. The observable and its transfer form one evidence chain. The proposal returns a signed contribution, supporting and opposing components, a nonnegative support coordinate, and uncertainty/missingness. Every arithmetic step is defined here. W05 would separately decide how an accepted coordinate enters the unchanged global credit system.

Input can be sequence, a selected file record/region, or snippets. No initial annotation or public name is required. The investigator can first establish physical features and subsequently ask which emerging functional interpretations they constrain. A physical feature shared by two interpretations supports both equally; it is not independent proof that one interpretation is correct.

## One fillable numerical table

One row is one prespecified feature question, one measured source state, and one source-to-target mapping alternative. An example feature is “six oxygen donors within 2.6 Å of the modeled Mn site in this construct.” A different question is “physiological Mg-dependent catalysis”: the former cannot silently be relabeled as the latter. Supporting provenance identifies exact material, construct, state, record, atom selection, expected feature, experiment, correspondence and source; those labels are not numerical grades.

| Numerical cell | Inputs and calculation | Units / range | Meaning and missingness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `q` occupancy/exposure fraction | The reported refined site occupancy, or number of observed eligible objects with the feature divided by all eligible objects. For a specifically present-state geometric statement, `q=1` is the mathematical conditioning on that resolved state, not estimated solution occupancy. | fraction [0,1] | Actual fraction represented by the observation. `fixed_model`, `refined`, `counted`, and `conditioned_present_state` remain distinct provenance. A fixed model occupancy can produce a model-conditional index but does not establish a measured occupancy or its uncertainty. |
| `D` local observation/model agreement | For the populated diffraction examples, `r=min(max(0,RSCC_j))` over the exact atoms/residues needed to establish this feature, then `D=r²`. Values above 1 are invalid, not silently fixed. | [0,1] | Local fit index. Taking the minimum uses the weaker endpoint once. Squaring is an explicit proposed fit transformation, not a probability calibration. A missing required endpoint RSCC leaves D unknown; unrelated residues do not create requirements. |
| Native validation diagnostics | RSCC, RSR, bond/angle Z and RMSZ, clash distances, refinement restraints, resolution, Rwork/Rfree, model/sequence coverage, and actual atom occupancies as available. | native units | Numeric diagnostic cells remain available for judging the selected feature and constructing its uncertainty set. They are not averaged into a universal structure grade. Resolution, `1−Rfree`, pLDDT/100 and a ligand name never substitute for D or q. |
| `G` geometric / stoichiometric agreement | `G=(Σ_j a_j)/J`. Each `a_j` is the measured satisfaction, 0 or 1, of an explicitly declared distance interval [L,U] in Å, angular interval in degrees, stoichiometry interval in subunits, or topology/identity constraint. J is the fixed number of relevant constraints. For a genuinely fractional constraint, the numerator and denominator are the observed compatible objects and total eligible objects. | [0,1] | Counts the share of the declared physical feature actually present. A distance predicate is not an affinity. Bounds define the physical question; they are not unreported measurement errors. Unmodeled/uncertain atoms give unknown predicates, not failures. A known incompatible atom/stoichiometry gives 0. |
| `T` target correspondence | `T=(Σ_k p_k)/K`, over K required source-to-target molecular roles. `p_k=1` when an explicitly specified role predicate is met by the actual mapping, `0` when a known mapped target fails it, and unknown when its mapping or required chemistry is unknown. Structure-based roles may be measured distance/angle/placement predicates under a retained superposition. Sequence-based roles may require a specified chemically necessary atom set at a retained alignment position. | [0,1] | Feature coverage and preservation conditional on the actual correspondence, not a generic homology grade. K is fixed even for snippets/partial sites. Ambiguous alignments produce alternative T values and an envelope. Native alignment scores/E-values, local posterior values, RMSD/TM-score and coverage remain traceable, but a whole A101/A102 score is never multiplied as another proof. |
| `C` experimental comparability | For the declared n nuisance variables excluding the intentional difference: `C=(Π_k c_k)^(1/n)`. Continuous conditions use `c_k=exp[−(x_A−x_B)²/(2τ_k²)]`, with matching units and an explicit τ. Chemically matched construct positions use `c_k=n_matching/n_union` after excluding the intended change. Exactly identical documented values give 1. | [0,1] | Similarities/differences of compared conditions. For a single-state proposition with no cross-condition comparison, the empty product is 1 by definition. It does not certify physiological relevance. Unknown declared nuisance values produce an interval; adding irrelevant matching metadata is prohibited. τ values must be supplied explicitly as measurement-based scales or labeled proposal assumptions. |
| `I` isolated attributed fraction | For an observed signal/effect magnitude `Y>0` and a measured upper bound B on contributions other than the intended component/change, `I=max(0,1−B/Y)`. At Y=0 the isolated positive-effect fraction is 0. | [0,1] | Separates assignment of the outcome from matching conditions. For a between-state structural effect, Y can be an absolute measured distance/occupancy difference with B in the same units. For a pooled feature population, Y and B can be object counts or attributed signal units. B unknown means I unknown, even if C=1. For a strictly named within-file geometric fact under its stated model assignment, B=0 is an algebraic condition on that fact, not evidence of ligand identity in solution. |
| `N` nondetection contradiction fraction | If a hypothesis entails occupancy ≥q_req>0 under these conditions, and calibrated detection gives an upper occupancy limit q_U, `N=max(0,1−q_U/q_req)`. | [0,1] | Quantifies only the gap below that explicitly entailed minimum. Unreported ligand, absence of a modeled atom, or “no density” without a calibrated upper bound gives N unknown. A detection limit above q_req gives N=0, not support. |
| `w` observed chain contribution | For detected supporting features, `w=q D G T C I`. For incompatible observed features, the same product is applied to a declared incompatible-feature predicate. For calibrated nondetection, replace q with N; G records whether the required state/feature location was actually examined. | [0,1] | Each factor has a physical operand and a declared denominator. These are dependent components of one chain, not independent probabilities or multiple experiments. |

The `q=1` conditioning and `C=I=1` exact single-state definitions are allowed only when the question literally names that measured/model-assigned state and its atoms. They cannot be used to claim a native substrate, solution oligomer, binding affinity, physiological metal, catalytic event, causal mutation effect, or organism-level relevance. For those broader questions the added identity/condition/attribution operands must be populated or remain unknown. This changes the question's numerical completeness, not whether an episode may proceed.

The RSCC transform is the concrete local-fit conversion used by this proposal. It retains the original score and its limits. A correlation near 1 can coexist with a wrong chemical assignment, overfitting or shared model bias. Unknown alternate identities do not become mandatory gates: a narrowly stated model-supported feature remains reportable, while identity-specific discrimination remains unknown. Countermodels with alternate ligand identities, anomalous scattering, chemical analysis or perturbation measurements can constrain identity. Such complementary experiments do not become independent repetitions of the same diffraction dataset.

## Complete combination across observations

For one physical feature family f, declare a finite list of elementary questions with nonnegative weights summing to 1. Default is equal weights among the originally declared questions. Six donor-distance predicates inside one coordination feature do not become six separately weighted families. Splitting/renaming a question conserves its original weight; changing the scientific scope creates a new sheet revision rather than repricing saved history.

1. **Representations:** paper text, table, PDB model and validation derived from one dataset reference one canonical observation. Do not sum them. Conflicting numbers from these representations remain separately attributed versions until an explicit reconciliation selects a value/range; neither mean nor latest-wins is automatic.
2. **Model copies:** average equivalent asymmetric-unit/symmetry-copy values within that dataset. Retain their range. An unsampled copy is not omitted from a predefined denominator; its cell is unknown. Symmetry copies are not independent experimental n.
3. **Dependence clusters:** shared raw measurements, alternate refinements of the same data, and demonstrably shared model/template dependence are grouped. Within each cluster, take the mean over its unique eligible observation/state rows, with equal weights unless a fixed sampling fraction supplies a measured alternative. Extra representations have zero extra weight. Preserve numbers of raw datasets and dependence clusters separately.
4. **Independent experiments:** take the equal-weight mean over the distinct experimental dependence clusters relevant to the same physical question. No citation, lab-name, publication-age, atom-count or paper-count bonus is used. Replication informs variation and precision, rather than multiplying the mean merely because an observation was repeated.

Let `h_e` be +1 for a declared supporting physical event or −1 for a declared incompatible event/calibrated nondetection. Its sign comes from the measurement's relation to the explicit predicate, never from a reader's confidence adjective. Let a cluster's positive and negative means be `P_fc=mean(max(h_e w_e,0))` and `O_fc=mean(max(−h_e w_e,0))`. With M_f clusters:

\[
P_f=\frac{1}{M_f}\sum_c P_{fc},\qquad
O_f=\frac{1}{M_f}\sum_c O_{fc},\qquad
Z_f=P_f-O_f.
\]

These means have the same complete denominator, including eligible contradictory observations. Missing observations are intervals, not silently dropped and not assigned zero. Truly nonexistent experiments do not enter M_f. This is a mean of **available experimentally observed support**, not a posterior probability or a population effect estimate. A weaker independent repetition can lower the mean; a contradictory experiment can make it negative.

For F declared feature families, weights `α_f≥0`, `Σα_f=1` (default `1/F`):

\[
P_{111}=\sum_f\alpha_fP_f,\quad
O_{111}=\sum_f\alpha_fO_f,\quad
Z_{111}=P_{111}-O_{111},\quad
A_{111}=\max(0,Z_{111}).
\]

`Z111∈[−1,1]` is the signed structural-chain contribution; `A111∈[0,1]` is the proposed nonnegative annotation-support coordinate. Opposition remains separately returned and is never discarded from the record. The optional display scale is `100×A111`; it adds no evidence. This is the explicitly defined whole-axis reducer for this proposal, **not** a replacement global hypervolume. No scientific acceptance, stopping threshold or minimum number of observations is defined.

For competing interpretations H1 and H2, compute the same sheet on the same observations and feature weights under each interpretation's explicit expected physical predicates. Report `ΔZ=Z(H1)−Z(H2)`, with shared inputs retained. Shared ATP-site occupancy contributes the same value to two hypotheses that both expect it and gives ΔZ=0. An unexamined alternative has a null ΔZ, not an arbitrary 0.5 or a veto on the measured physical feature. A contact or soaked analogue alone supplies no catalytic rate or ligand-affinity score.

## Uncertainty, partial sites and zero

Each native number has `(value, lower, upper, uncertainty_kind, units, source)`. Uncertainty kinds are `reported_CI`, `reported_SE_or_SD`, `reported_range`, `digitization_bound`, `model_ensemble_range`, `logical_bound`, or `unreported`. Do not convert an SD into a CI without n/design; a B factor is not automatically a coordinate SE. Fixed occupancy 1 without uncertainty retains the deposited-model value but its population occupancy remains unreported. A conditional model calculation and an unconditional experimental contribution are separately labeled; the latter must not silently inherit the former's point value.

For nonnegative products, lower and upper endpoints multiply. For a signed opposing contribution, `[wL,wU]` becomes `[−wU,−wL]`. Sums/means propagate endpoints with the fixed denominators. `A111` bounds are `max(0,ZL), max(0,ZU)`. Unknown fraction factors use logical bounds [0,1] with null point value. Unknown direction has [−wU,wU], null point. These are conservative sensitivity envelopes, not statistical confidence intervals. Correlated/shared variables are varied once jointly when their dependency is known; ordinary interval arithmetic may be conservative, but must not claim independence.

For K required target roles, let c be proven preserved, d proven incompatible, and u unresolved, with K=c+d+u. Then `T∈[c/K,(c+u)/K]`; nominal T is null if u>0 unless a clearly labeled conditional scenario is printed. For an uncertain geometric value interval, a predicate is 1 if the whole interval satisfies the criterion, 0 if none does, and [0,1] if it crosses a boundary. Alternative alignments, atom assignments and structures are scored separately and their min/max retained; do not pick only the best. A full-length protein's missing unrelated loop is not automatically a local-site penalty.

For n≥2 genuinely independent cluster values of one question, report their sample SD, range and `SE=SD/sqrt(n)` as descriptive repeat variation. n=1 gives unknown SD/SE. Shared crystal copies do not increase n. This variation is not multiplied into w again, since doing so would duplicate evidence used by the mean. Different endpoints or conditions are not “inconsistent repeats” merely because their numbers differ; their corresponding C/I and scope determine whether they belong in the same comparison.

Zero is used only for a known failed role/predicate, calibrated fully incompatible observation, computed zero isolated fraction, or a derived numerical limit. An unreported value, unseen density, omitted model atom, unresolved mapping, failed extraction and an out-of-scope measurement remain distinct null reasons. A confirmed absence can lower support and still advance investigation progress; the sheet does not redefine progress or local continuation.

## Bounded coverage and choices offered for review

The populated native method is X-ray diffraction model/map evidence. Its predicates also support native measured distances, counts, interfaces, stoichiometries and state comparisons once the relevant observation support is supplied. Cryo-EM, NMR, cross-linking, tomography and solution mass/stoichiometry are not falsely validated by these X-ray examples. A reader may preserve their native values, but a method-specific D definition is needed before calculating a complete scalar for those methods. That extension is outside this bounded proposal, and is not masked by D=1 or a universal resolution grade.

Within the bounded method all formulas and defaults are explicit. The scientific choices still requiring user judgment are: the usefulness of RSCC² as a local fit index; equal means rather than evidence accumulation across independent experiments; equal feature-family weights; product combination; the constraint definitions/denominators; and the chosen nuisance scales in any given comparison. These are proposed policies awaiting judgment, **not missing arithmetic**. They have not been calibrated to correctness of annotations. Actual missing measurements remain null, including target mapping in both published examples. The complete synthetic example fills every operand without pretending to annotate the project's target.
