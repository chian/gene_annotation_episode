## Source acquisition and actual inspection

The RCSB core API was accessed through the installed RCSB PDB skill's `rest_request.py`. Raw PDB coordinate and wwPDB validation files are retained in `sources/`. The author/repository PDF copies are source evidence, not new studies. Unsuccessful access attempts included publisher/repository URLs returning denials or timeouts; they are not claimed as inspected papers. The successful Fuhrmann repository file has a repository cover and eight publication pages. The successful Zarembinski retained paper has five pages.

Full initial authority reading preceded source work. Each paper's full authority reread, corrections, active goal and latest instructions were read again before conversion. Receipts are `receipts/MJ0577_reset_001.json` and `receipts/Fuhrmann2013_reset_001.json`; they list full authority paths, hashes, line counts and inspected/missing coverage. Fuhrmann paper-text, selected-category and authority-read truncations were explicitly repaired; no truncated output is passed off as a complete read.

## Additive interpretation correction to the first packet

`MJ0577_conversion_001.md` preserves its original local ATP fit example. The number .9225665 is the **mean ATP local fit index conditional on the deposited model**, with its two copies averaged. It must not be read as a complete six-donor coordination reliability score: all required water/metal/contact endpoint support was not populated. Gdistance and Gangle are separately calculated geometric cells. The final sheet requires the actual endpoint support appropriate to the selected question. This additive clarification leaves the earlier packet and raw facts intact.

The first packet's coordinate occupancy 1 similarly remains a model parameter. It is not a claimed measured population occupancy or a calibrated probability. Its conditional target envelope does not remove the source's unreported uncertainty. These clarifications apply to all printed examples and supersede any broader interpretation of that preliminary number.
