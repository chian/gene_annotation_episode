# Additive qualifications to numerical draft 001

These qualifications apply to NUMERICAL_SHEET_001.md without changing its
permanent text or either source-conversion record.

1. **Joint operating conditions.** Section 3's product of requirement indicators
   is valid only when each indicator is conditional on the relevant background,
   or the complete joint operating domain has been demonstrated/modelled with
   explicit support. Separate one-variable experiments do not automatically
   establish every corner of their Cartesian product. If a joint combination
   is untested and an interaction could change the endpoint, mark it unresolved.
   The synthetic section explicitly stipulates a jointly supported rectangle
   for its invented endpoint; it does not infer that rectangle from either paper.

2. **Function scope and rarity.** The sample denominator is conditional on the
   biological situation named by the proposed role. An inflammation-specific
   role is evaluated among the specified inflamed situations, not penalized
   because an organism also spends time without inflammation. A rarely used
   but useful function can be plausible. A low unconditional encounter fraction
   by itself is not evidence that the annotation is wrong. The scope must be
   fixed from the candidate's meaning and recorded before scoring, not shrunk
   retrospectively to remove unfavorable samples. Both the conditional result
   and the observed frequency of that situation may be reported separately;
   they are not automatically multiplied. This draft scores compatibility in
   the stated context and does not assess the evolutionary value of rare use.

3. **Supported domain versus absence of contradiction.** A domain can be
   demonstrated at discrete operating points. Interpolation between points
   requires an explicit supported response model. Outside that support is
   unknown unless a negative observation or a necessary physical constraint
   supports the contradicted domain. The Huc [40,3000] ppbv interpolation is
   specifically the proposed monotone-depletion interpretation, not an exact
   continuous set of directly sampled rate measurements. Its 100-point result
   is conditional on that interpretation and matched assay context. Removing
   interpolation leaves the benchmark membership unresolved if the relevant
   operating point cannot otherwise be supported. The tetrathionate 1.32
   concentration ratio supplies no such interpolation or numerical point score.

4. **Prediction versus observation.** A clade-wide trait is not automatically an
   observation of the supplied target. A valid scoped transfer relationship is
   needed; otherwise the target-state operand is unknown. Clade membership is
   never multiplied as a second tree/sequence score. The current draft has no
   calibrated numerical mapping for an unconstrained verbal plausibility
   judgment and does not pretend that one is needed whenever direct inputs are
   merely missing. Genuinely unmeasurable narrowly defined judgments would need
   their own explicit referent and criteria in a later additive proposal.

5. **Extent of the proposal.** The present formula is complete for context
   compatibility with stated necessary requirements and a defined opportunity
   denominator, including partial-identification bounds. It is not a universal
   numerical solution to every biological-plausibility argument. A claim about
   adaptation, rare evolutionary benefit or a causal physiological mechanism
   cannot be relabeled as a fraction of compatible exposures to force a score.
   The broad A108 axis remains uncompleted, pending user review and eventual
   reader/prompt/credit work. The deliberately bounded numerical sheet and its
   demonstrated arithmetic are the deliverable of this assignment.
