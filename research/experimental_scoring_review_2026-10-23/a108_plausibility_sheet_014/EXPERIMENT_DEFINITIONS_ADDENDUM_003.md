# Additive experimental-definition qualifications

This supplements the two conversion records and numerical draft, preserving
their original wording.

For Winter 2010, the organism is *Salmonella enterica* serovar Typhimurium.
Methods Summary specifies 10–12-week-old female mice, including wild-type
C57BL/6 and Cybb-deficient mice, with the mouse samples collected four days
after infection. Figure 1E's `ttr` strain designation must not be silently
equated to a particular lesion from the inaccessible strain table. The reported
2.5 mM sufficient-dose experiment is a broth co-culture of wild type and ttrA
mutant, distinct from the infection groups and from the 40 mM mucin experiment.
The exact independent n for the 2.5 mM dose result was not established from the
inspected main text. Figure 1F's n=3 cannot be borrowed for it.

The graphical tetrathionate axis is labeled mM and the article attributes it to
cecal contents; Methods Summary describes LC-MS measurement of cecal extracts.
The extraction/dilution recovery mapping to original cecal fluid was not read.
Consequently the 1.32 comparison is a **nominal reported-concentration ratio**,
not a validated local exposure ratio. The full environmental sheet must retain
the specimen/assay-volume basis and any missing extraction correction. Neither
the graph-derived number nor its visual reading interval resolves that gap.

For Huc, the four independent preparations and technical triplicates describe
gas-consumption replication. They do not provide four environmental soil
samples. The tagged Huc production background and isolated protein are not a
measurement of the supplied target's organism. No general transfer of that
operating range to an arbitrary target is established here.
