## Experimental definitions and native numbers

S. enterica YjgF, renamed RidA, is tested together with IlvA, which generates a short-lived enamine/imine from threonine. IlvA and RidA were separately produced with C-terminal His6 tags in E. coli BL21AI and purified by nickel affinity. They coexist as successive catalysts in the positive reaction; the comparator contains IlvA without RidA. Adding RidA is the intended assay variable. The products are 2-ketobutyrate and ammonia, but the quantitative readouts inspected measure 2-ketobutyrate or trapping of the intermediate, not a direct ammonia calibration curve. Spontaneous hydrolysis also produces 2-ketobutyrate, so product identity alone does not identify how much flux RidA carried.

Continuous assays used 0.9 µM IlvA and, where present,1.6 µM RidA, stated as monomer concentrations,10 µM PLP,200 µl volume,22°C. RidA is trimeric and IlvA tetrameric: the nominal oligomer ratio calculated from those stated concentrations is (1.6/3)/(0.9/4)=2.37037; the paper describes it as approximately2:1. Do not silently equate monomer concentration with oligomer concentration or assert that all oligomers are catalytically active. At15 mM threonine, RidA approximately doubled the initial 2-ketobutyrate rate at pH7.5 and increased it4–5-fold at pH9.5. These are reported approximate fold effects, not transcribed absolute rates with errors.

The assay uses A230 for keto-acid formation. At pH≤9, rates were measured over0–30 seconds; above pH9 they were measured over30–60 seconds for BOTH comparator and RidA conditions because IlvA alone showed a30-second burst. Across pH, both buffer mixtures and time windows change. Within a fixed pH comparison, the same window is used. This distinction matters to comparability.

Product TLC and mass spectrometry after2 hours gave one radiolabeled product matching 2-ketobutyrate. Expected m/z101.0245 versus observed101.0248 yields a calculated absolute relative mass error2.96958 ppm. It is a native identity-agreement number, not a probability of chemical identity. Two derivatization assays gave comparable increased product formation; their raw quantitative agreement was not reported in the inspected prose.

Ferricyanide reacts1:1 with an available enamine/imine, diverting it to a non-ketobutyrate product. At10 mM threonine and5 mM ferricyanide, IlvA alone reduced>98% of ferricyanide within1 hour. With RidA, only24% was reduced after2 hours. In a separate endpoint product-accounting experiment at the same nominal doses, the paper reports approximately5 mM ferricyanide reduced plus5 mM 2-ketobutyrate without RidA; with RidA,>90% substrate was recovered as2-ketobutyrate and about10% through the trapping branch. The sum of measured products was approximately10 mM across conditions. Do not force the24% time-course result to equal the separate approximately10%-of-threonine endpoint:24% of5 mM is1.2 mM, not exactly1 mM. Their precision and preparation relationship must be retained.

Figure2 reports SEM of two replicates, representative of more than five independent experiments; Figure3 reports SD of two replicates and two experiments; Figure4 reports SD of two replicates with nine time-course and four endpoint experiments. These are the source's experiment counts, not independently established protein-preparation counts. Figure7's serine reaction is a distinct substrate endpoint: at30 mM serine and pH9.5 the pyruvate rate increased2.2-fold. Diverse homolog assays include50%,56%,42%,45% and29% sequence identity subjects, measured individually with IlvA; those identities do not become universal transfer probabilities.

## User-template calculations: comparability separate from isolation

**Comparability.** For the plus/minus-RidA assay, RidA presence is intentional and is excluded from nuisance comparison. Keep IlvA active amount, substrate, pH, buffer, PLP, temperature and time window comparable. Across pH, pH is intentional but buffer composition and the change in measurement window need separate assessment. The overlapping buffer ranges around pH8.3–8.6 can help estimate a buffer response at a fixed pH if actual rates are extracted; a ratio of pH values is not a biochemical response factor. Proposed residual comparability component for a nuisance response g is `C_g=min[g(x_a),g(x_b)]/max[g(x_a),g(x_b)]`. Combine only separable effects; otherwise use a joint model. Rates are needed to fill this, not merely names of buffers.

**Isolation.** The native fold increase `v_with/v_without` describes the observed coupled system. It does not equal an intrinsic RidA rate divided by spontaneous hydrolysis. Removing the intermediate may relieve inhibition of IlvA and increase its production rate; the source's burst behavior is consistent with that possibility. An instantaneous mass balance is `dI/dt = v_IlvA − v_spontaneous − v_RidA − v_trap`, and `v_ketobutyrate = v_spontaneous + v_RidA`, where I is intermediate concentration. A proper isolation calculation requires intermediate concentration/time and generator flux or a validated model. The simple difference `v_with−v_without` is a controlled total-system effect, not automatically v_RidA. This stays an A105 reaction-assay limitation; genetic perturbation/restoration scores are not imported.

The trapping experiment adds a second route to measuring the intermediate fate, and product accounting constrains lost or unmeasured chemistry. It does not alone identify separate enamine and imine specificity or establish unchanged generator flux at every time.

## Concrete proposed numerical cells and combination

1. **Measured product accounting.** With corrected molar amounts, define `B=(n_ketobutyrate+n_trap_product_equivalent)/n_threonine_consumed`, where the trapped amount is inferred from ferricyanide reduction only under its established1:1 stoichiometry. The native no-RidA example gives approximately(5+5)/10≈1. The proposal `R_balance=min(B,1/B)` for positive B measures closure of the declared molecular account. Actual uncertainty and unmeasured intermediates remain essential. Near-complete closure does not prove which protein catalyzed the flux. This is validation of the molecular assay within A105, not a separate pathway/resource-completeness sheet.

2. **Flux partition, not a confidence grade.** Define `F_K=n_ketobutyrate/(n_ketobutyrate+n_trap_equivalent)`. The reported endpoint is approximately0.5 without RidA and>0.9 with RidA. `F_K_with−F_K_without` is therefore approximately>0.4, preserving the roughness of the input. It describes measured redistribution into the keto-acid route; it is not an isolated RidA flux fraction. `1−n_reduced_with/n_reduced_without` describes reduced accessibility to the chemical trap, again not a fraction of total chemistry definitively attributable to RidA. Do not multiply F_K, its difference and the trap-suppression ratio as three independent supports for the same experiment.

3. **Net-effect precision.** If the scoped quantity is `Δv=v_with−v_without`, then `Var(Δv)=Var(v_with)+Var(v_without)−2Cov(v_with,v_without)`. Proposed `R_Δ=1/[1+Var(Δv)/Δv²]` converts relative uncertainty for positive Δv. For a fold effect use log-ratio variance instead; these are alternative representations of the same contrast and must not both multiply the score. Raw uncertainties and common-batch covariance are missing, so no actual R_Δ is filled. The replicate counts alone cannot supply it.

4. **Readout agreement.** Under matched reaction conditions and common molar normalization, proposed `A_method=min(v_A230,v_derivatization)/max(v_A230,v_derivatization)` quantifies agreement between direct spectral and derivatization rates. Joint fitting with their covariance is preferable when they share samples or calibration. The paper says comparable rates but does not supply the required pair numerically in the inspected text; leave A_method unfilled. Multiple readouts can improve identification or calibration of the same reaction without increasing independent-preparation n.

5. **Analytical recovery and protein attribution.** A held-out standard supplies `R_cal=min(measured/known,known/measured)` after correction. A matched contaminant flux estimate supplies `I_protein=1−v_contaminant/v_net`. To claim a numerical fraction carried through RidA rather than spontaneous chemistry, use `I_route=∫v_RidA dt/∫v_ketobutyrate dt` from the validated mass-balance model. Do not set I_route to0.5 because the total rate doubles. The source's measurements constrain this model but do not fill its individual fluxes here.

An explicit candidate compilation for the demonstrated coupled-system reaction effect is `E_system=R_cal×R_Δ×C×R_balance`, with R_balance used only if its calibration information is distinct and its chemistry applies to the same measurement. A narrower molecular-attribution claim additionally needs a separately measured I_protein and, when using total product as a proxy for RidA flux, I_route. Do not insert a categorical “coupled assay” multiplier. Alternatively, a fully corrected direct RidA-rate estimate with its propagated variance can replace the proxy calculation; do not charge the same loss twice.

Synthetic worked illustration: held-out recovery0.98, independent product closure B0.97, positive controlled-effect relative SE0.10, nuisance comparability C1, and independently measured attribution I0.85 give `0.98×0.97×[1/1.01]×1×0.85=0.8000099`. These numbers are invented; the native paper contains approximate closure, not this0.97 measurement or an0.85 attribution. It shows exactly where a measured molecular account and an attribution estimate would combine, without manufacturing them.

Remaining inputs: actual absolute curves and variance, active enzyme amounts, same-window comparisons, quantitative response/recovery of each readout, generator flux, intermediate concentrations and decay, impurity activity, preparation identities, and the relationship to any supplied annotation target. No target evidence or transfer probability is invented. Strong mass agreement and endpoint closure cannot repair an unidentified flux attribution by themselves.
