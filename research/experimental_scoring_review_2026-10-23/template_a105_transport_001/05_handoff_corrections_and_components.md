## A concrete compilation proposal, within the requested sheet

For one clearly defined positive transport observation, the research proposal is:

`Q_observation = 100 × M_comparison × F_isolation × C_calibration × P_precision × D_specificity`.

This is a proposed unitless score scale, not a probability. The multipliers are individually calculated quantities, not ordinal grades. D is used only for a specific comparative inference such as fluoride rather than chloride; absence of a tested alternative must not silently become D=1. A simple existence-of-movement inference needs its own declared compilation without a substrate-discrimination factor. Unavailable relevant operands remain unknown.

- **Experimental comparability M:** first define the intended difference. Fit or use a justified response model for nuisance variables alone, then compare predicted opportunities while holding the intended difference fixed: `M=min(g(x_A),g(x_B))/max(g(x_A),g(x_B))`. Here x contains only measured non-intended differences and g predicts their effect on the readout. For an unwanted difference in functional membrane protein count, g(N)=N is justified only if the readout is linear in independently active molecules. For substrate concentration, g(S)=S/(Km+S) requires an applicable Km; nominal concentration ratios are insufficient near saturation. If direct normalization removes the nuisance and propagates its uncertainty, do not also apply M for that same corrected effect. None of these four studies supplies every nuisance operand for a completed comparison score.
- **Isolation F:** after a specified matched instrument/empty-system blank, let J be remaining observed movement-like signal and B the independently measured residual contribution from nontransport processes such as leakage or surface binding. `F=(J−B)/J` describes the fraction attributable to the specified movement process only when these contributions are additive, nonnegative and independently distinguishable. Shared uncertainty must be propagated. It is not a protein-purity ratio and not the fraction computed from a gene deletion's outcome. A blank already removed from J must not be removed again as B. Negative/incompatible estimates require their signed values and uncertainty, not silent clipping into an apparently valid score. No actual F was established in these reviews.
- **Calibration C:** a proposed symmetric recovery component `C=min(r,1/r)` uses r=measured standard/known standard, with positive r. It measures departure from unit recovery; it does not replace a calibration curve, detection limit or uncertainty. If the same recovery bias has already been corrected in J, represent its residual uncertainty instead of also applying a bias penalty. The actual FucP counting efficiency and YjeH extraction recovery were not extracted.
- **Precision P:** `P=J²/(J²+u²)` is a bounded proposed signal-to-uncertainty transformation for a specified sign and endpoint. Carry J's sign separately so a precise negative result does not become strong positive support. For an estimate across independent preparations, u must reflect its actual uncertainty model. For example, independent variance contributions can combine as `u²=u_within²+u_between²+u_calibration²`; correlated inputs instead require the covariance terms. A repeat SD and a standard error of a mean are different operands. The YjeH value above is explicitly repeat precision; unknown independence prevents relabeling its SD/√3 as a valid independent-preparation standard error. Do not multiply an additional CV penalty from the same SD.
- **Specificity D:** for a physically comparable ratio r of the tested activity/permeability to the named alternative, `D=(r−1)/(r+1)` preserves direction and gives zero at equal measured contributions. The Fluc r bound illustrates an actual populated value. It does not justify comparing whole-cell rates across different loading protocols, substrates, denominators or detection ranges. A nondetection needs an upper bound, not zero or an infinite ratio.

Independent preparation counts belong in the uncertainty calculation and provenance, not as a separate reward that double counts the same repeated measurements. Complementary readouts can resolve B, recovery, gradients or orientation; they do not automatically add independent observations to a score. The assay-reliability compilation above still requires a separately worked target-relevance calculation. No target sequence, structural correspondence or interchangeable target system was supplied for these cases, and no ybhP transfer score has been invented. An unspecified target-relevance multiplier remains unfinished work rather than a fixed categorical fallback.

## Additive corrections and completion of illustrative operands

1. In note 02, the mixed real/synthetic Fluc demonstration was rounded as 90.9755. The arithmetic is `100×0.95×0.98×(1.8²/(1.8²+0.1²))×(99/101)=90.9756466108`. It should read a bound greater than approximately 90.9756 under those synthetic assumptions, not an actual Fluc overall score. Current=1.8 pA and the source's r>approximately100 are real; residual contribution, uncertainty and calibration assumptions are synthetic.
2. Note 04's synthetic m=0.9 was stated as an assumed output without numerical model inputs. To make that illustrative operand reviewable, use **synthetic** independent functional membrane protein counts N_A=90 and N_B=100, with identical per-molecule transport, gradient and substrate opportunity and a linear independent-molecule model. Then `g(N)=N`, `m=90/100=0.9`. This is an unwanted abundance difference in a hypothetical comparison whose intended variable is something else. It is **not** the real induced/uninduced FucP comparison, where altered abundance may be part of the intended induction effect. Continuing the other synthetic operands in note 04 gives `100×0.9×0.98×0.9×400/404=78.5940594059`; none is a FucP score.
3. Note 04's physical radiotracer conversion is dpm per pmol. Its count-slope notation distinguishes counting time from incubation time: CPM is count rate measured by the counter; the slope is change in that CPM across uptake incubation minutes. Without measured counting efficiency and blank control, CPM is not itself pmol transported.
