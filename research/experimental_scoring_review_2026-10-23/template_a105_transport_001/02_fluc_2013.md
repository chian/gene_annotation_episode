## Definition

Ec2 is an *E. coli* virulence-plasmid Fluc (YP_001481330.1), not automatically the K-12 chromosomal protein; Bpe is a *Bordetella pertussis* homolog. Synthetic pASK constructs were expressed in *E. coli* BL21-DE3, purified, and reconstituted in defined membranes. Direct transport experiments compare fluoride and chloride in the same assay design, or protein-containing versus protein-free membranes. The intended difference is permeant ion or presence of purified protein; lipids, vesicle geometry, electrochemical driving force, channel number and electrode response are other relevant quantities. The cell-free movement/readout is A105. Cellular fluoride-resistance deletion/restoration claims are A106 and are not scored here.

Fluoride and chloride coexist in the vesicle assay: liposomes contain 150 mM of each potassium salt. Valinomycin permits counterion movement. Separate ion-selective electrodes report extracellular ions; detergent release defines the remaining trapped pool. Under Poisson dilution 30–50% of liposomes lack protein. Thus incomplete release before detergent can be intended protein occupancy, not leak or inactive protein. Random orientation is measured in Bpe topology experiments, but passive bidirectional channel activity must not be multiplied by an assumed 0.5 orientation penalty.

The manuscript compares a tenfold concentration ratio (300/30 mM) with an eightfold ionic-activity ratio. Those are not interchangeable. Gradient agreement belongs to experimental validity through an explicitly computed Nernst expectation with ion activities, temperature and junction correction. The electrode junction correction was <3 mV. Calculating a score from measured-minus-expected reversal requires uncertainty in both measured and expected values; rounding both to 53 mV does not justify a perfect score.

### Comparability is separate from isolation

For fluoride/chloride selectivity, electrochemical driving force is normalized into permeability. A raw current ratio at unequal driving force would mix permeability with conditions. Let `g_j=I_j/(V−E_j)` where an ohmic relation is established. Then compare `g_F/g_Cl`, with uncertainty and limits; do not also multiply by a driving-force mismatch penalty for the same corrected difference. The Goldman-type permeability comparison in the paper likewise needs its actual ion activities and membrane assumptions. Measured vesicle-size distributions, channel loading and background leak can quantify remaining non-target differences. Equal recipes alone do not establish a numerical comparability score.

Isolation asks how much current/ion release remains assignable to this protein and ion after nonspecific leak and cross-reactivity. The source reports no electrode cross-reactivity under the used conditions and no unitary current without reconstituted protein. Those are useful controls, but absent numerical leak/recovery/error data are not an invented isolation fraction of 1. A proposed cell is `f_specific=max(0,ΔI−I_residual)/ΔI`, using protein-minus-blank current ΔI and a distinct residual off-target-current estimate. Do not subtract the blank twice. Fluoride-specific release plus selective reversal and discrete unitary events address different weaknesses: membrane leak, ion identity and mechanism. Shared preparations make independence an additional question.

### Explicit proposed compilation

For the particular *fluoride-selective channel* claim, extend the component proposal in note 01 as `Q=100 × m_nonintended × f_specific × c_cal × q_signal × d`. Here `m_nonintended` is a measured residual experimental-balance component after physical normalization; `c_cal=min(r_cal,1/r_cal)` uses actual known-standard recovery; `q_signal=ΔI²/(ΔI²+SE_ΔI²)` uses a positive specified current contrast with its signed value preserved. All operands are quantitative; none is an ordinal quality grade. For mere fluoride movement, chloride discrimination would not be required in the same way: claim scope changes what is being evaluated, not the measurements.

Actual d is bounded near 0.9802, but the other measured operands required for an actual aggregate were not extracted. An actual Q is therefore unknown. **Mixed actual/synthetic arithmetic demonstration:** retain the reported 1.8 pA amplitude and r>~100 bound, but **invent** residual current 0.09 pA, SE=0.1 pA, calibration 0.98 and perfect residual matching for illustration only. This gives f_specific=0.95, q_signal=3.24/3.25=0.996923 and Q>~90.9755. This is not a paper-derived total. The invented quantities are exactly the data needed before such a total can be claimed. A target-relatedness component still requires the actual supplied target relationship; none is assigned for ybhP.

The source's discussion combines bulk-flux and electrical bounds to argue much greater selectivity. Those measurements differ in driving force and protocol. This review uses the within-method ~100 permeability lower bound for the worked conversion rather than silently treating all turnover values as a same-condition ratio. A non-detection remains an upper bound, never an exact zero or infinite selectivity.
