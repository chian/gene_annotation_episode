Donor: *Rhodobacter capsulatus* SB1003. Assay host: *E. coli* S1039, reported genotype `birB13(Ts) Δbio-61 bioP98 (up promoter) recA1 thi rpsL λb515 b519 galQ6 red-270 cI857`, lacking intrinsic high-affinity biotin uptake, with pFDX500. The proteins compared are BioY alone and BioMNY expressed from related plasmids in separate cells, not two alternative BioY variants mixed in one cell. BioM/BioN coexist with BioY in the full-system condition by design. BioY has a C-terminal FLAG epitope and the native TTG start was changed to ATG after initial negative expression results. Empty-vector and BioMN-only cells supply background comparisons. Molecular uptake measurements are used here; the causal interpretation of subunit deletion or BioM K42N belongs to A106.

Intended variable: transporter composition. Shared protocol: 37 °C, pH 7 phosphate/glucose uptake medium, approximately OD578 0.2, radiolabeled biotin, filtration/washing and scintillation. Actual cell-by-cell protein abundance, membrane incorporation, accessible area, cell volume and energy state are not quantified. The measured endpoint is cell-retained label after washing; surface binding, metabolism/trapping and residual host uptake are possible contributors unless separately resolved.

## Conversion into the user's numerical sheet

**Endpoint relevance and signal.** For a Michaelis–Menten description, `f(S)=S/(K+S)` is the fraction of the fitted maximum attained at concentration S. With the reported rounded parameters at 4 nM, BioY gives `4/(250+4)=0.01575`; BioMNY gives approximately `4/(5+4)=0.44444`. These are calculated operating fractions, not measured new points, not uncertainty bounds and not annotation scores. Their ratio is approximately 28.22. The actual rate ratio also requires `Vmax_MNY/Vmax_Y`; it cannot be equated with 28.22. This is why a long accumulation endpoint cannot score high-affinity initial uptake merely because accumulation is large. A low fraction of Vmax is not poor evidence when the question is whether movement occurs at low substrate.

**Comparability, excluding the intended change.** A useful candidate cell is the balance of measured transport opportunity, not a count of protocol fields that match. For unequal quantities of membrane-accessible BioY, let `N_A` and `N_B` be measured membrane molecules per mg total protein. Proposed abundance-balance component `m_N=min(N_A,N_B)/max(N_A,N_B)` is a continuous 0–1 measure of this particular unwanted difference. It is unknown here. It is not applied to BioM/BioN because their addition is the intended variable. Equivalent components could use independently measured membrane area or viable-cell normalization when those affect the assay, but only with an explicit response model; pH units must not be ratio-scored. If amounts can be corrected directly, first calculate per-molecule rates and carry amount uncertainty, rather than penalizing abundance again. Matched recipes establish the design, not measured equivalence.

**Isolation of the measured effect.** For one concentration and common linear interval, `ΔJ=J_construct−J_empty` removes measured host/filter background. If a second control estimates a remaining nonspecific contribution `B_remaining` within that excess, proposed isolation fraction is `f_specific=max(0, ΔJ−B_remaining)/ΔJ`, for positive ΔJ. This is unknown, not 1, when retained label has not been separated into internal transport versus surface binding/trapping. Empty-vector subtraction and this remaining-background estimate must refer to different contributions; subtracting the same blank twice is wrong. Uptake/wash chase, internal-versus-external label discrimination and purified membrane reconstitution could address these limits. They need not count as independent replications of the same rate.

**Validity, uncertainty and consistency.** Convert counts into pmol with an actual tracer/recovery calibration; preserve recovery uncertainty. A proposed calibration component is `c_cal=min(r,1/r)` for positive recovery ratio `r=measured_standard/known_standard`. Lack of a reported calibration leaves this unknown. Obtain each preparation's initial slope from its actual time series, then calculate the mean net slope and variance across independent preparations. A proposed signal component `q_signal=ΔJ²/(ΔJ²+SE_ΔJ²)` states signal size relative to its uncertainty; it is not a probability that the annotation is correct. Preserve signed ΔJ separately; the squared expression is used only for a specified positive-transport contribution. Between-preparation inconsistency enters `SE_ΔJ` or an explicitly estimated heterogeneity term, not an additional multiplication of the same CV. Independent assay count alone cannot fill SE.

**Explicit proposed compilation.** For review, `Q_assay=100 × m_N × f_specific × c_cal × q_signal` combines measured comparability, isolation, calibration and signal/uncertainty contributions. The factor 100 is a presentation scale, not a measurement. A missing applicable operand leaves Q unknown; absent raw data are not a fixed half-score. This is a proposed experimental contribution, not the final annotation score. A target-relevance calculation would additionally need measured molecular correspondence and the relevant system/condition relationship; none is provided for ybhP, and no universal transfer constant is supplied.

**Synthetic arithmetic demonstration, not BioY data:** N_A=80 and N_B=100 membrane molecules per common normalization give m_N=0.8. Construct flux 10 and empty flux 2 pmol min^-1 mg^-1 give ΔJ=8. A distinct residual contributor of 1 gives f_specific=7/8=0.875. Calibration 9.8/10 gives c_cal=0.98. SE_ΔJ=1 gives q_signal=64/65=0.984615. Then Q_assay=`100×0.8×0.875×0.98×64/65=67.5446`. These are four explicitly scoped measured contributions in the example; none is an ordinal overall grade. Direct correction of abundance would require a differently specified calculation, not both correction and this balance multiplier. If the residual contributor is unmeasured, this demonstration cannot produce an actual BioY score.

The paper supplies kinetic parameters and a useful endpoint contrast, but lacks the calibration, independent-preparation slopes/errors, membrane protein quantities and readout partition needed to fill that proposed score. Its strongest lesson for the sheet is that kinetic operating context and endpoint validity must be explicit before amplitude is transformed. Do not sum Fig. 2 accumulation, Fig. 3 fits and Fig. 4 bars as independent evidence units; their constructs/readouts overlap.
