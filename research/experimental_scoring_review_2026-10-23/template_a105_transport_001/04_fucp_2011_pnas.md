# FucP: a radiotracer slope requires recovery and cell normalization

Source: Stahl et al., 2011, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1014125108 and https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3084102/. The retained corpus/lane packet identifies this and a second FucP paper. ## Experimental definition

The native system is *Campylobacter jejuni* NCTC11168. Whole-cell radiolabeled fucose uptake was measured in wild type and separately cultured insertion mutants, with native and heterologous complementation experiments. These are not mixtures of wild-type and mutant transporter proteins in one cell. Gene perturbation, complementation, growth and colonization conclusions are reserved for A106; A105 receives the direct uptake measurements and their measurement controls. The fact that complemented transcription is reported below 11% of wild type is RNA abundance, not measured membrane transporter abundance.

Cells were grown to exponential phase with or without 20 mM fucose induction, washed three times, and resuspended at OD600=1 in 50 mM Tris-HCl pH 7.5, 50 mM NaCl and 10 mM KCl. Transport used 20 µM fucose, specific radioactivity 40 mCi/mmol, at 37 °C. One-ml aliquots were collected between 30 seconds and 10 minutes on 0.22-µm filters and washed with 5 ml of 0.1 M LiCl. Fig. 2's caption defines initial velocity from the linear first 120 seconds. Methods say experiments were in triplicate; biological preparation independence is not established by that phrase. The figure shows SD, but numerical bars and their uncertainty were not extracted.

The reported approximately threefold induced/uninduced uptake is a condition-specific rate ratio, not evidence reliability. The stated mutant uptake is less than 0.2 in the source's printed pmol/min/10^9-CFU-type units; exponent formatting is ambiguous in the extracted narrative, so exact denominator typography must be verified from the figure before machine use. It is a bound, not zero. This perturbation contrast is not used to manufacture an isolated molecular fraction for A105.

## Quantitative sheet contributions

**Signal conversion.** The physical conversion is `40 mCi/mmol × 2.22×10^12 disintegrations/min/Ci = 88.8 disintegrations/min/pmol`. For net filter-associated count slope `b` in counts/min of counting time per minute of incubation, counting efficiency `η` (counts/disintegration), and a measured cell quantity `n` in units of 10^9 CFU, the normalized rate is `J=b/(88.8 η n)` pmol per incubation minute per 10^9 CFU. Blank counts must be subtracted before fitting or with an explicitly propagated blank slope and covariance. The assay's stated specific activity is actual; `b`, η and the CFU conversion are not supplied numerically in the text read. OD is not automatically viable CFU.

An explicitly **synthetic** arithmetic illustration uses a net slope of 888 counts/min per incubation minute, η=0.5 and n=1: `J=888/(88.8×0.5×1)=20 pmol/min/10^9 CFU`. This is not a paper rate. It shows which missing measurements would populate the numerical cell.

**Time/readout comparability.** The first-120-second slope and a ten-minute accumulated count answer different questions. They do not produce a `120/600=0.2` reliability score. A reader must fit the specified initial linear window, quantify slope uncertainty, and check departures from linearity using the actual time points. Induction at 20 mM before washing and uptake at 20 µM are different assay stages; their 1,000-fold concentration difference is not an uncontrolled comparison between transport arms.

**Comparability distinct from isolation.** Fucose induction is the intended condition difference. Differences in starting viable cell numbers, available fucose, temperature, buffer or filtration recovery are nuisance differences. A normalization to measured CFU and recovery can directly remove a measured nuisance; the same nuisance should not then receive another penalty. For remaining differences, a proposed similarity operand `m= min(J_pred,A,J_pred,B)/max(J_pred,A,J_pred,B)` compares predicted transport opportunity due only to nuisance conditions, holding the intended induction change fixed. This requires a response model, not ratios of arbitrary metadata. No suitable model is supplied here. Isolation instead asks how much retained radiolabel represents internalized fucose rather than filter retention, surface binding or downstream retained metabolites. Washes help, but do not numerically partition these contributors.

**Proposed compilation.** One possible A105 assay contribution is `Q=100 × m × c_recovery × f_internal × q_slope`. Here `c_recovery=min(r,1/r)` uses a spike-recovery ratio r; `f_internal=(J_retained−B_noninternal)/J_retained` uses an independently measured remaining noninternal contribution; and `q_slope=J²/(J²+u_J²)` uses propagated rate uncertainty u_J. Bounds and signed rates must be retained, and this transformation is not an annotation probability. Each is a proposed calculation, not an adopted score or a categorical grade. Actual count slopes, recovery, noninternal background and rate uncertainty were not numerically extracted, so the real total is unknown.

A **synthetic-only** filled calculation continuing the example above, with m=0.9 from a specified nuisance-response calculation, r=0.98, B_noninternal=2 and u_J=2 in the same units, gives `100×0.9×0.98×(18/20)×400/404=78.5941`. The 0.9 is an assumed output of that nuisance model, not an experimentally grounded FucP comparability value; it is included solely to expose how the arithmetic would compile. A corrected numerical example must supply the nuisance measurements/model before using m with evidence.

Cell-free filter blanks, rapid surface-label displacement or compartment separation, chemical speciation of internal label, calibrated counts/quench recovery, and time-resolved CFU normalization address different weaknesses. They are complementary controls, not automatically independent transport replications. The source's 37.2% sequence identity to *E. coli* FucP does not become a 0.372 transfer weight; it is one native measurement that requires alignment coverage and relevant-site/architecture correspondence. No relationship to the project's target sequence is fabricated. Heterologous growth/acidification and animal colonization remain A106, not flux observations.
