    Crystallization and Data Collection.


      The crystals of cytosine deaminase from Escherichia coli complexed with Zn2+ were grown by

 hanging drop vapor diffusion at room temperature. The protein solution contained wild-type CDA

 (18 mg/mL) in 50 mM Tris (pH 7.5), and 1 mM ZnCl2; the precipitant contained 35% pentaerythritol

 propoxylate, 0.05 M Hepes (pH 7.5), 0.2 M potassium chloride, and 1 mM ZnCl2. The crystals of the

 enzyme appeared in 3-4 days and exhibited diffraction consistent with the space group R32, with one

 molecule of the protein per asymmetric unit (Table S1).

    Isoguanine was insoluble in the number of different solvents; therefore, the powder of the

isoguanine was used directly for soaking experiments. Crystals containing CDA.Zn2+ were soaked for

6 days in mother liquor and excess of the isoguanine powder. Prior to data collection, crystals were

transferred to cryoprotectant solutions composed of their mother liquids supplemented with 20%

glycerol. After incubation for ~10 s, the crystals were flash-cooled in a nitrogen stream. Diffraction

data were collected at the NSLS X4A beamline (Brookhaven National Laboratory) on an ADSC CCD

detector. Diffraction intensities were integrated and scaled with programs DENZO and SCALEPACK

(1). The data collection statistics are given in Table S1.

   Structure Determination and Model Refinement.


    The structure of CDA crystalline complex was determined by molecular replacement with the

fully automated molecular replacement pipeline BALBES (2), using only input amplitudes, sequence

data and the E. coli CDA (PDB code: 1K6W) as the search model. A partially refined structure of

CDA complex was the output from BALBES and several iterative cycles of refinement were

performed for the CDA ternary complex, including model rebuilding with COOT (3), refinement

with PHENIX (4), and automatic model rebuilding with ARP (5). Final crystallographic refinement

statistics for the CDA·Zn2+·Isoguanine complex are provided in the Table S1.


    The model of the CDA·Zn2+·Isoguanine complex was refined at 2.25 Å with an Rcryst of 0.207

and an Rfree of 0.243. The final structure contains protein residues 4-425, two well-defined Zn2+ ions,

and one isoguanine molecule bound in the CDA active site. The first Zn2+ ion is bound in the active

site and the second Zn2+ is bound between two symmetry related molecules.
