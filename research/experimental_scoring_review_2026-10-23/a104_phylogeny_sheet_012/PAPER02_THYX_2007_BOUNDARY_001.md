# Paper 02: ThyX seed does not provide an A104 tree measurement

Source: Leduc et al. (2007), *Flavin-Dependent Thymidylate Synthase ThyX Activity:
Implications for the Folate Cycle in Bacteria*, Journal of Bacteriology
189:8537–8545, [10.1128/JB.01380-07](https://doi.org/10.1128/JB.01380-07),
[PMC primary](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2168944/).
Full retained main text including Methods, Results, all legends and references
was read in ranges 1–600, 601–1150 and 1151–1535 of
`thyx_primary_text_001.txt`. The supplement was not inspected. No prior scoring
packet was read or reused.

The experimental comparison is native *R. capsulatus* ThyX versus loss of ThyX
and heterologous replacement with *R. sphaeroides* ThyA, with or without FolA.
The compared enzymes perform a related endpoint by different mechanisms; the
paper expressly studies nonorthologous replacement. Those comparisons cannot
be treated as tips in a common ThyA/ThyX sequence tree merely because both make
thymidylate. WT ThyX is absent in the disrupted strain, rather than coexisting
with a mutated native copy. The ThyA-plus-FolA comparison deliberately changes
two proteins. Growth includes all cellular contributors; DNA labeling is a
separate measured outcome. These definitions prevent an A106 rescue result from
being redescribed as a phylogenetic placement score.

The liquid minimal-medium Results give doubling times 103 min (WT), 108 min
(ThyA plus FolA) and 310 min (ThyA alone). Since growth rate is ln(2)/doubling
time, the derived relative rates are 103/108 = 0.9537037 and 103/310 = 0.3322581.
Those arithmetic examples are intentionally EXCLUDED from A104; they concern
experimental replacement, and are not branch lengths, clade support or
organism-tree concordance. The percentages of genome occurrence in the paper
are also excluded from this axis. They do not measure tree agreement.

This is a useful contrasting failure case for the numerical sheet: a strong
experimental functional argument does not populate phylogeny cells. The
investigation can retain the functional knowledge without awarding invented
A104 credit. Missing tree data are missing information, not evidence that the
function is wrong. No genetic-code assumptions or scientific computations were
made; only arithmetic for the explicitly excluded example was checked.
