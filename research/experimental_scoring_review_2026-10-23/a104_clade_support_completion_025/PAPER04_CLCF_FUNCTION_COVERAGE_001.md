# Paper 04: characterized natural CLC fluoride-clade members

[Stockbridge et al. (2012)](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1210896109), primary
Results, Table 1, Methods and figure captions inspected through PMC/PNAS.
Fig. 1 reports the CLC-F clade; the family tree used PhyML after alignment of
transmembrane regions. An independent organismal tree was not supplied here.

Of 29 screened clade members, eight purified, separately reconstituted natural
homologs exhibited fluoride flux and fluoride-driven proton uptake. Table 1's
efflux half-times are 25,7,4,12,8,5,30,28 seconds. Assays used 10 µg protein/mg
lipid and 300 mM cation; cpi/cdi used sodium, the others potassium. Repeats are
mostly duplicate determinations, not independent homologs. NMR/light scattering
on the same liposome batch are complementary readouts. Four additional proteins
were inactive under tested conditions; expression and purification failures
also occurred. Those outcomes do not establish another substrate function.
The exact 1:1 exchange stoichiometry was measured for CLC-eca alone.
