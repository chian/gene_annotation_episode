## What was measured, and what this can score

The protein was produced in E. coli; ATP was not added during purification or
crystallization. MnCl2 was present in crystallization conditions. The crystal
contains two protein copies, each with ATP and a modeled Mn site. The paper's
final resolution is 1.7 Angstrom. These are experimental definitions, not
quality grades or evidence for transfer to an unspecified target protein.

Deposited ATP local real-space correlation coefficients are 0.963 and 0.958.
The proposed positive squared-correlation conversion gives 0.927369 and
0.917764; their copy mean is **0.9225665**. Averaging these crystal copies does
not manufacture two independent experiments. Deposited average occupancy is
one for both ATP models; that establishes the model's occupancy parameter,
not a measured probability that ATP occupies an unrelated protein in solution.

The corresponding proposed local-geometry cells can be computed directly from
the deposited coordinates. The independent parent script checks six oxygen
neighbors around each Mn and the example predicates: donor distances in
[2.0,2.6] Angstrom and pair angles within 15 degrees of 90 or 180 degrees.
Those tolerance choices are explicitly proposed geometry definitions. They
are not reported experimental uncertainties or universal Mn criteria.

**0.9225665 is only the ATP-local-fit component.** A claim about all six
coordination donors requires the local-fit information for those donors too.
It cannot use ATP fit as a substitute. The whole target contribution also
requires the actual source-to-target feature mapping, scope, experimental
comparability and effect isolation appropriate to the claim. These operands
are not populated by crystal resolution or by the protein name.

The paper's separate ATP hydrolysis experiment is not counted in A111 merely
because it occurs in the same article. Its molecular-activity measurements
belong to A105. A crystal interface is likewise not independent proof of
solution oligomerization without the necessary observation.
