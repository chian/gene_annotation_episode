## Experimental definitions and actual numbers

The protein is E. coli IscR. WT and S40A/Y41A/E43A/Q44A/R59A proteins were
overexpressed separately in derivatives of BL21(DE3) ΔhimA::tet ΔiscR::kan and
purified anaerobically. WT and mutant are compared in separate assay tubes.
Anaerobic isolates retained partial cluster occupancy: 40–50% WT and 27–50%
mutants in purification Methods; the binding section gives 27–56% across
anaerobically isolated proteins. Do not silently replace these ranges with
complete holo occupancy. Apo protein was generated with 100-fold EDTA and
10-fold dithionite excess until A420 stopped falling, followed by ion exchange
to remove EDTA. Different state-preparation histories can create nuisance
effects even when sequence is identical.

Binding assays used 5 nM 30-bp Texas Red-labelled dsDNA, either hya type 2 or
iscRB type 1 sequence, 40 mM Tris pH 7.9, 150 mM KCl, 5% glycerol, 10 µM DTT,
12 minutes and anaerobic handling. Increasing protein generated polarization/
anisotropy curves; concentration basis is protein input, and a four-parameter
Hill fit supplied apparent affinity, slope and maximum anisotropy.

These are the source's Kd-labelled apparent midpoints. Table 2 alone does not
uniquely specify every variant's cluster state; retain that unresolved linkage
until the supplementary curve-to-preparation mapping is inspected. Table 4
states that purified proteins were incubated anaerobically, which is also not
a claim of 100% holo protein. Do not substitute the triple-cysteine,
His-tagged crystallography constructs for these binding preparations.

The source defines table errors as standard deviations derived from fitting the
mean of at least three independent binding experiments. They are not automatically
standard errors across independent protein preparations. Maximum anisotropy is
about 0.18–0.21; variant-specific values and the free endpoint belong in the
signal model. The methods acknowledge Hill slopes above 2 have unclear physical
meaning and an effect of monomer–dimer equilibrium cannot be excluded. Slope
3.7 is not a directly measured 3.7-protomer complex.

Competition explicitly compares WT **apo** IscR and **apo** E43A. Each has
5 nM labelled WT hya DNA and 8–1000 nM unlabelled competitor; WT protein is
22 nM and E43A is 20 nM. Buffer uses **250 mM** KCl, not the 150 mM of direct
titration. The intended variables are protein substitution and DNA base identity;
the deliberately different protein dose is an additional comparison nuisance.
The endpoint is competitor concentration reducing the measured binding signal
by one half, fitted by a four-parameter logistic curve.

## Numerical components and explicit combination

**Affinity and signal.** The directly derived E43A/WT hya midpoint ratio is
22/17 = 1.294118. The paper's "% WT binding activity" is a transformed inverse
affinity: 100×17/22 = 77.2727%, rounded in the table to 80%. It is not the
percentage of active protein, the fraction of DNA occupied at every concentration,
or an independent datum. S40A gives 170/17 = 10-fold weaker apparent affinity.
The proposed sheet keeps Kapp, its uncertainty meaning and the exact curve
response instead of turning these into a quality grade.

**Specificity separate from affinity.** Let `s_p,j=ln(IC50_p,j/IC50_p,WTsite)`
for protein p and competitor j. A useful contrast is
`Δs_j=s_WT,j−s_E43A,j`, a difference of log selectivity ratios. For 7C→G,

`exp(Δs_7G) > (510/32)/(17/11) = 10.3125`,

so `Δs_7G>2.333356751` from central values and censoring alone. For 8C→G the
ratio is >36.339286. These are derived lower bounds before uncertainty, not
confidence bounds, and do not treat censored endpoints as exact. Similar
affinity to WT hya can coexist with a large change in discrimination. A score
for "binds this DNA" cannot stand in for a score for "selectively recognizes
the CC motif".

**Comparability excluding intended variables.** Equal labelled DNA and common
buffer reduce some nuisance differences. The 22 versus 20 nM protein doses
cannot be assigned a 20/22 comparability multiplier without a response model.
A joint competition model with measured active concentrations can predict
`b_cmp = expected Δs from dose difference alone`; subtract it from Δs and
propagate its uncertainty. To compare direct titration with competition, model
the 150 versus 250 mM salt response as well. Without this model those assays
remain different endpoint/condition records, not inconsistent repeated Kd values.

**Isolation of the measured effect.** WT and E43A do not coexist. Each apo
preparation is treated by the same cluster-removal protocol, improving this
comparison's molecular definition. Residual cluster, reagent, inactive fraction
and altered oligomerization are specific unresolved contributors. For the
partially occupied anaerobic preparations, cluster fraction is an input to a
mixture model, never a blanket reliability multiplier. The structural evidence
can motivate which residues matter, but it does not supply a missing active
concentration or convert occupancy into a binding curve.

**Validity, uncertainty and consistency.** Fit all contrasts from shared raw
data when available. For `Δs=ln C_WT,j−ln C_WT,ref−ln C_E43A,j+ln C_E43A,ref`,
let coefficient vector a=(1,−1,−1,1); its variance is `a'Σa`, using the joint
log-IC50 covariance. Across DNA substitutions the WT reference and E43A reference
are reused, so contrasts are correlated. The lower-bounded measurements need
censored likelihood terms, for example `1−F(bound | model parameters)`, not
unweighted substitution of 510/740/1000. The three occasions do not automatically
become three protein-preparation replications or a threefold score multiplier.

**Proposed compilation within the user's template.** For this defined motif
discrimination claim, combine measured and correction components as

`d_target = Δs−b_cmp−b_other+b_correspondence`,

`V_total = V_measurement+V_cmp+V_other+V_correspondence+2Σcovariance`,

`E = d_target/sqrt(V_total)` for fully observed approximately Gaussian contrast
estimates. With censored terms, use the joint likelihood instead of manufacturing
a finite E. b_cmp represents condition/dose differences; b_other represents
signal from unintended contributors; b_correspondence represents a calibrated
change in the predicted endpoint between studied and target systems. Those are
distinct numerical operands. Exact matching can justify algebraic cancellation
of a shared nuisance, but missing information cannot be declared zero.

This proposal provides explicit sums and a normalized evidence component; it
does not settle the common annotation-credit conversion. A joint model may
instead compile log likelihood ratios by summing only independent experiment
blocks, with covariance/latent shared controls within each block. Existing Kapp,
derived % activity and Δs may not all be multiplied as independent proof.

**Target/system relevance.** Mapping the protein and DNA recognition features,
demonstrated interchangeability or other measured correspondence can inform
target predictions. The result warns against using broad sequence similarity
or similar affinity alone to infer unchanged recognition specificity. No
relationship to ybhP or native repression is established by this conversion.
