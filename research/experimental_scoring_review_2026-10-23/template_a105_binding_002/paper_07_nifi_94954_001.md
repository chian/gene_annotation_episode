# NifI source boundary — A105 binding conversion 007

Kessler, Daniel and Leigh, *Ammonia Switch-Off of Nitrogen Fixation in the
Methanogenic Archaeon Methanococcus maripaludis: Mechanistic Features and
Requirement for the Novel GlnB Homologues, NifI1 and NifI2*, J Bacteriol
183:882–889 (2001), DOI 10.1128/JB.183.3.882-889.2001,
[PMC94954](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC94954/).
Primary Methods, Results and discussion of mechanistic alternatives were
inspected. Parsed text is retained as `nifi_94954_primary.txt`.

## What was actually compared

The organism is Methanococcus maripaludis strain LL and derivatives. Mm53 is
the nifI-positive control carrying the puromycin-resistance marker near the
nif cluster; Mm54 lacks both nifI genes, Mm55 lacks nifI2, and Mm56 lacks nifI1.
Mm70 is a merodiploid combining ΔnifI1/nifI2+ and ΔnifI2/nifI1+ constructs.
Thus complementation restores the two intact genes in one cell together with
the corresponding deletion alleles. These are whole-cell genetic arrangements,
not separately purified WT/mutant binding reactions or a measured mixed-protein
binding composition.

The main endpoint is acetylene reduction during ammonia addition/removal,
reported as accumulated ethylene percentage:

`100 × amount C2H4 / (amount C2H2 + amount C2H4)`.

That is a nitrogenase activity readout in whole cultures. It is not the fraction
of NifI bound to nitrogenase, a binding affinity, or a direct reaction catalyzed
by NifI. Other measurements are NifH Western mobility/abundance, nif mRNA and
growth. The intended variables are gene deletion/complementation and nitrogen
regime; reporter kinetics, culture state and assay conditions are additional
differences. The source itself notes slightly different assay conditions may
explain apparent reduced ammonia sensitivity in the complemented strain.

No direct NifI–nitrogenase, NifI1–NifI2 or NifI–effector binding assay was found
in this paper. Its discussion explicitly retains noncovalent association as one
possible mechanism alongside covalent-modification alternatives. The mention of
2-ketoglutarate binding to bacterial PII in the introduction cites other work
and is not a measurement of these archaeal NifI proteins. The absence of a NifH
mobility change also does not measure absence or presence of a NifI complex.

## Consequence for the numerical template

For this assigned **binding** branch, affinity, occupancy, stoichiometry,
specificity, active protein concentration, binding signal and binding uncertainty
remain **not measured in this source**. They are not zero. A lack of a binding
experiment is distinct from an assay nondetection with an established detection
limit. There is no binding numerator/denominator or uncertainty to fabricate.

Experimental comparability and effect isolation are separable questions for
the whole-cell perturbation experiments, but those calculations belong to A106
under the current assignment. An organism-level activity change cannot be
converted into a numerical binding-isolation factor, even with complementation.
The paper supplies no quantitative model connecting the recorded whole-cell
activity to the amount of a specified molecular complex.

The proposed binding compilation therefore adds **no binding experiment block**
from this paper. In a sum `S_binding=Σ_e log(L_e(H)/L_e(H0))`, this source
contributes no term because the binding data e do not exist. The resulting zero
increment of an empty sum is an accounting identity for no new binding evidence,
not a measured zero binding magnitude, a negative result, or a confidence score.
Missing binding cells remain null with the exact reason. No generic 0/1 paper
quality category is introduced.

A future direct binding dataset would need defined interacting molecules,
active concentrations, assay-specific binding response and matched reference,
background contributors, a valid calibration/response model, replicate units
and a quantitative target correspondence. Such data could populate the same
continuous signal, comparability, isolation and uncertainty calculations as the
other binding cases. This paper alone cannot supply those missing operands.
The present review does not assert that later publications lack such evidence.
