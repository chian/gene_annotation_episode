# PopZ–ParB binding: A105 component conversion

Research proposal, 2026-10-23. No production scoring policy is installed. Source:
Bowman et al., *A Polymeric Protein Anchors the Chromosomal Origin/ParB Complex
at a Bacterial Cell Pole*, Cell 134:945–955 (2008), DOI
[10.1016/j.cell.2008.07.015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2008.07.015),
[PMC2745220](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2745220/).

## Experimental definitions before scoring

The assayed molecules are *Caulobacter crescentus* PopZ and ParB. PopZ was
expressed in *E. coli* with a six-histidine tag, purified on cobalt resin,
cleaved with thrombin and further separated by gel filtration. Concentration
was measured by BCA. The main text does not establish active binding-site
concentration or the concentration convention for its oligomeric PopZ.
ParB is covalently attached to a CM5 SPR surface by amine chemistry; PopZ is
the soluble analyte. These two different proteins coexist during binding.
There is no WT/mutant mixture in this pair experiment.

Figure 3D changes soluble protein identity and concentration: PopZ, ParB,
FtsZ or BSA at 125, 250 and 500 nM, against immobilized ParB. The endpoint is
surface-associated mass over time, in SPR response units, not a catalytic
rate, fractional cellular localization or necessarily monovalent occupancy.
The controls test nonspecific surface/protein association. Their molecular
masses differ, so equal molarity does not imply equal response units per bound
molecule. The displayed curves have a 180-second association phase.

The reported equilibrium dissociation constant is **20 nM**. No uncertainty,
independent-preparation count, fit residual, active-fraction correction,
surface loading or model specification was recovered from the main text.
Crucially, the screening SPR buffer is 50 mM HEPES pH 7.2, 50 mM KCl and
5 mM MgCl2, whereas the affinity experiment uses 50 mM sodium phosphate
pH 8.0, 25 mM NaCl, 5 mM MgCl2 and 0.1 mg/ml BSA, with 75 microliters at
30 microliters/min and a 240-second dissociation phase. The 20 nM value must
not be fitted onto Figure 3D as though these were identical conditions.

Figure 3E uses 1.1 nM radiolabeled DNA, a 614-bp probe with two tandem parS
sites and a 435-bp probe without parS, 450 nM ParB, and 650 nM PopZ.
Lanes are DNA alone; DNA plus ParB; DNA plus ParB plus PopZ; and DNA plus
PopZ. At 30 C for 30 minutes, buffer contains 40 mM phosphate pH 8.0,
20 mM NaCl, 5 mM MgCl2, 7% glycerol, 20 micrograms/ml BSA and
4.75 micrograms/ml sheared salmon-sperm DNA. The endpoint is gel migration
of labeled DNA. Adding PopZ to ParB–parS gives a supershift; PopZ alone does
not shift the probe under these conditions. The paper notes nonspecific
PopZ–DNA interaction at reduced competitor-DNA concentration. Probe length,
sequence and site number differ between the positive and negative DNA probes.

Co-IP uses strain GB135, with endogenous popZ replaced by popZ-m2 and DSP
crosslinking before lysis. ParB coprecipitates while FtsZ does not. This is
complex association in a native mixed lysate; it does not alone isolate a
direct binary interaction. Heterologous colocalization uses PopZ-YFP and
CFP-ParB in *E. coli*. Those fluorescence/localization and genetic outcomes
are not substitutes for direct binding numbers. The paper's deletion,
restoration and cell-division effects remain outside this A105 calculation.

The following is a **model calculation, not measured occupancy**. Under a
single independent site at equilibrium, with free analyte concentration c,
theta = c/(Kd+c). At c=20 nM and Kd=20 nM, theta=0.5. At c=125 nM,
theta=0.86207. The latter must not be asserted for the differently buffered
Figure 3D experiment. PopZ oligomerization, surface avidity, active concentration
and transport limitation must be resolved before treating this model as a
valid biological occupancy prediction. A 20 nM affinity does not establish
that binding activates, anchors or otherwise changes a physiological process.

## Conversion into the user's numerical sheet

These are proposed reusable component meanings, not categories coded as numbers.

**Signal and uncertainty.** A quantitative SPR row should expose
R_pair(c,t), R_reference(c,t), R_buffer_pair(t), R_buffer_reference(t),
their replicate covariance and fitted endpoint/time. Compute the double
referenced response

    R_net = [R_pair - R_reference] - [R_buffer_pair - R_buffer_reference].

Use the actual covariance matrix in Var(R_net)=a' Sigma a, with
a=(1,-1,-1,1). Do not subtract the same blank again as an isolation penalty.
For a specified contrast delta between paired binding responses, retain its
units and calculate Z=delta/SE(delta). Z measures separation from a defined
null relative to experimental uncertainty; it is not the probability of the
annotation being correct. No numerical Z is recoverable here without the raw
responses/replicate errors. The Kd point estimate alone cannot fill it.

**Experimental comparability outside the intended variable.** If the
question is whether PopZ binds ParB more than an alternative analyte, analyte
identity is the intended variable. Buffer, immobilized ParB loading/activity,
temperature, injection history, analyte active concentration and instrument
response calibration are nuisance variables. A comparison between screening
and affinity experiments also changes pH, salt species/concentration, flow
and BSA; none can be assigned a comparability number from a raw pH or salt
ratio. A reference-analyte response model g(x) measured across these conditions
can estimate a nuisance effect b_cmp=g(x_A)-g(x_B), in the same units as delta.
Correct delta to delta-b_cmp and propagate Var(b_cmp), including shared data.
Without g or paired controls, this component stays unknown.

**Isolation of the measured effect.** Double referencing removes surface and
buffer responses; separate BSA/FtsZ controls test additional association routes.
Their response cannot simply be averaged and subtracted without a model of
what background they represent. Let b_other be a measured expected response
from competing/mixed contributors in the tested sample. Then
delta_target=delta-b_cmp-b_other. This is distinct from matching conditions.
The gel experiment helps identify ParB-dependent ternary association but does
not supply an SPR b_other. It shares molecules with SPR and is complementary,
not an independently estimated second Kd. Native co-IP by itself leaves
indirect bridging unresolved. Quantified binary solution binding, a reference
surface, matched molecular-mass controls and tests of PopZ oligomeric state
would constrain the corresponding terms.

**Explicit proposed compilation, with no double correction.** One permissible
evidence statistic for a declared molecular contrast is

    E = delta_target / sqrt(V_meas + V_cmp + V_other + V_transfer
                            + 2 * sum(relevant covariance terms)).

Each V has response-units squared and represents an independently identified
source of uncertainty after correction, rather than an arbitrary quality grade.
For independent zero-covariance components, this is equivalently

    E = Z_target * sqrt(C_cmp * I_remaining * T_remaining),
    Z_target = delta_target / sqrt(V_meas),
    C_cmp = V_meas / (V_meas + V_cmp),
    I_remaining = (V_meas + V_cmp) / (V_meas + V_cmp + V_other),
    T_remaining = (V_meas + V_cmp + V_other) /
                  (V_meas + V_cmp + V_other + V_transfer).

This telescoping parameterization demonstrates explicit multiplication while
retaining separate physical components; the preferred stored cells are the
biases and covariance entries. These are information-retention multipliers,
not a general probability that two systems are equivalent. The ordering only
labels the displayed conditional ratios; the sum of variances is invariant.
Zero denominator, unquantified systematic bias, missing uncertainty or an
invalid endpoint leaves this statistic undefined. A valid, well-matched assay
does not acquire nonzero evidence merely because its uncertainty is small.
The response contrast must actually address the emerged molecular claim.

**Synthetic arithmetic only:** suppose a 30-RU contrast has a measured
5-RU nuisance correction and zero estimated remaining-contributor bias.
Let V_meas=4, V_cmp=9 and V_other=16 RU^2, and suppose this demonstration
concerns the exact assayed system so V_transfer=0. Then delta_target=25 RU,
Z_target=12.5, C_cmp=4/13=0.30769,
I_remaining=13/29=0.44828, T_remaining=1, and E=25/sqrt(29)=4.64238.
This is not a PopZ result. Neither missing V_transfer nor a different target
may be silently assigned zero. A bounded annotation-score conversion and a
choice of contrast/null remain for the common sheet; this proposal supplies
an explicitly combined evidence statistic, not a completed axis.

## Relevance, dependence and gaps

To apply the result to a supplied protein, preserve measured PopZ identity,
construct, molecular features and experimental system. No relationship to
the project's ybhP target is established here. Measured interchangeability,
binding-site/structural correspondence, sequence comparisons or relevant
experimental system comparisons can constrain transfer; a generic overall
transfer grade cannot fill V_transfer. A validated predictive mapping would
give delta_target-system=h(delta_assayed, measured correspondence) with its
uncertainty. Otherwise report the direct source effect and the unresolved
relationship separately.

The same raw surface traces must not supply both a blank-corrected response
and an extra multiplicative background-removal reward. Likewise an SPR Kd
and a model occupancy calculated from it are one underlying experiment.
The two parS sites and heterogeneous PopZ oligomers prevent converting a
supershift intensity into a monovalent binding constant without an explicit
model. A matched-length site-mutant DNA comparison would address probe
comparability; molecule-specific fluorescence or mass measurements would
address component attribution. These complement different weaknesses.

Reader-populated inputs are exact molecules/constructs and mixture members;
active versus nominal concentrations and conventions; reference/surface
identity; condition vector; intended variable; trace values/time/units;
blank/control values and reuse; independent preparation identifiers; fit
model, Kd, SE/CI and residuals; potential contributor measurements; target
correspondence measurements and their prediction relationship. Unknowns are
nullable with a specific missing operand, never 0 or 1 by default.
