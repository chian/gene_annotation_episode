# PopZ purified co-IP: recovered amount is not equilibrium occupancy

Research proposal, 2026-10-23. Ebersbach et al., *A self-associating protein
critical for chromosome attachment, division, and polar organization in
Caulobacter*, Cell 134:956–968 (2008), DOI
[10.1016/j.cell.2008.07.016](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2008.07.016),
[PMC2614312](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2614312/).

## Subjects, mixtures, intended change and endpoint

Figure 3J tests purified *C. crescentus* His6-ParB and His6-PopZ, separately
expressed in *E. coli* BL21/pET28a and then mixed. Both proteins coexist in
the full reaction. It is not a WT/mutant mixture. One microgram of ParB is
captured overnight on anti-ParB/Protein-A agarose. The beads are washed twice,
blocked with 0.25% BSA, exposed to one microgram of PopZ for five hours at
4 C, and washed five times. The buffer is 20 mM HEPES pH 7.5, 100 mM NaCl,
0.05% Triton X-100 and protease inhibitors, without the 20% glycerol used
in the native-lysate IP buffer. Eluates are immunoblotted with anti-Tetra-His.
Controls omit either PopZ or ParB. Direct-load lanes contain 25 ng of each
purified protein. The intended changed variable is availability of the
binding partner. The endpoint is PopZ retained and detected after capture,
incubation, washes and elution. It is not an equilibrium bound fraction or Kd.

The two proteins have distinct preparation histories: ParB comes from soluble
lysate, whereas PopZ comes from the pellet and is purified in 6 M urea then
dialyzed. Both retain His tags. The recovered PopZ oligomer state, folded/active
fraction, effective immobilized ParB amount and quantitative blot calibration
are relevant to the molecular interpretation. An exact molar input ratio
cannot be obtained from the equal masses alone without the construct masses.

Figure 3I instead uses native *C. crescentus* CB15N popZ::popZ-yfp and
untagged wild-type CB15N negative-control lysates. Anti-GFP captures PopZ-YFP
and associated native proteins. Lysates undergo DNaseI treatment. The loaded
lysate amount is one hundredth of the amount used for IP. This native mixture
may contain bridges or other contributors; DNase treatment does not prove
all nonbinary association is eliminated. It is complementary to purified
co-IP, not the same isolation question. Imaging, deletion/restoration,
growth and division phenotypes remain outside the direct-binding A105 row.

## Numbers that can actually be populated

| Numerical cell | Calculation | Result and meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Purified-input/direct-load mass ratio | 1000 ng / 25 ng | 40; establishes the required loading correction, not a 40-fold enrichment |
| Direct-load fraction of purified input | 25 ng / 1000 ng | 0.025; multiply a same-exposure, calibrated signal ratio by this fraction before claiming recovered input fraction |
| Native lysate load fraction | loaded lysate / IP-input lysate | 0.01, explicitly stated in Figure 3I; no measured IP recovery follows without band calibration and loading volumes |
| Shape-independent PopZ apparent mass range | SEC-UV/LS/RI result | 120–170 kDa; self-association/assembly evidence, not affinity |
| Nominal mass/monomer calculation | (120–170 kDa)/(21 kDa His6-PopZ calculated mass) | 5.714–8.095 monomer masses; not proof of a unique integer stoichiometry |

Native PAGE instead migrates between 480 and 720 kDa standards, and SDS-PAGE
around 35–36 kDa despite calculated 21 kDa. These are method-dependent
apparent masses; dividing the native-gel value by the SDS-gel value would
not yield a defensible oligomer count. SEC-UV/LS/RI is an important
complementary shape-independent measurement, but it is not a second binding
curve. Binding affinity, independent purification counts, quantitative
recovery, blot error and calibration linearity remain unknown here.

## Numerical conversion into the user's template

The source teaches a deterministic measurement correction that must precede
any score. Let I_P be background-subtracted PopZ-band intensity in the full
IP, I_0 its no-ParB control, and I_S the intensity of the 25-ng PopZ standard.
Let l_P and l_0 be fractions of the entire respective eluates loaded on the
gel. In a verified linear-response exposure with equal immunoreactivity:

    f_P = (I_P/I_S) * 25 ng / (l_P * 1000 ng)
    f_0 = (I_0/I_S) * 25 ng / (l_0 * 1000 ng)
    f_specific = f_P - f_0.

f_specific is the fraction of initially added PopZ recovered above the
specified no-ParB background. It is a useful signal magnitude and effect-
isolation cell. Its denominator is input PopZ mass, not the visually adjacent
input lane, cell number or captured ParB. It does not estimate a binding
constant because the bead capture and washes change the system.

**Synthetic illustration only:** if both full eluates were loaded, I_P/I_S=8
and I_0/I_S=2, f_P=0.20, f_0=0.05 and f_specific=0.15. This is 150 ng
specifically recovered from 1000 ng input. The fraction of the full recovered
signal explained by the measured background is 0.05/0.20=0.25. Either use
0.20*(1-0.25)=0.15 or subtract 0.05; do not subtract and then multiply again.
No such intensity ratios were extracted from this paper.

**Comparability outside the intended variable:** input PopZ mass, loaded
eluate fraction, exposure, antibody response, incubation, buffer, bead amount
and wash conditions should be common. Availability of ParB is the intended
variable and must be excluded from a nuisance similarity penalty. If capture
capacity or input PopZ active fraction differs unexpectedly, a measured
response model supplies a correction b_cmp in recovered-fraction units.
Matching pH numerically or taking 5 h divided by another time is not that
model. Unknown buffer addition volume in a protein-omission control cannot
be silently declared matched.

**Isolation:** the no-ParB control estimates PopZ binding to beads/antibody
under that preparation. The no-PopZ control establishes detection-position
specificity, but does not measure the PopZ-background intensity a second time.
Both tagged proteins are purified separately, reducing possible bridges;
residual contaminants, refolding/aggregation and the shared His-tag chemistry
remain potential contributors. A tag-free reciprocal assay and calibrated
solution binding address different limitations. Native co-IP should retain
a separate unknown indirect-contributor term instead of inheriting the
purified experiment's isolation as a perfect grade.

**Explicit combination:** use delta=f_specific-b_cmp-b_unresolved,
where b_unresolved is a measured remaining contribution, if any. Then

    E = delta / sqrt(Var(f_specific) + Var(b_cmp) + Var(b_unresolved)
                     + Var(target-system prediction) + covariance terms).

The variance of f_specific uses the common standard I_S and shared controls;
the two normalized bands are correlated. The sum combines measurable
uncertainty from signal, comparability and effect isolation without
invented category multipliers. A separate comparison-relevance multiplier
cannot be declared 1 for an unspecified target. The conditional information-
retention product equivalent is defined in paper_01, but this paper supplies
the **fraction-recovery** operands rather than an SPR response. A bounded
annotation-credit transformation remains a common-sheet design choice, not
something established by this paper. No full E can be computed from the
published presence/absence blot descriptions alone.

Consistency must compare the same endpoint under modeled conditions.
Purified co-IP and native co-IP are complementary isolation/system checks;
they are not two replicate Kd measurements. New biological preparations are
the independent units, not control lanes, microscopy frames or publications.
No source-to-ybhP relationship is supplied. Identification of the exact
assayed construct, experimental interchangeability, relevant structural
features and sequence/context comparisons can constrain a target prediction;
the relationship must be measured or explicitly unresolved.
