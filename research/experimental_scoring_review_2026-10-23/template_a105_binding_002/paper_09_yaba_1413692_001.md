## Experimental definitions and actual numbers

The biochemical experiment mixes separately purified *B. subtilis* YabA, DnaA and/or DnaN. DnaI is an additional protein control. These are protein mixtures in vitro, not a wild-type strain compared with a strain in which a mutant replaces the native protein. The paper describes proteins as purified to homogeneity; a purity percentage and independently prepared sample count are not supplied for these binding experiments. YabA, DnaA and DnaN nominal subunit masses in Figure 1 are 14, 51 and 42 kDa.

**Zinc precipitation (Methods and Figure 1A).** Incubate 25 µM YabA, 2 µM DnaA, 2 µM DnaN or 3 µM DnaI, alone or in combinations, in 50 µl containing 25 mM Tris pH 8, 150 mM NaCl and 0.5 mM DTT. Incubation is 2 h on ice, followed by 100 µM ZnSO4 overnight at 4 °C; centrifugation is 13,000g, 15 min, 4 °C. Resuspend pellet in 50 µl with 2 M urea; load 15 µl each of pellet and supernatant onto SDS-PAGE and stain with Coomassie. Source endpoint is the partition of each protein into pellet versus supernatant after this treatment. Zinc precipitates YabA alone but does not precipitate DnaA or DnaN alone. With YabA, most DnaA and part of DnaN coprecipitate. Some YabA remains soluble in the DnaA mixture. DnaI does not coprecipitate; the DnaI result is described as data not shown.

The intended variables are presence of YabA/partner and, in the calibration of the precipitation mechanism, presence of zinc. Partner identity and concentration differ for the DnaI control (3 µM) versus DnaA/DnaN (2 µM); this is not a fully matched quantitative specificity contrast. Direct derived exposure ratios are YabA:DnaA = YabA:DnaN = 25/2 = 12.5 nominal protomers/protomer and YabA:DnaI = 25/3 = 8.3333. Zn:YabA = 100/25 = 4 mol/mol. These define dosing; none measures affinity, occupancy, a binding-site count, or experimental comparability.

**Crosslinking (Methods and Figure 1B).** Preincubate combinations for 30 min on ice in 22.5 µl buffer D, with 8.5 µM YabA, 2.7 µM DnaA and/or 2 µM DnaN expressed as monomers. Add 0.01% glutaraldehyde, incubate 20 min at 25 °C, quench with 0.2 M glycine and use SDS-PAGE/silver staining. Pair mixtures and the three-protein mixture produce high-molecular-weight material; the three-protein mixture has a distinct band pattern. Derived nominal YabA:DnaA = 8.5/2.7 = 3.14815 and YabA:DnaN = 8.5/2 = 4.25. Crosslink yields or quantitative band identities/stoichiometries are not reported. The paper says YabA is tetrameric by gel filtration/sedimentation (data not shown), but only its dimer is trapped by this crosslinking protocol. DnaN is a dimer in solution but is not crosslinked under these conditions. A crosslink band is therefore a capture product whose yield depends on reactive-group geometry and chemistry, not an unbiased count of all occupied complexes.

Interactions were not detected by gel filtration under the tested conditions. This is an assay-specific nondetection, with no reported detection limit or concentration-dependent recovery curve; it is not zero binding and not a numerical contradiction of the trapping experiments.

Yeast-two-hybrid interaction screens use GAL4 fusions; the DnaA interacting fragment is residues 71–331. The purified biochemical DnaA construct must not be assumed to be this fragment. Colony growth and interaction-screen categories do not provide a calibrated molecular Kd. Cellular focus restoration and initiation phenotypes are parked for A106, not counted as A105 binding magnitudes.

## Conversion into the user's numerical sheet

### Experimental comparability outside the intended variable

For each protein's pellet fraction compare the same partner preparation, concentration, zinc, incubation, fraction volumes and detection settings with and without YabA. Differences in those conditions belong to comparability; presence of YabA is the intended variable and must be excluded from a mismatch calculation. Treat the 3 µM DnaI control as a different exposure unless a concentration-response relationship permits standardization to 2 µM. A raw concentration ratio 2/3 is not a comparability score. Likewise, overnight/20 min or pH ratios do not compare precipitation with crosslinking: they are different measurement processes.

Proposed numerical comparability cell: `b_cmp = sum_k beta_k * (x_test,k - x_control,k)` in pellet-fraction units, where each beta is a measured local response sensitivity for a nuisance condition. The sum excludes the deliberately tested protein addition. Its uncertainty is `v_cmp = Delta_x^T Cov(beta) Delta_x + beta^T Cov(Delta_x) beta`, including covariance as applicable. This is a measured-response correction and uncertainty, not counting matched metadata. No relevant beta estimates are given, so an unmeasured condition effect remains unknown. Equal documented settings justify a zero documented difference for those settings, not proof that every hidden bias is zero.

### Isolation of the measured effect

The separate isolation question is whether increased partner pelleting is attributable to YabA-dependent association rather than nonspecific entrapment, zinc aggregation, protein loss, or changed fraction recovery. Measure each partner alone with zinc, YabA plus partner, and an unrelated protein under quantitatively matched conditions. The DnaA/DnaN-alone lanes address independent zinc precipitation. The DnaI control addresses some nonspecific capture but has unequal concentration and no shown quantified band. A recovery control or calibrated spike addresses total protein losses. A second direct equilibrium-binding method would address the fact that precipitation can capture weak/transient and nonspecific association.

Proposed input and calculation, **conditional on linear, protein-specific calibration**: let `P` and `S` be background-corrected band signals for one partner in pellet and supernatant, `k_P` and `k_S` the signal per mass in their respective matrices, and `V_P/l_P` and `V_S/l_S` the total-volume/loaded-volume factors. Then

`m_P = (P/k_P)*(V_P/l_P)`; `m_S = (S/k_S)*(V_S/l_S)`;

`f_pellet = m_P/(m_P+m_S)`.

The nominal 50 µl fraction and 15 µl load factors cancel if both fractions have those actual volumes and recover equally. Urea in pellet and matrix-dependent staining require calibration, not an assumed exact cancellation. `recovery = (m_P+m_S)/m_input` is a separate measurable validity check; do not multiply by recovery a second time after recovery correction has already entered the fraction/model. Neither a silver-stain intensity nor a publisher JPEG is a calibrated mass measurement.

A controlled effect is `d = f_partner,+YabA - f_partner,-YabA`. A quantitatively matched unrelated-protein contrast could further yield `d_specific = d_partner - d_unrelated`. The latter estimates selectivity against that tested alternative, not specificity against every protein. Shared YabA/control preparation and calibration make these contrasts correlated. All four band-derived values enter one covariance calculation, `v_d = a^T Sigma_f a`, with `a=(1,-1,-1,1)` for the four-cell contrast. Do not treat lanes, numerator and denominator, or coprecipitation and its plotted fraction as independent experiments.

A glutaraldehyde capture proportion can be modeled as `f_trapped = f_occupied*c_capture + b_capture` only after defining the complex and calibrating capture efficiency `c_capture` and background `b_capture`. DnaN's failed self-crosslinking illustrates why `c_capture=1` cannot be assumed. Missing `c_capture` prevents converting a high-molecular-weight band's presence into equilibrium occupancy. The distinct three-protein band is useful qualitative evidence; it does not establish one particular ternary stoichiometry without composition/mass identification.

### Signal, uncertainty, consistency, relation to the inference and explicit combination

A proposed measurable signal component is `z = (d - b_cmp - b_other)/sqrt(v_total)` where `b_other` is separately estimated nonspecific capture/recovery bias, and `v_total` is the full covariance-propagated variance of the numerator, including calibration and both correction estimates. `b_cmp` concerns nuisance condition differences; `b_other` concerns effect isolation. Their separation is meaningful even when both are zero in an adequately measured design. Unknown correction or variance operands leave the component unfilled; they do not earn a fixed penalty grade. A large z would support controlled YabA-dependent recovery, not a Kd or replication-initiation regulation.

**Synthetic arithmetic only:** if calibrated fractions are 0.55 with YabA and 0.05 without, their difference is 0.50. If a measured nuisance correction is 0.02 and nonspecific capture correction is 0.03, the corrected effect is `0.50-0.02-0.03=0.45`. If total propagated variance is 0.0025 fraction², `z=0.45/sqrt(0.0025)=9`. The synthetic uncertainty must not be inferred from visual gel bands or from treating pixels as independent protein molecules.

For explicit combination into evidence for a *specified* binding inference, a proposed likelihood component is `ell = log p(y | H_binding, theta_cmp, theta_isolation) - log p(y | H_background, theta_cmp, theta_isolation)`. For a Gaussian corrected fraction contrast with variance v and specified expected effects delta_H and delta_0, `ell = ((d_corr-delta_0)^2-(d_corr-delta_H)^2)/(2*v)`. Using the synthetic corrected 0.45, v=0.0025, and explicitly synthetic predictions delta_H=0.40, delta_0=0 gives `(0.2025-0.0025)/0.005 = 40`. This is a proposed score transformation conditional on declared hypotheses and a justified noise model, not a source value, confidence probability or approved policy. Independent preparation blocks add `sum ell_j`; within a block all shared controls/calibrations remain in a joint model. The source lacks quantitative fractions, their uncertainty, independent preparation identities, and competing-model predictions, so no source-derived ell is calculated here.

Between-preparation variance or an actual repeated-condition discrepancy would quantify consistency. Different capture methods cannot simply be averaged or declared inconsistent because their units and observation mechanisms differ. The complementary crosslink experiment constrains a precipitation-artifact explanation; its shared proteins and uncertain capture model prevent treating it as an independent duplicate affinity measurement.

The source-to-target relationship is separate. Binding depends on partner identity, interfaces, oligomeric state and assay environment; identity, mapped molecular features or experimentally shown interchangeability can inform that relationship. No relationship from these proteins to the project's supplied ybhP material is established. Do not assign a generic transfer multiplier or convert sequence identity alone into an effect-size prediction. Target binding likelihood requires an explicit relationship model and its uncertainty; these remain missing.
