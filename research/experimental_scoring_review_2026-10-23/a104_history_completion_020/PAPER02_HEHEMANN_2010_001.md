## Definitions and actual comparison

The historical recipient is B. plebeius; the reported marine comparators include
Z. galactanivorans and Microscilla sp. PRE1. These are extant representatives,
not observed historical donor and recipient cells. PorA/PorB activity establishes
functional reference labels, while the relevant HGT evidence is phylogenetic,
genomic and ecological. No donor–recipient mating assay, historical cohabitation
duration, or measured cargo-entry rate was reported in the inspected material.
No mutant/wild-type mixture pertains to this historical comparison.

Fig. 3 places Bp1689 with porphyranase references and Bp1670 with agarase
references. This worker visually confirms the prior four-triplet comparison:
using PorA/PorB against AgaA/AgaB gives P=4/4=+1 for Bp1689 and P=-4/4=-1
for Bp1670. These are contrasting placements, not two independent HGT events.
The printed tree does not supply the complete branch-length and organism-tree
operands needed for a full numerical A104 value.

The proposed food route connects marine algal material with an intestinal
recipient despite different resident environments. Mobility-related genes and
the Microscilla plasmid provide a different kind of support from environmental
similarity. They do not report the fraction of B. plebeius cargo successfully
transferred by conjugation. Supplementary Table 3 contains best-hit identities;
those percentages must not be relabeled as mechanism probabilities or phylogenetic
event support. Mixed marine-like and gut-like context also prevents assuming one
undifferentiated ancestral history for the entire region.

## Conversion into the user's numerical template

The new numerical proposal keeps direct environmental overlap distinct from a
route that changes compartment. For a one-hot marine source and one-hot gut
recipient, route transition K(marine,gut)=k gives routed overlap B=k exactly.
The unknown k is not an unknown formula. A targeted reader would populate the
fraction of exposure units delivering transferable donor material to the gut,
or a bounded, explicitly labeled judgment of that exact route step when no
native observation exists. A broad “HGT seems plausible” number is forbidden.

Separate cargo membership M measures the fraction of the declared cargo linked
to a transfer-capable element or demonstrated transfer. A source-specific reader
must distinguish annotated mobility proteins from demonstrated transfer capacity.
The full proposal combines B, M and temporal feasibility explicitly; these
extant data do not alone produce a point historical score. A source-linked
synthetic completion will illustrate every missing operand without presenting
invented values as paper findings.
