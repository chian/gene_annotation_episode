## Proposed numerical sheet, still open for user feedback

The useful unit is **one observed result against one bounded, evidence-emergent inference in one stated system**. Different inferences from the same result get distinct claim rows with shared provenance; they do not become additional observations. Discovery can begin from sequence/file/snippets; no initial candidate name is required. These part-2 cells are proposals, not an adopted calibration, probability model or annotation-credit formula. They are not a design for a reader that independently invents cells. Part 3 must wait for the exact sheet to be settled.

| Numerical cell | How its number is obtained / what it evaluates | Type dependence and counterexample |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **D — endpoint relation**, proposed ordinal code 0/1/2 | 0: no measured endpoint relevant to this particular inference. 1: measured proxy, binding or partial step leaves the defining endpoint unresolved. 2: measured endpoint directly discriminates the bounded inference. A reader must quote the inferential boundary, not grade an entire paper. | O2 consumption is D=2 for oxygen consumption, D=1 for L2HG product identity. NMR/reference product identification is D=2 for product identity, still D=1 for native pathway priority. Crystal binding is direct for binding and proxy for catalysis. Code spacing is ordinal, not twice as much confidence. |
| **O — outcome against a stated discriminating prediction**, -1/0/+1/null | +1: observed result agrees with a claim-discriminating prediction; -1: it contradicts that prediction; 0: the attempted comparison does not discriminate; null: not established/read. The prediction and competitor are mandatory operands. | HFP preparation has real sugar isomerization, but contaminant tests give O=-1 for assigning it to HFP. Non-detection is a bounded result with unknown sensitivity, not an exact zero rate. |
| **T/X/H — named-alternative test counts**, nonnegative integers, plus nulls | For a declared list of material alternatives, T counts named alternatives with at least one discriminating experimental test, X counts alternatives contradicted by those outcomes under tested conditions, H counts alternatives supported. Retain the per-alternative O values. Denominator/list is explicit, revisable and not a universal number. | HFP host-contaminant alternative: T=1, X=0, H=1 for that one named alternative after other-preparation/MS evidence. Do not count NMR in two nuclei as two tests of protein attribution. A high X/T is not a confidence probability. |
| **S — controlled signal magnitude**, native units and explicit denominator | Difference, rate ratio, selectivity ratio, product yield or stoichiometric imbalance calculated from reported matched operands. Keep the untransformed numerator/denominator and uncertainty; use null if missing. | FrsA 36 M^-1 s^-1 is real but does not identify native substrate. BioC's half-rate at 8mM CoA versus 0.05mM ACP is not a 2-fold catalytic-efficiency difference. Coupled product fractions cannot be upstream specificity constants. |
| **U — precision and statistical support**, native numeric fields | n_biological/preparation, n_technical, reported SD/SE/CI or unknown-type ±, interval width, detection/calibration limits, and fit-range operands. A model parameter value does not supply its missing error type. | CsiD pool experiment has biological duplicates and technical duplicates, not four independent biological measurements. FrsA Smax/Km=0.323 measures limited range support, not formal identifiability. CysQ low-PAP linearity was not confirmed despite high R² at25μM. |
| **K+/K−/K? — consistency counts**, integers by claim and comparable condition | Count supporting, opposing and unresolved **dependency groups** for the same inference/system; preserve links and individual outcomes. If dependence is unknown, report an unknown-dependence group and no manufactured independent count. | Different products in the same normalized LC-MS spectrum are not three replications. LhgO purified no-O2 and membrane O2 do not contradict once intrinsic versus chain-mediated endpoints are distinguished. FrsA ester positivity does not cancel PDC negativity. |
| **G — system correspondence**, per-dimension 0/1/null flags | For declared target dimensions (protein identity, biochemical/cofactor state, substrate form, partner, environment/organism), 1 means experimentally matched, 0 means a stated mismatch, null means unknown. The dimension vector is the score; an optional mismatch count is only descriptive. | Cognate versus foreign CofD is a measured partner mismatch with a large effect. An E.coli rescue by Mtb CysQ supports cross-system capability but is not a native Mtb flux measurement. Do not hide a wrong substrate form by averaging it with four matching dimensions. |

**Compilation proposal:** preserve a typed per-inference vector and an experiment-dependency graph. Within a shared preparation/readout group, complementary controls can change D/O or resolve a named alternative, but do not add independent replication. Across sufficiently distinct results, retain K+/K−/K? by comparable condition. Report direct support, material unresolved alternatives, native effect/precision and system correspondence separately. An annotation-support compiler could use these vectors as coordinates after user review of their meaning/calibration; a scalar confidence, weighted sum, product of ordinal grades or confidence probability is not justified by this sample. In particular, D=2 plus a contaminant-supported attribution challenge cannot compile as direct target-catalysis support merely because signal is strong. A specific proposed aggregation function still needs user choice and cross-lane challenge; this lane does not settle it.

A minimal numerical compilation example for two tested alternatives could report `(T=2,X=1,H=1)` and preserve their opposing outcomes. Reporting `X/T=0.5` is permissible only as **fraction of these two tested alternatives contradicted**; it is not 50% probability the annotation is correct. Unknown/unexamined alternatives remain outside that denominator and explicitly visible. A new complementary experiment can move one named alternative from untested to contradicted; it does not automatically increase annotation support if it instead supports contamination or identifies a wrong product.

## Worked contrasts with actual operands

**1. Strong bounded chemistry: CsiD, then native relevance.** The oxygen-electrode result gives specific activity **53±3 μmol min^-1 mg^-1**, apparent Km(glutarate) **650±20μM**, Km(αKG) **100±7μM**. Uncertainty type and replicate meaning for these kinetic values were not identified in the inspected text. Oxygen uptake receives D=1 for the inference “makes L2HG.” NMR/LC-MS plus derivatization compared to L/D standards raise that inference to D=2; no D product was detected, with detection bound unknown. The native ΔcsiD pool increase adds system applicability during starvation. Its Fig2d legend describes biological duplicates with SD including technical duplicates. No exact metabolite-fold change was digitized. The compiled result is direct stereochemical activity plus bounded native contribution, not three independent confirmations or quantified oxygen/product stoichiometry. [Primary Results and Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6265302/).

**2. Strong but misleading signal: HFP isomerase attribution.** The preparation reaches **39.7% Ru5P /60.3% R5P**, and 31P/13C NMR agree on the chemistry. That is D=2 for preparation-level isomerization. Further purification and **90°C for2min** slow but do not abolish activity; purification survival therefore cannot be a universal attribution threshold. **4/5 randomly chosen other phosphatase preparations** also show isomerization, and MS detects host RpiA. For the named host-contamination alternative, T=1,X=0,H=1, and O=-1 for attributing isomerase to HFP. The4/5 proportion is a preparation background observation, not confidence. The result remains chemically real while target-function support decreases. Separately, **2/22** staged screen hits (HFP and IMP) become only HFP in the one-pot pathway; NMR explains IMP's NADPH/Ru5P depletion. The absent quantitative yield remains null. [Primary Results/Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12051542/).

**3. Complement changes reaction identity: YihS.** New product resonances were not assigned from mass alone. Authentic SF, synthesized SR and D2O exchange establish SF plus the additional C2 epimer SR; starting from all three sulfosugars gives the same equilibrium. Fig2b fractions are **SF30.2%,SR21.3%,SQ48.5%**. D moves from1 for an unexplained extra signal to2 for the specific SR product. Fractions are three components of one mixture, not three experiments. SQ kinetics give **kcat79.0±2.7s^-1**, **Km1.89±0.28mM**, efficiency **41700±5100M^-1s^-1**; the reported **178-fold** comparison to earlier mannose kinetics is cross-paper and cannot be treated as a matched intracellular competition. No G6P conversion at10mM supplies no exact zero without a limit. Salmonella H248A structure is a different species/inactive state, not an extra E.coli turnover replicate. [Primary YihS Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8006165/).

**4. Coupling changes specificity: CofC/CofD.** MycetohabitansB3 CofC with the common archaeal CofD gives normalized products **65%3-PG,26.5%2-PL,8.5%PEP** with equal **0.5mM** candidate substrates. The publication calls n=3 biological replicates and displays SD; this review does not infer three independent expression/purification lots. Replacing only CofD with its cognate partner shifts almost completely to3-PG; the textual description does not supply an exact new percentage. S stores the first fractions; partner-shift magnitude is null. The assay is D=2 for the pair's product spectrum but D=1 for isolated upstream specificity. G_partner changes from0 to1 for the source-organism pair. This is a controlled measurement-operator change, not failed replication. [2022 Results, Figs2/6 and combined assay Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8764529/).

**5. Comparative kinetics with a calibration limit: CysQ.** Table1 reports PAP efficiency **42±8μM^-1min^-1**, IMP **0.0027±0.0015**, FBP **0.025±0.001** in the same units. Central-value ratios calculated here are **≈15,556** and **1,680**. These are useful S values, qualified by different substrate-optimal pH/Mg and assay methods; they are not exact native flux preferences. PAP Km **8.1±3.1μM** required MS because the color assay was near its limit. The calibration progress experiment at25μMPAP gave R² **0.993** over0–6min and **37%** conversion at4min; low-PAP4min linearity could not be confirmed because of ion suppression. The paper reports three independent experiments but does not establish preparation independence or the type of each ± in the inspected text. E.coli growth rescue adds G information for heterologous capability, not Mtb native flux. [Table1, kinetic Methods/Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2711008/).

**6. Weak artificial substrate and fit support: FrsA.** Newly read2019 Methods specify “various concentrations of pNPA (0.05–6.0 mM).” The fitted Km is **18.6±1.3mM**, kcat **0.67s^-1**, efficiency **36M^-1s^-1**. The ± type is unknown; assays were performed in triplicate with no inferred preparation independence. Calculated here: **Smax/Km=6/18.6=0.323**; under the reported Michaelis–Menten fit, **v(6mM)/Vmax=6/(18.6+6)=0.244**. This suggests limited saturation coverage; it is not a formal identifiability analysis or proof of an invalid fit. D=2 for the observed model-ester hydrolysis after background control, D=1 for an esterase native-role inference. Earlier PDC negatives remain a separate retained-only result, and the native function stays unresolved. [2019 Methods/Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6450606/).

**7. Same terminal signal, different system meaning: LhgO.** Purified LhgO reduces UQ1 at **0.33±0.002μmol min^-1mg^-1**, but no O2 consumption was detected (bound unknown). Native membranes show L2HG-dependent quinone reduction and O2 consumption; deleting lhgO removes the redox activity, complementation partly restores it. HQNO above **80μM** abolishes membrane O2/DCPIP activity. The same O2 endpoint is a proxy for downstream respiration in membranes and cannot establish protein-intrinsic oxidase chemistry. Direct quinone reduction plus native quinone/genetic tests support the dehydrogenase interpretation. Keep the unquantified complement effect and possible menaquinol reoxidation limitation visible. [LhgO Results, Fig3 and Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6265302/).

## What compilation must preserve

- Opposing endpoint and attribution outcomes: HFP isomerization is present; assignment to HFP is challenged. Neither observation disappears.
- Genuine side activities and condition-dependent physiological roles: YihU SSA chemistry, SuhB IMPase, YigL/PNP, YjjG salvage and FrsA artificial esterase do not collapse to true/false native-function labels.
- Dependence: shared preparations, cofactor-state changes, linked pathway outputs, normalizations and composition constraints do not create independent replications.
- Unknown sensitivity, raw curves, preparation counts, uncertainty types and physiological concentrations: source citation supplies no missing number.
- Specific complementarity: product chemistry repairs proxy ambiguity; contaminant controls repair attribution; cognate-pair swaps repair system transfer; none repairs every weakness at once.
- Investigation progress versus annotation support: discovering contamination or an extra product can be useful progress while weakening the previously favored specific annotation. No scoring change to production credit is made here.

## New follow-up lanes and next primary reads

1. **Fit-range/design support versus precision.** FrsA2019 and CysQ2008 show distinct problems: limited saturation coverage versus unconfirmed low-substrate initial-rate behavior. Next inspect raw initial-rate data, fit residuals/covariance and supplemental definitions. Do not label a fitted value invalid solely from Smax/Km.
2. **Coupling operator and downstream selectivity.** F4202022 and HFP2025 expose partner/intermediate filtering and competing-substrate destruction. Next read direct intermediate measurements and product response calibration; request exact Fig6 data before numerical partner-shift claims.
3. **Protein attribution despite purity and apparent orthogonality.** HFP2025, retained CodA2011, HydABC2009 and Mvh-Hdr2011. Next recover complete purification/contamination controls and native-system attribution; orthogonal readouts of one contaminant remain dependent.
4. **Nonproductive binding/adduct/half-reaction.** Retained EUBREC2651, QueF-L and YadB. Recover original full Methods/Results and quantify productive product versus partial signal under matched substrate state. This is a distinct failure mode from weak signal.
5. **System-conditioned endpoints and true negative bounds.** LhgO, EF1143, YigL/YjjG and archaeal phosphatases. Next read exact sensitivity limits, biochemical state/metal controls and genotype-by-environment designs. A conditional change is not automatically an inconsistent experiment.

## Explicit numerical compilation candidate to challenge

A concrete **reporting** compilation can be tested before choosing annotation-credit calibration. Let `c` be an emergent bounded claim, `z` its measured system, and `g` a known dependency group. Keep each result's `(D,O)` and the named attribution alternatives. Compute, per dependency group:

- `P_g=1` if at least one directly discriminating endpoint supports the claim (`D=2,O=+1`); `P_g=0` if all necessary classification operands are known and none does; otherwise null.
- `A_g=1` if a discriminating control supports a material alternative to the claimed target attribution; `A_g=0` only for the explicitly tested alternatives contradicted under the stated conditions; otherwise null. This is not a claim that all possible alternatives were excluded.
- `N_g=1` if a direct discriminating endpoint contradicts the claim; `N_g=0` if all relevant recorded outcomes are known and none does; otherwise null.

Compile `(sum_known(P_g),sum_known(A_g),sum_known(N_g),number_of_unknown_groups)` with the system correspondence vector and native `S/U` operands still attached. Never subtract the second/third coordinate from the first or count shared preparation readouts as new groups. Unknown preparation dependence is an explicit group with unknown independence; the displayed sum is then a **record-group count**, not independent replication. This concrete compilation makes a misleading strong signal reviewable: the HFP preparation's isomerization can have `P_g=1,A_g=1` simultaneously. For target-HFP catalysis that pair signals a material attribution challenge and cannot silently produce unqualified direct support. For a preparation-level chemistry claim the same attribution issue has a different claim boundary.

For CsiD product identity, oxygen uptake alone has `P_g=0` for a direct L2HG identity endpoint; after chemical/stereochemical product identification the same group's `P_g=1`. Attribution alternatives not examined in the selected reading remain null, not zero. For YihS product identity, new SR identity changes the reaction definition and its directly observed product coordinates; normalized SF/SR/SQ components remain one dependent mixture. For an intrinsic LhgO oxidase claim, a purified no-detection result carries a negative observation with an unknown detection bound; it must not be converted into `N_g=1` for an absolute-zero claim. The definition must instead be bounded to the reported assay sensitivity, which remains unquantified here.

These formulas are a concrete candidate for compiling the sheet, not an adopted annotation-credit formula or a scientific acceptance gate. Conversion of these preserved coordinates to annotation-credit hypervolume needs root/user review, calibrated coordinate meanings and W05 ownership. This lane does not invent a scalar confidence to close that remaining choice.
