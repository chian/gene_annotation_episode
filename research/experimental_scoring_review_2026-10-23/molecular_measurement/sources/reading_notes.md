# Primary reading ledger

All successful new readings used `https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/{PMCID}/fullTextXML`. The canonical article links below identify the same primary papers. Only the selected sections below are claimed newly read. Main-text statements describing supplemental experiments were not treated as independent inspection of underlying supplemental data. Numerical values in the review are reported values or explicitly labeled calculations, never new biological measurements.

1. **PMC12051542, HFP / Aquifex riboflavin phosphatase.** [Primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12051542/). New Results: Detection of ARAPDP phosphatase activity; Determination of phosphatase specificity; Determination of competing activities; FMN phosphatase activity. New Methods: Purification of histidine-tagged HFP; Phosphatase activity screens; One-pot assay; 31P-NMR experiments. Read main-text reports of Figures S6–S8, not their underlying spectra. Important separation: initial preparation NMR genuinely demonstrates Ru5P/R5P chemistry; unrelated-preparation controls and RpiA MS oppose assigning that chemistry to HFP. One-pot experiments reported triplicate; preparation independence not established. NMR conditions contain apparent concentration-unit inconsistencies between paragraphs; this review uses the unambiguous main comparison and does not silently correct raw Methods text.

2. **PMC8764529, CofC/CofD diversification.** [Primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8764529/). New Results: Assessment of substrate specificities of CofC enzymes from several organisms; Role of CofD in substrate specificity of F420 side chain biosynthesis. New Methods: Combined CofC/D assay. Read Figures 2/6 legends through article XML; no bar digitization. First pair has 65%,26.5%,8.5% normalized products with common archaeal partner; cognate-partner changes described qualitatively. Authors call the three replicates biological; this review does not infer independent protein lots. Relative AUC/product fractions are not upstream kcat/Km values.

3. **PMC6450606, FrsA esterase.** [Primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6450606/). New Methods: Substrate specificity of VvFrsA towards p-nitrophenyl alkanoate esters; Kinetic constants of VvFrsA towards pNPA. New Results: Purification and characterization; Substrate specificity; Steady-state kinetic constants; full Discussion. Exact retained Methods fragment: **“various concentrations of pNPA (0.05–6.0 mM)”**. Km18.6±1.3mM, kcat0.67s^-1 and efficiency36M^-1s^-1 are reported. Smax/Km=0.323 and fitted v(Smax)/Vmax=0.244 are calculations in this review. They describe limited saturation coverage, not formal identifiability or a proven invalid fit. Triplicate wording does not define independent protein preparation or the type of ±. The 2013 negative-PDC study was not newly read.

4. **PMC8006165, molecular basis of sulfoglycolysis selectivity.** [Primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8006165/). New main Results: complete SQ-SF Isomerase Also Produces Sulforhamnose section, including product chemistry, H/D exchange, mutarotase, kinetic and structure discussion; SF Kinase Activity Is Regulated by Sulfoglycolytic and Central Metabolites and Table1 also read. No Supporting Information Methods, raw spectra or kinetic fits read. Detailed worked reconstruction is YihS. Figure2b fractions30.2%,21.3%,48.5%; reported SQ efficiency41700±5100M^-1s^-1; ratio178 compares earlier mannose parameters. The inactive Salmonella H248A structure cannot be counted as an E.coli turnover replication.

5. **PMC2711008, Mtb CysQ.** [Primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2711008/). New Experimental Procedures: Activity Assays; Single-Point Normalization Factor; Reaction Progress Curve; Determination of Michaelis–Menten Parameters for PAP, IMP and FBP. New Results: ESI-MS Assay; Steady-State Kinetic Assays; Genetic Complementation. Table1 and Figures5–7 text/legends read. Values/units preserved as printed, including efficiencies in μM^-1min^-1. Cross-substrate assays differ in pH/Mg/readout. Progress linearity is supported at25μMPAP, not confirmed at low PAP. PAPS remains unstable/unquantified. Three independent experiments is reported wording; preparation independence/±type not established.

6. **PMC6265302, CsiD/LhgO lysine degradation.** [Primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6265302/). New Results: Characterisation of glutarate hydroxylase CsiD; LhgO is a membrane bound quinone-dependent oxidoreductase. New Methods: NMR of CsiD reaction; CsiD reactions analysed by Clark-type O2 electrode; Stereospecificity of CsiD reaction; Activity of purified LhgO and membrane fractions; Bacterial ubiquinone reduction. Figures2/3 legends read. CsiD kinetic ±type not specified in inspected text; Figure2d biological duplicates and technical duplicates described. LhgO specificactivity0.33±0.002μmolmin^-1mg^-1; ±type not established for this number. Figure3b/c explicitly use SD of triplicate experiments, with preparation independence not inferred. Oxygen negative bound, quantitative pool/genetic effects and topology remain unknown.
