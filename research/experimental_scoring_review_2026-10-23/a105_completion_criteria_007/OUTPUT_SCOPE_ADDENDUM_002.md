# Addendum: exact numerical row delivered and aggregation still outside it

2026-10-23. Clarifies the output-scope item in
`COMPLETION_CRITERIA_REVIEW_001.md`; the original assessment remains intact.
This addresses the parent's later request to make the material whole-axis limit
visible in the residual checklist. No scoring policy is elected.

The smallest usable proposed sheet contract produces a **numerical evidence row
for one declared molecular statement, one supplied-target material scope, and
specified molecular state/conditions**. The row's scope and formula route are
explicit. A statement may arise from investigation; it is not a starting
candidate prerequisite. Native quantities, uncertainty and signed results remain
alongside the proposed support coordinate.

| Row/model scope | Numerically defined output | Aggregation not supplied by that row |
|---|---|---|
| One assayed source; uncalibrated measured correspondence to the target | Source S and B; native correspondence components; elected-form R; `D=R*B`; if support-only projection is elected, `c=max(0,D)`; missing-family bounds and conditional basis retained | A chosen combination across multiple different source-to-target relationships |
| One or more calibrated sources mapping to the same target contrast | Mapped target contrasts and full covariance; joint target effect/variance; target S/B; `D=B_target`; proposed `c=max(0,D)` | Adding a separate uncalibrated D as if it were another target likelihood; pooling unrelated endpoints |
| Several uncalibrated sources for the same molecular statement | Each source's S/B/R/D and the explicitly labeled candidate M/K calculations | The intended combined target operand remains unresolved. A per-source c is not already the final combined coordinate. |
| A specified compound molecular statement with its joint observation model | Define H+ and H−, calculate their joint constrained-fit S and B, and the elected coordinate mapping | Combining arbitrary endpoints or conventional indexes without defining the compound statement/model |
| An entire annotation containing several molecular claims | Separate scoped rows remain available | No general scalar reducer over all those rows is defined by the inspected A105 sheet. |

The finite residual checklist therefore explicitly includes:

- [ ] Label the delivered output as the scoped row above and provide its exact
  model/field crosswalk; do not present a single-source or single-statement value
  as a complete whole-annotation A105 scalar.
- [ ] Resolve the still-open combination across uncalibrated source relationships
  **for the same statement**. That is an A105 research-design issue, even when
  the whole-annotation reducer is outside the scoped row.
- [ ] State which promise is being made about whole-axis output. If the promised
  deliverable is one A105 scalar for the entire annotation, its numerical
  claim/endpoint reducer is an **essential undefined calculation** and must be
  defined before that promise is complete. It cannot be dismissed as reader
  implementation or annotation-path wiring. If the deliverable is the explicitly
  scoped row, arbitrary annotation-wide claim assembly belongs to the later
  receiving design/W05 and is not an additional completion gate for that row.

This distinction does not invent an averaging, maximization, conjunction or
independence rule. It identifies exactly where a number exists and where it
does not. The parent reports having printed this scope distinction to the user;
no answer or acceptance is inferred. A105 and the goal remain unchecked.

**positive semidefinite in the matrix/Loewner sense**, not entrywise nonnegative.
The negative residual-correlation endpoint is therefore allowed in that stated
set. The separately restricted nonnegative-correlation family has the narrower
