# Additive reader arithmetic clarifications

This clarifies two optional fields in `READER_IO_ADDITIONS_001.md`; it changes no prior measurement, adopts no policy and introduces no additional native method.

1. **Actually measured conformer/state weights.** If measured physical weights π_m exist, they must map explicitly to mutually exclusive physical states represented by the admitted scenarios, obey π_m≥0 and Σπ_m=1, and retain their experimental normalization. Mere ensemble membership cannot establish them. For complete known weights, the single observation has `P_obs=Σ_m π_m max(h_m w_m,0)` and `O_obs=Σ_m π_m max(−h_m w_m,0)`, with `Z_obs=P_obs−O_obs`. It still contributes one observation to its dependence cluster. A synthetic π=(.7,.3) with signed scenario products(.4,−.2) yields P=.28,O=.06,Z=.22. Unknown weights leave the population point null and use the admitted complete-scenario envelope. Weight uncertainty is evaluated jointly over the actual constrained admissible weights, not by adding separate copies as experiments. No measured physical weights are available in the two NMR examples here; their output remains model-conditional plus an envelope.

2. **Bound correction for named single protons.** The four selected native NOE restraints name single protons, use their deposited bounds as written, and add no pseudoatom offset. The reader's `unknown_bound_correction` failure applies when an actually used grouped/pseudoatom/alias convention is unresolved. It does not require an additional calibration measurement for an exact native single-proton pair or invent a new gate on the present examples. A native correction already embodied in U is retained in U, not added again.

3. **Support versus opposition in native partial rows.** The published NMR ensemble tables give the support-only DG envelope for the declared ≤4 Å predicate. They are not an asserted complete signed-axis result under every possible incompatible-event definition. If a question also declares >4 Å to be an incompatible event, compute h and the entire signed product separately in each admitted model scenario, then take the joint signed envelope. No model-independent direction is inferred from a medoid.

4. **Conditional cross-link arithmetic remains a scope probe.** The hypothetical local p→1−p relation in `CROSSLINK_SOLUTION_SCOPE_001.md` supplies exact conditional arithmetic but has no established measured p in this packet and no method selector. Generic cross-linking coverage is therefore not claimed. Its synthetic .276 calculation is not a third empirical example.
