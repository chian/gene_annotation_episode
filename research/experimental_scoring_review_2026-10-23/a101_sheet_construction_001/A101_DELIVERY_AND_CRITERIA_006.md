## Complete current equation

For BLAST: w=(1-exp(-.025*max(bits,0)))*paired_reference_fraction*(.5+.5*identity).
For HMMER: w=(1-exp(-.020*max(bits,0)))*paired_reference_fraction. Identity uses
all alignment columns; reference fraction uses actual paired residues or
emitted model states according to the explicit biological orientation.

At each target position and within each tool, M is the strongest supporting
comparison weight and O the strongest same-scope incompatible weight. The
region contribution is mean(M*(1-O)); included tool weights are fixed by the
named calculation profile. Required regions use their geometric mean. The
feature-qualified result multiplies by the mean of the declared relevant
feature-panel fractions and the conjunction of source-established necessary
predicates. These are explicit conventional index operations, not independent
probabilities. Every coefficient and missing-input envelope is in the sheet.

The complete synthetic example gives BLAST .402949681974 and HMMER
.456031123675, equal mean .429490402824, and feature factor (.6+1)/2=.8.
Its selected result is **.3435923222594366**. Two genuinely required regions
instead give .287866586234 under that different stated biological meaning.
Halving/doubling the inverse-bit constants gives .278513274975/.363839961812.
These sensitivities are explicit policy consequences, not experimental errors.
