# Additive domain and correspondence clarification

This adds detail to `paper_component_conversion_001.md`; it replaces none of its numbers.

The main Results distinguish **one T-fold domain** in unimodular bacterial QueF from **two weakly homologous tandem T-fold domains** in bimodular QueF. In the unimodular form the cofactor-associated signature and substrate-pocket residues occur in the same domain. In the bimodular form the signature is in the N-terminal domain and substrate-pocket residues are in the C-terminal domain. Figure 2C uses only the C-terminal portion of the bimodular references. Thus absence of that signature from a displayed C-terminal fragment is expected and cannot be treated as absence from the full bacterial protein.

The archaeal comparison is to the substrate-pocket-bearing C-terminal T-fold. The reviewed native statement supplies neither precise domain boundary coordinates nor an independent sequence-domain score for Pcal_0221. The conserved pocket and absent complete signature in its supplied full record remain the usable sequence observations. A domain count or a ratio of sequence lengths must not be mislabeled alignment coverage.

Supplemental Data 1 gives a 287-aa *V. cholerae* sequence under ZP_01677885.1. The main paper uses crystal structure 3BP1 as its structural reference. This review did not establish the exact sequence/construct mapping between that supplied accession and 3BP1. Consequently, 287 aa must not silently become the denominator for the reported C-terminal structural comparison. The 165-aa B. subtilis record is a different reference used for the four named site columns, and its length also cannot be substituted for that denominator.
