## Conservative arithmetic and interval meaning

The claim that a contrary addition lowers a positive statement is pointwise:
existing operands are held fixed, the added signed contribution is negative,
and its H is nonnegative. Additional observations can separately alter a
statistical fit, covariance or nuisance estimate. Such genuine information
changes must be evaluated through the complete current calculation; they are
not an exception that permits hiding the negative contribution.

In the generic box method, nonidentical H expressions linked by a shared
upstream factor can be relaxed to containing boxes only with that relaxation
explicitly recorded. The original shared factor is not forgotten and is never
resampled independently per occurrence. The resulting enclosure can be wider
than an exact joint result. The worked candidate instead uses the exact shared
H and delta and proves its joint extrema analytically.
