## Subjects, mixtures and actual measurement

Fig.1 compares E. coli ΔmurJ ΔlysA strains NR2592 and NR2593 producing FLAG-MurJ lacking endogenous cysteines (called MurJ WT by the paper) or its A29C derivative. The native murJ locus is deleted in these strains, so WT and A29C are replacement constructs in separate cultures, not an unmeasured coexpressed mixture. A separate WT-allele suppression experiment is Fig.2 and belongs to A106. The assayed cultures retain biosynthesis enzymes, other membrane proteins, and periplasmic carboxypeptidases. “WT” in the assay does not mean an untouched native MurJ sequence.

Cells were metabolically labeled with [3H]-meso-diaminopimelate for 15 min, then treated with ColM and/or MTSES for 10 min. ColM cleaves accessible lipid II, yielding soluble PP-Mpep5-G subsequently processed to PP-Mpep4-G. Hot-water extracts were analyzed by HPLC/radiodetection; a sequential butanol extract contained lipid-linked precursors. Fig.1B's ordinate is **cps × min**, integrated chromatographic peak area. Fig.1C is **cpm** in the lipid fraction. Neither ordinate is nmol of transported lipid II per minute. The two detector units cannot be directly added or divided into a molar mass balance without calibration and recovery conversion.

For Fig.3, ΔfhuA cells producing the respective MurJ variants were labeled/treated and converted to spheroplasts, removing the outer-membrane access barrier. Spheroplasts were incubated with 100 µg ColM for 15 min at 37°C; 0.8 mM MTSES was added where indicated. One A29C/MTSES/ColM sample lacked sucrose and lysed. That condition deliberately changes access of the reporter to the inner leaflet. The plotted endpoint is residual butanol-extractable radiolabel, cpm. Fig.1 and Fig.3 give mean ± SD, N=3; the text does not identify whether all three constitute independently grown preparations. The statistical annotations are Student t tests, with actual reported bounds or exact P=0.052 retained as such, not converted to annotation probabilities.

The intended variables depend on the comparison: ColM addition tests reporter cleavage; intact versus lysed spheroplasts tests membrane protection/access; A29C ± MTSES tests chemical-genetic inactivation. Exclude the variable being tested from experimental-comparability penalties. The last causal comparison is A106; the A105 measurement is the substrate-accessibility/cleavage endpoint and the demonstrated molecular operation it reports.

## Numerical components and their combinations

**Reporter-access isolation.** Let `L_0` be calibrated residual lipid-II label in a matched no-ColM aliquot, `L_C` with ColM, and `L_B` non-lipid-II/background label. A measured cleavage fraction would be

`F_cleaved = (L_0 − L_C)/(L_0 − L_B)`.

This subtraction and denominator evaluate how much of the measured pool is reporter-accessible, conditional on comparable synthesis, extraction, and detector response. They do not independently measure the fraction transported by a particular molecule. For inhibited A29C spheroplasts, lysis restores cleavage, addressing access versus reporter inactivity. An access-dependent signal cell is `D_access = L_intact − L_lysed`; a normalized form is `F_access = D_access/(L_intact − L_B)` only when matched recovery and background are established. Here the two conditions differ in osmotic support and lysis, which may change extraction recovery; a quantified recovery comparison is missing.

The Fig.3 image permits a deliberately coarse **visual interval**, not exact tabulated data: inhibited intact A29C/ColM bar 6 lies between 7,000 and 8,000 cpm; lysed counterpart bar 7 lies between 2,000 and 3,000 cpm. Consequently the apparent count decrease is 4,000–6,000 cpm. If background is provisionally omitted solely to describe the raw counts, `1−L_lysed/L_intact` lies between `1−3000/7000 = 0.5714` and `1−2000/8000 = 0.75`. These bounds reflect coarse figure reading, **not sampling confidence, not extraction uncertainty, and not a transport efficiency**. Raw replicate counts are needed for an uncertainty-weighted value. This is a useful numerical example of an access control whose scope must not be inflated into an overall reliability score.

**Comparability outside the intended variable.** Use matched-cell input, labeled precursor pool, extraction recovery `r`, scintillation efficiency `e`, and ColM catalytic capacity `v_C` as quantitative conditions. A calibrated lipid amount is `(counts−blank)/(e × specific_activity × r)`, with the detector's time-unit convention explicitly converted. It is not valid to use total [3H] labeling added as the intracellular precursor specific activity without measuring isotope dilution. Cell input, labeling efficiency, intracellular precursor abundance, and MurJ abundance are separate denominators; their actual values are not available from the inspected main text.

If a known response function `g(x)` relates a nuisance condition to reporter output, use `g(x_reference)/g(x_assay)` once to standardize, propagating its error. A proposed similarity component `min(g_A,g_B)/max(g_A,g_B)` can instead describe the uncorrected response comparability; using both as multiplicative penalties would count the same mismatch twice. Raw ratios of 100 µg enzyme, 0.8 mM inhibitor, or 15 min assay time do not supply a relevance score. Lysis is deliberately tested in the access comparison, not itself a nuisance difference to penalize there.

**Coupled reporter versus transport rate.** With `L_out` the accessible lipid-II pool, a transport model is

`dL_out/dt = J_flip − J_reverse − J_PG − v_ColM`,

and `dP_reporter/dt = v_ColM − product_loss`, with carboxypeptidase processing and recovery included for the actual PP-Mpep4-G reporter. Only under demonstrated fast reporter capture, negligible competing consumption/reversal, and validated substrate labeling does product slope estimate forward flipping. A 10- or 15-min endpoint cannot establish that condition. The paper has compelling access controls but does not report a calibrated initial flipping slope in the inspected material. Thus flux is unknown, while reporter-dependent substrate accessibility is measured.

**Uncertainty and consistency.** For independent inputs, the variance of `D_access` is `u_intact²+u_lysed²`; shared preparation/calibration adds the covariance term `−2cov`. Ratio uncertainty must include that covariance and the reused denominator. With exactly three independent replicates, SD would become a standard error by SD/√3; independence is not established merely by N=3. Fig.1 water-product gain and lipid-label loss are linked through the same substrate pool, so they are complementary mass-balance observations, not automatically two independent experiments. Likewise Fig.3 is an access control that addresses a Fig.1 weakness; summing them as independent confidence would ignore the shared mechanism and materials.

**System and target correspondence.** The measurement concerns lipid-II topology in engineered E. coli cells/spheroplasts. Applicability to native MurJ, other organisms, or purified membranes requires explicit correspondence of the construct, substrate, topology, membrane composition, and energetic state. The constructed Cys-free reference and A29C must remain visible. Structural analogy alone cannot supply their missing activity normalization. Genetic lysis/suppression, MTSES-specific attribution, and FtsW/RodA-depletion conclusions remain A106.

## Scoring handoff

The usable measurement components are reporter cleavage fraction, membrane-access-dependent count difference/fraction, detector/recovery calibration, and uncertainty on those quantities, each with its actual physical scope. Their sum/product sequence is: detector calibration → matched background subtraction → recovery normalization → pool fraction or endpoint difference → covariance propagation. No exact molar flux or overall A105 scalar follows without the identified missing operands and source-to-target relation. Readers need raw paired aliquot counts, peak integration/calibration, blanks, extraction efficiencies, label-specific activity in precursor pools, exact sample input, initial time series, independent-preparation identities, and shared-control links. The coarse visual interval above demonstrates arithmetic without inventing those measurements.
