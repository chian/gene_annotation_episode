# Proposed recurring channel/transport investigation TASK

Proposal for root conversation review; not installed instructions, a scoring sheet or a new goal. The complete TASK must recur on every relevant existing LiteratureEpisode iteration, including after lookup and reconstruction. The parent retains its own assignment and receives only requested numerical evidence as concise STEERING. Root must print this proposal and the concrete source-based example before coding.

> Evaluate what the selected membrane-transport experiment establishes about the studied molecule's function. Identify the source organism, expression host, actual construct and preparation separately. Establish whether tested molecules coexist or are in separate preparations, and whether the claimed functional unit is a polypeptide, an assembled channel, a pore or an unresolved unit. Work from the supplied material and source evidence; an initial annotation or public searchable name is not required.
>
> Identify the actual measured endpoint and the experimental comparison. An ion-selective electrode reports external anion appearance; an optical volume response is a proxy; electrical current reports charge flow. Retain substrate/counterion composition, lipid system, activation or counterion shunt, calibration, initial baseline, normalization endpoint and assay time resolution. A released fraction is not an initial flux. When release finishes within instrumental dead-time, preserve the source's qualified rate limit rather than fitting an invented initial slope. Preserve nondetection and its actual numerical limit when supplied; do not make a flat trace exact zero.
>
> For electrical measurements establish the voltage-reference convention, grounded compartment, cis/trans ionic concentrations or activities, applied voltage, reversal potential and recording/filtering conditions. Distinguish macroscopic vesicle-insertion packets from source-supported unitary events; a discrete step need not be one channel. Determine whether a reported step is already open-minus-closed current and whether that closed level is distinct from a protein-free membrane control. A common source, anion name or compatible unit does not establish the same driving force, preparation or physical measurement.
>
> Keep comparability outside the deliberately tested change separate from attribution. For a functional ion comparison, identify other differences in voltage, gradients, membrane, counterion, detector or active-unit denominator. Do not penalize the intentionally changed ion or reconstitution density merely for changing. Ask what establishes that the signal is due to the intended molecule and which chemical species carries it. Current divided by elementary charge gives charge equivalents; an F-specific flux requires a supported selectivity interpretation or quantified carrier fraction. A purified preparation, protein-dependent signal or close reversal match does not provide an exact zero contaminating contribution.
>
> Select exact source values, units, qualifiers and experimental-relationship witnesses. Have deterministic readers and calculators populate the requested signal, driving-force-normalized conductance, charge conversion, state-weighted contribution or appropriately matched selectivity components. A conductance from one current and reversal is a chord value, not automatically a fitted differential slope. Do not silently use applied voltage alone when reversal is nonzero. A current ratio at different driving forces is not a permeability ratio. Preserve the assumptions and ionic activities needed for any Nernst or bi-ionic model; leave unsupported operands unknown.
>
> Retain every uncertainty's definition and sampling basis. Distinguish insertion events, bilayers, time samples, fitted curves and independently prepared proteins. A histogram or a minimum count does not establish independent biological replication, normality or covariance. Reused current levels, reversal estimates and normalization endpoints remain shared across derived cells. A reported plus-minus magnitude is not automatically SD, SEM or a confidence bound. A source's reported limit, an author's combined selectivity inference and your calculated threshold ratio are separate outputs with their own meanings. Complementary electrical and chemical readouts may address different weaknesses without becoming matched independent repetitions.
>
> Return the parent's requested numerical components, original measurement ownership and necessary scientific qualifications through its existing typed form. Preserve valid partial results, conflicting source descriptions and missing relationships. Separate missing source information from an unimplemented reader or conversion. Do not invent a general reliability category, function probability, target-transfer weight or annotation credit. Use ordinary investigation choices to resolve useful uncertainty; add no report-composition model call, fixed itinerary or continuation gate.
